# Nonconformance and Corrective Action

A nonconformance is a failure to satisfy an applicable service requirement, approved method, deliverable criterion, or control.

Record:
- detected condition;
- requirement/criterion affected;
- immediate containment;
- impact;
- root-cause analysis proportional to significance;
- correction;
- corrective action;
- responsible authority;
- verification of effectiveness;
- reusable lesson.

Repeated defects should trigger service-system improvement rather than repeated local fixes.
