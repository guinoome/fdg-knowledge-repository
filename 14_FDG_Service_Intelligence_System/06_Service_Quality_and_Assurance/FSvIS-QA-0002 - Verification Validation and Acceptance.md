# Verification, Validation and Acceptance

**Verification:** Did FDG produce the service output correctly against specified requirements?

**Validation:** Does the output adequately address the intended use or outcome?

**Acceptance:** Has the authorized recipient accepted the defined deliverable under the agreed criteria?

These are distinct controls and should not be collapsed into a single informal review.

Evidence should identify reviewer, date, criteria checked, exceptions, disposition, and resulting status.
