# Service Quality Assurance Framework

Quality assurance establishes confidence that the service process is capable of producing conforming outputs.

Controls may include competency checks, approved methods, peer review, checklists, evidence gates, independent verification, controlled templates, and acceptance reviews.

The level of assurance should scale with safety, legal exposure, professional liability, cost, irreversibility, and client impact.
