# Service Tier and Option Framework

Service tiers may vary depth, turnaround, support level, evidence depth, deliverable set, or resource intensity.

Tiers must not imply lower compliance with mandatory legal, safety, professional, or quality requirements.

Optional modules should be independently identifiable, have defined dependencies, and state whether they change acceptance criteria, cost, schedule, or risk.

Custom work should be treated as controlled variation, not an undocumented exception.
