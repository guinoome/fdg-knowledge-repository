# Service Packaging Standard

Packaging translates a canonical service into an understandable offering without weakening its technical or governance basis.

A package should state: target client, outcome, included scope, exclusions, prerequisites, deliverables, estimated delivery basis, options, client responsibilities, acceptance basis, and commercial dependencies.

Commercial simplification must not conceal technical limitations, safety requirements, or material assumptions.
