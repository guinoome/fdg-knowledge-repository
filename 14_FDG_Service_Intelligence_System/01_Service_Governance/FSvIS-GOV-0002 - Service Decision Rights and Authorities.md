# Service Decision Rights and Authorities

## Principle
Decision authority must follow competence, accountability, and governing domain.

## Roles
**Service Owner:** accountable for service definition, lifecycle, performance, and controlled improvement.

**Technical Authority:** approves technical methods, engineering criteria, and technical deviations.

**Delivery Lead:** accountable for engagement execution within approved service boundaries.

**Quality Reviewer:** verifies required evidence and acceptance criteria independently where warranted.

**Commercial Authority:** approves commercial packaging, pricing rules, and material economic exceptions.

**Legal/Security Authorities:** govern legal and security matters through FLIS and FSIS.

## Escalation Triggers
Escalate when a requested change alters regulated requirements, professional liability, safety, deliverable acceptance, material cost/risk, IP rights, security classification, or the canonical service boundary.

## Rule
Convenience is not authority. A collaborator or software agent may recommend a decision without possessing approval authority.
