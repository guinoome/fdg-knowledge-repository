# Service Document and Change Control

## Objective
Prevent uncontrolled copies and contradictory service instructions.

## Rules
- Maintain one canonical controlled source for each service artifact.
- Use stable identifiers where practical.
- Record version, status, owner, and change rationale.
- Prefer references to authoritative FDG knowledge over duplicated content.
- Separate current standards from archive material.
- Mark draft and experimental artifacts clearly.
- Evaluate downstream impact before approving material change.

## Change Record
A material change should record: trigger, affected service/version, reason, evidence, impacted requirements/deliverables/workflows, risk assessment, decision authority, effective date, and migration action.
