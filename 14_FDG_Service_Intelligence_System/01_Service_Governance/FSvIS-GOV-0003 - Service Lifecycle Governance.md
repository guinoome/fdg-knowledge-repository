# Service Lifecycle Governance

## Lifecycle
1. Identify candidate.
2. Validate need.
3. Define service.
4. Review dependencies and risks.
5. Pilot where appropriate.
6. Approve and activate.
7. Deliver and measure.
8. Improve through controlled changes.
9. Deprecate when superseded.
10. Retire when no longer defensible or valuable.
11. Archive records.

## Versioning
Material changes to scope, acceptance, workflow, risk controls, legal basis, technical method, or economics require version review. Editorial changes may remain minor revisions.

## Retirement
Retirement must preserve historical evidence, identify replacement services where applicable, and prevent obsolete templates from being mistaken for active standards.
