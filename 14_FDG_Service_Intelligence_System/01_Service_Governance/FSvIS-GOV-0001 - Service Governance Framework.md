# Service Governance Framework

## Purpose
Establish the minimum controls required for FDG services to remain coherent, authorized, traceable, and maintainable.

## Governance Objects
Service definition, service version, package, workflow, acceptance criteria, evidence requirement, KPI, risk control, reusable component, and improvement.

## Lifecycle States
`Candidate -> Draft -> Review -> Approved -> Active -> Controlled Change -> Deprecated -> Retired -> Archived`

No Draft service should be represented as a controlled standard. Deprecated services remain usable only where explicitly authorized. Retired services are not offered for new work.

## Minimum Approval Questions
- Is the service needed?
- Is ownership clear?
- Are scope and exclusions unambiguous?
- Are technical and legal dependencies identified?
- Can delivery be reproduced?
- Are deliverables and acceptance criteria measurable?
- Are material risks controlled?
- Can evidence be retained?
- Is the service economically understandable?
- Is customization bounded?

## Governance Cadence
Review active services after material incidents, repeated nonconformance, major requirement changes, significant cost shifts, or accumulated improvement evidence. Calendar review frequency should be set according to service criticality rather than imposed uniformly.
