# New Service Development Gate

A new service should progress through evidence gates.

**Gate 0 — Opportunity:** clear need or strategic hypothesis.

**Gate 1 — Feasibility:** capability, authority, dependencies, risks, and economics are plausible.

**Gate 2 — Definition:** scope, outcome, workflow, deliverables, acceptance, and owner are defined.

**Gate 3 — Pilot:** limited controlled delivery validates assumptions.

**Gate 4 — Approval:** evidence supports active service status.

**Gate 5 — Scale:** repeatability, quality, economics, knowledge capture, and governance support wider deployment.

Do not automate or heavily integrate a service whose fundamental workflow has not been validated.
