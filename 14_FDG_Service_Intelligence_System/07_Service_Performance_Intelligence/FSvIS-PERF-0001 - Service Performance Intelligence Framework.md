# Service Performance Intelligence Framework

Performance intelligence determines whether a service is reliable, valuable, efficient, and improving.

Measure only what supports decisions. Candidate dimensions include outcome attainment, first-pass acceptance, rework, cycle time, schedule reliability, defect/nonconformance rate, client effort, resource consumption, margin, recurrence of known failures, and reuse rate.

Metrics require definitions, data sources, owners, frequency, limitations, and decision thresholds.
