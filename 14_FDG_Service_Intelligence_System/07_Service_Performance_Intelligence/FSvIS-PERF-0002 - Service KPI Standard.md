# Service KPI Standard

Every KPI should define:

`Name | Purpose | Formula | Unit | Data Source | Frequency | Owner | Target/Threshold | Interpretation | Known Limitations`

Avoid vanity metrics. A KPI is useful only if a decision or improvement action can follow from it.

Leading and lagging indicators should be distinguished where practical.
