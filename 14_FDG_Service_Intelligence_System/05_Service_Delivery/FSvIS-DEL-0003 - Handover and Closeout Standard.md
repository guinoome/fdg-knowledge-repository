# Handover and Closeout Standard

Closeout should confirm that all required deliverables, evidence, approvals, outstanding items, client responsibilities, operating limitations, and follow-up actions are visible.

A handover record should identify what was delivered, version/date, recipient, unresolved items, acceptance status, required future action, and knowledge captured.

Do not treat file transmission alone as successful handover.
