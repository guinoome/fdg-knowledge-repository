# Service Engagement Lifecycle

## Intake
Confirm request, intended outcome, client identity, and basic feasibility.

## Qualification
Check fit against an approved service, prerequisites, competence, capacity, conflicts, and major risks.

## Definition
Freeze engagement-specific scope, exclusions, assumptions, inputs, deliverables, and acceptance.

## Execution
Perform approved workflow and retain required evidence.

## Verification
Check completeness, technical quality, compliance, and deviations.

## Delivery and Acceptance
Transmit controlled deliverables and record acceptance or unresolved exceptions.

## Closeout
Complete records, handover, commercial/administrative interfaces, and lessons learned.
