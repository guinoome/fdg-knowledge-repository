# Service Risk Intelligence Framework

Service risk intelligence identifies conditions that can prevent the intended outcome or create unacceptable exposure.

Risk categories may include technical, safety, legal/regulatory, professional liability, scope, client dependency, schedule, resource/competency, commercial, information/security, supplier, quality, reputation, and lifecycle risks.

For each material risk record: cause, event, consequence, existing controls, likelihood/impact basis, owner, treatment, residual risk, trigger, and evidence.

Escalate risks to the governing FDG system when they exceed FSvIS authority.
