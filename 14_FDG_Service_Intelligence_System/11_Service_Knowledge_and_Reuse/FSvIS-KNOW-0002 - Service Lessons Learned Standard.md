# Service Lessons Learned Standard

A useful lesson records:

- context;
- expected result;
- observed result;
- evidence;
- causal analysis;
- what worked or failed;
- applicability limits;
- recommended change;
- affected service artifacts;
- owner and disposition.

Lessons are incomplete until they are either integrated into controlled knowledge, deliberately rejected with rationale, or retained as an open improvement.
