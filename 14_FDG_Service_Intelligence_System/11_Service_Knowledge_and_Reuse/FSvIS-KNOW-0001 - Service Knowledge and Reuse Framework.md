# Service Knowledge and Reuse Framework

The objective is to convert delivery experience into organizational capability.

Reusable assets may include templates, checklists, calculation patterns, decision rules, evidence patterns, standard clauses by reference, service modules, blueprints, reference deliverables, lessons, and failure modes.

Reuse requires provenance, applicability limits, version/status, owner, and validation. Reuse is not blind copying.
