# Service Experience Intelligence Framework

Service experience intelligence examines how clients/users encounter the service across the full journey, not merely whether a final deliverable was produced.

Map expectations, touchpoints, waiting, information requests, handoffs, friction, trust signals, decisions, delivery, acceptance, and post-service support.

Experience improvements must not compromise technical rigor or create misleading expectations.
