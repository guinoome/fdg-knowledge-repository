# Feedback, Complaint and Recovery Standard

Feedback is evidence, not automatically truth. Record the source, context, affected service/version, severity, and corroborating evidence.

Complaints should be triaged for immediate safety/legal/security implications before normal service recovery.

Recovery should address the affected client outcome while preserving root-cause learning. Repeated complaint patterns should feed the improvement register and service redesign.
