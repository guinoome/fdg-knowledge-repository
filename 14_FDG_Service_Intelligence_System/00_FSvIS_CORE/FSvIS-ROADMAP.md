# FSvIS Roadmap

## Milestone 0 — Architecture Baseline

**Status:** Established by this package.

Objectives:
- establish FSvIS charter and boundaries;
- create canonical folders;
- establish service lifecycle and governance;
- define initial standards and templates.

## Milestone 1 — Define Real Services

Priority:
- inventory actual FDG services;
- assign owners and lifecycle status;
- create Service Definition Records for highest-value services;
- establish scope, exclusions, prerequisites, deliverables, and acceptance criteria;
- identify dependencies on FEIS, FPJIS, FLIS, FSIS, FCIS/FMCIS, and business intelligence.

Exit condition: priority services are explicitly defined rather than existing only as tacit knowledge.

## Milestone 2 — Make Delivery Repeatable

Develop:
- service blueprints;
- standard workflows;
- checklists;
- evidence requirements;
- handover and closeout controls;
- reusable templates.

Exit condition: competent authorized collaborators can reproduce the service with controlled variance.

## Milestone 3 — Quality and Performance

Implement:
- verification and validation gates;
- nonconformance process;
- service KPIs;
- outcome tracking;
- service experience capture;
- risk registers.

Exit condition: service quality and performance can be evaluated with evidence.

## Milestone 4 — Economics and Portfolio Decisions

Implement:
- resource models;
- cost drivers;
- pricing intelligence;
- margin and lifecycle economics;
- service portfolio review;
- retire/redesign/scale decisions.

Exit condition: service decisions are supported by operational and economic evidence.

## Milestone 5 — Organizational Learning and Reuse

Implement:
- formal lessons learned;
- reusable service components;
- reference deliverables;
- pattern libraries;
- controlled improvement register.

Exit condition: each service engagement can strengthen future delivery.

## Milestone 6 — Digital Enablement and Selective Automation

Only after workflows are stable:
- structured service records;
- dashboards;
- workflow assistance;
- automated evidence collection where reliable;
- AI/agent assistance with explicit authority boundaries;
- integration with project, collaboration, and business systems.

Technology selection is intentionally deferred until validated workflows and data contracts exist.

## Deferred Scope

Do not turn FSvIS into a full CRM, ERP, accounting platform, issue tracker, project-management replacement, DevOps platform, coding-agent platform, payment processor, or general automation engine. Those are separate future capabilities or integrations.
