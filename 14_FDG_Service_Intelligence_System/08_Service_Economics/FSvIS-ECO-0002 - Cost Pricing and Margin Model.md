# Cost, Pricing and Margin Model

## Cost Model
Identify cost drivers and distinguish fixed, variable, direct, indirect, one-time, and lifecycle costs.

## Pricing Intelligence
Pricing may consider cost floor, value created, market context, risk, scarcity of expertise, delivery urgency, support burden, and strategic purpose.

## Margin
Margin analysis should use consistent definitions and explicitly state whether overhead, taxes, contingencies, commissions, and post-delivery obligations are included.

Do not confuse price with cost or accounting profit with engineering value.

## 2026-10-19 — Assisted-service pilot contribution and acquisition economics

For each FDG FEIS/FBPOIS-related paid pilot, record **priced offer version, accepted service scope, invoice/settlement reference, reporting period, amounts collected, currency and tax treatment, refund/chargeback risks, direct engineering/review labor, founder labor hours, external/software fees, transport, revisions/support and attributable lead-acquisition spend**. Use actual evidence, not zero-filled guesses.

- **Cash contribution before customer acquisition** = verified applicable collected revenue, consistently defined and net of refund/tax pass-through, minus real direct delivery cash costs, fees and supported rework/support cost. The treatment of unpaid founder time is displayed separately; a zero cash wage does not imply an economically free service.
- **Contribution after acquisition** = comparable pre-acquisition contribution minus attributable marketing/sales acquisition spend. Do not call it overall net profit when overhead, fixed cost, tax or working capital is excluded.
- **Effective contribution per founder hour** = contribution after acquisition / actual founder hours, reported only for positive observed hours; separately show total engineering/reviewer time.
- **For a recurring pilot:** renewal/payback or month-two contribution is unknown until an actual subsequent settled payment and accepted repeat deliverable. Never call recurring revenue passive before measuring labor and support over repeated months.
- **For customer ROI:** baseline cost/value from client source, actual observed result, comparison time window, implementation/support cost, attributable savings and unknown factors must be explicit. Hypothetical percentages are projections, not independently verified outcomes.
- **Marketing ROI/CAC:** align source attribution with [[11_FDG_Business_Intelligence_System/11_Business_Frameworks/FBIS-BP-003_FDG_First_Payout_Commercial_Validation_Portfolio|FBIS sales measures]]. Never double-count paid media in both direct delivery cost and acquisition cost.

**Go/Pause/Stop:** when support/revisions exceed scope, positive margin disappears, client questions accuracy, applicable regulated deliverable lacks reviewer or customer data permission is absent, pause expansion and correct before selling additional identical work. An attractive quoted fee is not evidence of contribution.
