# Service Definition Standard

Every controlled service definition should contain:

1. Identity and purpose
2. Problem/need addressed
3. Intended client/user
4. Target outcome
5. Scope
6. Explicit exclusions
7. Preconditions and required inputs
8. Requirements and authoritative references
9. Assumptions and constraints
10. Roles and competencies
11. Workflow summary
12. Deliverables
13. Evidence requirements
14. Acceptance criteria
15. Risks and controls
16. Dependencies/interfaces
17. Resource requirements
18. Performance measures
19. Knowledge capture
20. Version/status/owner

A service definition is incomplete if a competent reviewer cannot determine what constitutes successful completion.
