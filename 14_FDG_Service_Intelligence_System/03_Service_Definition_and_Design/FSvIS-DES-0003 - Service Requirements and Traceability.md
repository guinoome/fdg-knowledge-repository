# Service Requirements and Traceability

Requirements may originate from client needs, laws/regulations, codes/standards, contracts, engineering criteria, internal policy, security controls, or operational constraints.

Each material requirement should identify its origin, applicability, implementation point, verification method, and evidence.

Preferred chain:

`Requirement ID -> Source -> Service Element -> Deliverable/Control -> Verification -> Evidence -> Status`

Assumptions must not be silently promoted into requirements or facts.
