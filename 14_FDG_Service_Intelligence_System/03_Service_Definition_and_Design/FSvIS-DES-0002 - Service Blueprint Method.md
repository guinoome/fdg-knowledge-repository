# Service Blueprint Method

A service blueprint visualizes how a service produces an outcome.

## Minimum Lanes
- Client/user actions
- Frontstage FDG interactions
- Backstage delivery activities
- Technical/support processes
- Evidence and control points
- Systems/dependencies
- Handoffs and decisions

## Method
Start from the intended outcome, map the end-to-end journey, expose dependencies and failure points, then connect each critical step to evidence and acceptance criteria.

Blueprints should be simple enough to inspect visually before automation or integration is attempted.
