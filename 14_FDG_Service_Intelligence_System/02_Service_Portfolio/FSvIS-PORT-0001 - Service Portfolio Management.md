# Service Portfolio Management

The service portfolio is the controlled inventory of FDG services.

Each service should record identity, category, owner, intended users/clients, value outcome, maturity, lifecycle status, dependencies, strategic relevance, and current version.

Portfolio reviews should identify duplication, service gaps, obsolete offerings, excessive customization, weak economics, repeated delivery problems, and candidates for standardization or reuse.

Portfolio management decides **what services FDG should maintain**. Project management decides **how a specific engagement is executed**.
