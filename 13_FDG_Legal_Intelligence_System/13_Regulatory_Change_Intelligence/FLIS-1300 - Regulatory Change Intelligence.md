# FLIS-1300 - Regulatory Change Intelligence

## Purpose

Detect, verify, assess, route, and close legal/regulatory changes
affecting FDG.

## Workflow

Change Signal → Source Verification → Baseline Comparison →
Applicability → Affected Requirements → Affected
Systems/Contracts/Projects → Impact → Owner → Action → Evidence →
Closure.

## Change Record

Change ID; authority; old requirement; new requirement; effective date;
transition period; affected records; impact; owner; due date; status;
evidence; review outcome.

## Rule

A news article or AI summary may trigger investigation but does not
itself establish a regulatory change.
