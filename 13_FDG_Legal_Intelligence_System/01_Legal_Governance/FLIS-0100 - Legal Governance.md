# FLIS-0100 - Legal Governance

## Purpose

Govern how FLIS knowledge is created, verified, classified, approved,
changed, reviewed, retained, and retired.

## Required Controls

Document identification and versioning; source provenance; jurisdiction
tagging; verification status; authority classification; review
ownership; approval status; change history; confidentiality
classification; retention status; supersession links.

## Status Vocabulary

Draft → Under Review → Verified → Approved → Superseded → Archived.

## Governance Rule

A legal summary cannot silently become authoritative. Material
interpretations must retain links to source evidence and record
uncertainty where present.
