# FLIS-0400 - Intellectual Property

## Purpose

Identify, classify, evidence, and govern FDG intellectual-property
rights and obligations.

## Coverage

Copyright; trademarks; proprietary methodologies; software/source code;
licensing; IP created by employees, contractors, collaborators, or
vendors.

## Core Questions

What is the asset? Who created it? Who owns it? What evidence supports
ownership? What license applies? Who may use it? What restrictions
exist? What protection or registration action is required?

## Boundary

FLIS governs legal rights and licensing. FSIS governs technical access
and protection.
