# FLIS-9900 - Archive Governance

## Purpose

Preserve superseded, inactive, or historical FLIS materials without
confusing them with current authority.

## Archive Rules

Archive rather than delete when retention, evidence, history,
auditability, or legal hold may matter. Mark superseded status and point
to the current record. Preserve original dates and provenance. Apply
confidentiality and retention requirements.

## Prohibition

Moving a record to the archive does not remove legal retention,
privilege, confidentiality, or evidence obligations.
