# FLIS-1000 - Legal Risk Intelligence

## Purpose

Identify, assess, prioritize, monitor, and learn from legal exposure.

## Initial Risk Dimensions

Likelihood; legal consequence; financial exposure; regulatory exposure;
professional exposure; contractual exposure; operational impact;
reputational consequence; reversibility; evidence strength; uncertainty.

## Governance

The formal scoring methodology is a controlled future work package.
Until approved, use qualitative ratings with documented rationale rather
than invented precision.
