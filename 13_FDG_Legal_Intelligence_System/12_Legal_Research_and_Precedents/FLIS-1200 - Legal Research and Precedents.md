# FLIS-1200 - Legal Research and Precedents

## Purpose

Preserve reusable legal research while separating authority from
interpretation.

## Research Note Pattern

Question; context; jurisdiction; search date; primary authorities;
secondary sources; facts; assumptions; analysis; conflicting authority;
conclusion; uncertainty; reviewer; affected systems; review trigger.

## Precedent Rule

A prior FDG decision is institutional memory, not automatically binding
legal precedent. Revalidate when facts, law, jurisdiction, or
contractual context differ.
