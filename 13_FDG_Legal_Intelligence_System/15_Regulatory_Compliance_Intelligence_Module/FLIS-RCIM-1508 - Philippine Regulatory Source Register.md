## Source Admission Rule

A new source enters production rule logic only after:

1. issuer/jurisdiction verified;
2. publication/source authenticity checked;
3. effective date identified;
4. supersession/amendment relationship checked;
5. affected requirement extracted;
6. applicability rule reviewed;
7. validation cases passed;
8. approval recorded.

## Legal/Professional Review Trigger

Escalate when:

- authority is ambiguous;
- current source cannot be verified;
- national and local/special rules conflict;
- regulator interpretation differs from stored rule;
- potential penalties/shutdown/material liability exist;
- a licensed professional is required for the submission;
- a client asks for a legal opinion rather than compliance workflow support.
