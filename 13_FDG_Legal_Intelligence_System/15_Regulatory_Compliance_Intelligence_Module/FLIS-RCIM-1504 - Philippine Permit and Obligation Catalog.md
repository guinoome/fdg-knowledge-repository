# FLIS-RCIM-1504 - Philippine Permit and Obligation Catalog

## Use

This catalog is a governed starting set for Philippine applicability screening. It is not a declaration that every listed item applies to every business, project or facility.

“Authority” identifies the principal legal/regulatory family. Current agency issuances, permit conditions and jurisdiction-specific rules must also be checked.

## Permit Passport

Each permit/authorization shall expose a normalized “passport”:

- Permit Type
- Authority
- Jurisdiction
- Covered Entity
- Covered Site
- Covered Equipment/Activity
- Permit Number
- Application Reference
- Issue Date
- Effective Date
- Expiry Date
- Conditions
- Capacity/Limit
- Required Monitoring
- Required Reports
- Renewal Lead Time
- Responsible Owner
- PCO / Professional
- Evidence
- Last Verification
- Next Review Trigger
- Status

## Required Dependency Behavior

The catalog shall support dependency relationships rather than flat checklists.

Examples:

- WDP may require current PCO evidence and ECC/CNC information as applicable.
- PTO may require equipment/source information, PCO evidence and source testing depending on source category.
- Hazardous-waste transfer requires generator, transporter, manifest and TSD chain evidence.
- Sanitary approval does not replace DENR wastewater authorization.
- ECC issuance does not automatically replace permits issued under separate environmental laws.
- Business permits do not automatically establish environmental compliance.

## Region VII Implementation Note

Cebu / Central Visayas users shall load the Region VII checklist and workflow overlay from official EMB Region VII sources. The national catalog remains the base layer.

## Catalog Governance

Every item must link to a requirement/source record before production use.

When the source changes, the catalog item is versioned; prior records are retained.
