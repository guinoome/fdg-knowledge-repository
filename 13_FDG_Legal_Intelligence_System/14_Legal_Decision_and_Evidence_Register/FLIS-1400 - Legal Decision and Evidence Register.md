# FLIS-1400 - Legal Decision and Evidence Register

## Purpose

Preserve material legal decisions and the evidence/reasoning behind
them.

## Decision Record

Decision ID; question; context; jurisdiction; authority; facts;
assumptions; evidence; analysis; alternatives; risks; conclusion;
recommendation; reviewer; approval; affected systems; required actions;
review trigger; superseding decision.

## Evidence Record

Evidence ID; description; source; custodian; date;
authenticity/verification status; confidentiality; related
decision/matter; retention requirement; location/reference.

## Rule

The register preserves reasoning and accountability so decisions do not
disappear into chats, email, or individual memory.
