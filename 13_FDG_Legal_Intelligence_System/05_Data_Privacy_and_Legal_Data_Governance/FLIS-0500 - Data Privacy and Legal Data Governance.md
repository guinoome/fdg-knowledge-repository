# FLIS-0500 - Data Privacy and Legal Data Governance

## Purpose

Maintain legal intelligence concerning personal-data processing and
legal data-governance obligations.

## Coverage

Lawful basis; purpose limitation; transparency; data-subject rights;
sharing/disclosure; processors; retention; cross-border considerations;
breach/legal notification requirements; privacy impact obligations;
records and accountability.

## Interface

FLIS identifies legal privacy requirements. FSIS and responsible
platforms implement technical/organizational controls and return
evidence.

## Rule

Do not store unnecessary personal data in FLIS merely because the system
analyzes privacy obligations.
