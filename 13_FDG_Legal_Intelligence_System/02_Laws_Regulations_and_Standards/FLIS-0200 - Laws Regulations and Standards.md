# FLIS-0200 - Laws, Regulations and Standards

## Purpose

Maintain verified legal and regulatory authority relevant to FDG.

## Classification

Philippine Laws; Local Regulations; Industry Regulations; Regulatory
Agencies.

## Minimum Metadata

Authority title, citation/identifier, issuer, jurisdiction, publication
source, effective date, amendment status, verification date,
applicability tags, affected FDG domains, and related requirement
records.

## Rule

Store or reference authoritative sources where legally and technically
practical. Summaries must be identified as summaries.
