# FLIS-0700 - Employment and Collaboration Law

## Purpose

Maintain legal intelligence for employment, contractors, consultants,
collaborators, and organizational working relationships.

## Coverage

Relationship classification; contracts; confidentiality; IP ownership;
workplace obligations; compensation-related legal requirements;
termination; contractor boundaries; access and data obligations.

## Rule

Do not infer worker classification from labels alone; assess actual
legal criteria and escalate material uncertainty.

## Human Resources Interface Clarification — 2026-09-27

FLIS owns employment-law intelligence and legal requirements. Human Resources and People Operations owns recruitment, hiring process, onboarding, benefits administration, employee records, performance-process administration, learning and development administration, employee relations, compensation-administration workflow, and offboarding.

HR implements applicable legal requirements supplied by FLIS; FLIS does not become the HR Department.

The requesting department remains responsible for its functional and technical competency requirements and participates in hiring and performance decisions through the HR-governed process.

See [[06_Organizational_Architecture/NEX-STD-125_ENTERPRISE_FUNCTION_AND_DEPARTMENT_STANDARD|Enterprise Function and Department Standard]].
