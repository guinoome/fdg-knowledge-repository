# FLIS-0300 - Contracts and Agreements

## Purpose

Create reusable intelligence from contractual rights, obligations,
risks, clauses, and negotiation outcomes.

## Contract Lifecycle

Template → Draft → Review → Negotiation → Approval → Execution →
Obligation Tracking → Amendment/Renewal → Closeout → Retention.

## Review Dimensions

Parties; authority; scope; deliverables; price/payment; term;
termination; warranties; indemnity; liability; insurance; IP;
confidentiality; privacy; subcontracting; dispute resolution; governing
law; force majeure; notices; acceptance; change control.

## Rule

Templates are starting points, not automatic legal approval.
