# Contract Review Template

## Identification

Parties, document title, version, date, governing law, reviewer.

## Commercial Terms

Scope, deliverables, price, payment, term, renewal, acceptance.

## Risk Allocation

Warranties, indemnities, liability limits, insurance, termination, force
majeure, disputes.

## Information and IP

Confidentiality, privacy/data, ownership, licenses, permitted use.

## Operational Obligations

Deadlines, notices, approvals, records, audit rights, compliance
obligations.

## Findings

Facts, assumptions, deviations, risks, unresolved items,
recommendations, escalation needs.
