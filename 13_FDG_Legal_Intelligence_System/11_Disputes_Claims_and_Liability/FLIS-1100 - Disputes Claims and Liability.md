# FLIS-1100 - Disputes, Claims and Liability

## Purpose

Preserve structured intelligence for disputes, claims, potential
liability, defensive evidence, lessons, and prevention.

## Record Pattern

Matter ID; parties; issue; chronology; contractual/legal basis; evidence
index; communications; exposure; status; counsel/escalation; decisions;
settlement/closure; lessons; affected templates/processes.

## Confidentiality

Apply heightened access controls where privilege, litigation strategy,
personal data, or commercially sensitive information may be involved.
