# FLIS Roadmap

## Milestone 1 --- Foundation

Complete the approved architecture, core definitions, governance,
templates, registers, naming, evidence rules, and system boundaries.

## Milestone 2 --- Structured Legal Knowledge

Populate verified laws/regulations, regulator profiles, contract
templates, IP register, compliance registers, research notes, and
standardized metadata.

## Milestone 3 --- Traceability

Connect authority → requirement → obligation → owner → FDG system →
action/control → evidence → status. Establish legal decision and
evidence records.

## Milestone 4 --- Intelligence

Add controlled contract comparison, clause intelligence, applicability
assistance, legal risk scoring methodology, evidence-gap detection, and
change-impact analysis.

## Milestone 5 --- Adaptive Intelligence

Add verified regulatory monitoring, cross-system impact routing, review
triggers, recurring compliance checks, and institutional learning loops.

## Deferred Until Validated

Autonomous legal agents, autonomous contract approval, paid legal APIs,
external production integrations, complex workflow engines, and
autonomous legal decision-making.

## Roadmap Rule

Do not introduce later-stage complexity until the preceding workflow is
proven, reviewable, and useful to FDG.
