# FLIS-0001 - Charter and Scope

## Charter

FLIS is authorized to organize, preserve, analyze, trace, and govern
legal intelligence needed by the FDG Ecosystem.

## Objectives

1.  Maintain trustworthy legal and regulatory references.
2.  Convert relevant authority into traceable obligations.
3.  Maintain reusable contract and clause intelligence.
4.  Protect legal knowledge concerning FDG intellectual property.
5.  Support privacy-law and legal data-governance compliance.
6.  Identify and monitor legal risk, claims, disputes, and liability.
7.  Detect and assess regulatory change.
8.  Preserve legal decisions, evidence, assumptions, approvals, and
    review triggers.

## In Scope

Philippine and applicable local law; industry regulation; regulatory
agencies; contracts; IP; privacy law; corporate/business compliance;
employment/collaboration law; engineering/professional liability;
procurement/commercial law; legal risk; disputes/claims;
research/precedent; regulatory change; legal decisions and evidence.

## Out of Scope

Autonomous legal representation; unauthorized practice of law; final
professional legal opinions where counsel is required; cybersecurity
implementation; engineering calculations/design; accounting; payroll;
full CRM/ERP/project-management/issue-tracking functionality.

## Authority Boundaries

FLIS may classify, retrieve, summarize, compare, trace, flag, and
prepare review packages. It may not fabricate authority, silently treat
secondary commentary as primary law, or make unreviewed high-impact
legal determinations.

## Escalation Triggers

Escalate when a matter is high-value, high-liability, disputed,
privileged, criminal/regulatory enforcement related, jurisdictionally
uncertain, professionally consequential, contractually material, or
unsupported by sufficient authority.

## Inputs

Official legal sources, contracts, permits, licenses, regulator
issuances, professional requirements, business/project context,
evidence, correspondence, and verified internal records.

## Outputs

Requirement records, applicability assessments, obligation maps,
contract analyses, risk records, research notes, regulatory-change
assessments, decision records, evidence indexes, and review packages.

## Definition of Done

A material FLIS output is complete when its source, jurisdiction,
applicability, evidence, assumptions, analysis,
conclusion/recommendation, responsible owner, affected system, status,
and review trigger are recorded as applicable.
