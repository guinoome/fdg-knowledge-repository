# FLIS-0000 - FDG Legal Intelligence System

## System Identity

The FDG Legal Intelligence System (FLIS) is the legal intelligence layer
of the FDG Ecosystem. It converts laws, regulations, contracts, legal
evidence, professional obligations, privacy requirements,
intellectual-property rights, commercial obligations, disputes, and
regulatory changes into structured and reusable organizational
intelligence.

**Core question:** What is FDG legally required, permitted, prohibited,
obligated, or entitled to do?

## Mission

Establish a persistent, evidence-based legal intelligence capability
that enables FDG to understand, trace, manage, and respond to legal,
regulatory, contractual, intellectual-property, privacy, professional,
and commercial obligations throughout their lifecycle.

## Governing Principles

-   Evidence before interpretation.
-   Preserve authoritative sources and provenance.
-   Separate verified facts, assumptions, analysis, conclusions, and
    recommendations.
-   AI output is analysis, not legal authority.
-   Determine applicability before treating a requirement as binding.
-   Trace material obligations from authority to implementation
    evidence.
-   Escalate high-impact, uncertain, disputed, or privileged matters for
    qualified legal review.
-   Capture legal learning as organizational knowledge.
-   Remain technology-neutral and local-first where practical.

## System Boundary

FLIS owns legal intelligence, legal requirement traceability, legal
evidence, contract intelligence, IP legal governance, privacy-law
intelligence, legal risk, disputes/claims intelligence, legal research,
regulatory-change intelligence, and legal decision memory.

FLIS does not replace licensed counsel, courts, regulators, engineering
design systems, cybersecurity controls, accounting systems, HR systems,
project-management systems, or operational business systems.

## Intelligence Lifecycle

Source → Capture → Verify → Classify → Interpret → Determine
Applicability → Extract Requirement → Assess Risk/Impact → Assign
Responsibility → Implement Through Responsible FDG System → Collect
Evidence → Monitor → Review → Preserve Learning.

## Requirement Traceability

Legal Authority → Requirement → Obligation → Applicable Entity/Activity
→ Responsible Role → Responsible FDG System → Control/Action → Evidence
→ Compliance Status.

## Relationships

-   **FSIS:** FLIS determines legal/security obligations; FSIS converts
    protection obligations into security controls.
-   **FEIS:** FLIS identifies engineering/professional requirements;
    FEIS operationalizes them in engineering practice.
-   **Project Intelligence:** consumes project-specific permits,
    contract, procurement, liability, and statutory obligations.
-   **Business Intelligence:** consumes corporate, commercial,
    licensing, employment, and compliance requirements.
-   **Collaboration Intelligence:** consumes NDA, IP ownership,
    confidentiality, contractor, licensing, and access obligations.
-   **FDG CORE:** may orchestrate cross-system questions but shall not
    silently override authoritative FLIS requirements.

## Maturity Model

0 Fragmented → 1 Organized → 2 Structured → 3 Traceable → 4 Intelligent
→ 5 Adaptive.

Current baseline: **Level 1 development**, architected toward Levels
3--5. Higher levels are roadmap targets, not current capability claims.

## Architecture Governance

The approved FLIS structure is authoritative. New capabilities must
first be mapped into an existing approved domain. Renaming, merging,
restructuring, or adding top-level domains requires explicit
architectural justification and approval.

## Current Milestone

Foundation: establish system definition, scope, architecture,
governance, evidence principles, classification, interfaces, and
roadmap. Autonomous legal decision-making and complex external
integrations are explicitly deferred.

## Governing Statement

FLIS converts legal information into persistent organizational
intelligence. Legal intelligence belongs to FDG, not to the tool, model,
platform, or collaborator used to create it.
