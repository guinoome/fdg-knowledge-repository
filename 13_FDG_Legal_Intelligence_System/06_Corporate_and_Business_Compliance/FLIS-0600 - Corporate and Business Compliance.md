# FLIS-0600 - Corporate and Business Compliance

## Purpose

Track legal and regulatory obligations associated with FDG entities and
business activities.

## Coverage

Registrations, permits, licenses, renewals, statutory filings, operating
authorities, regulated activities, business-name/trademark interfaces,
and compliance evidence.

## Register Pattern

Requirement \| Entity \| Authority \| Frequency \| Due Date \| Owner \|
Evidence \| Status \| Risk \| Review Trigger.

> **Knowledge path:** [[FDG Ecosystem|FDG Ecosystem]] → [[13_FDG_Legal_Intelligence_System/README|README]] → this document
