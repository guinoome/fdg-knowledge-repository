# FLIS-0600 - Corporate and Business Compliance

## Purpose

Track legal and regulatory obligations associated with FDG entities and
business activities.

## Coverage

Registrations, permits, licenses, renewals, statutory filings, operating
authorities, regulated activities, business-name/trademark interfaces,
and compliance evidence.

## Register Pattern

Requirement \| Entity \| Authority \| Frequency \| Due Date \| Owner \|
Evidence \| Status \| Risk \| Review Trigger.

> **Knowledge path:** [[FDG Ecosystem|FDG Ecosystem]] → [[13_FDG_Legal_Intelligence_System/README|README]] → this document


## Applied Regulatory Capability

Entity-, branch-, facility- and project-level permits and recurring regulatory obligations may be operationalized through [[13_FDG_Legal_Intelligence_System/15_Regulatory_Compliance_Intelligence_Module/FLIS-RCIM-1500 - FDG Regulatory Compliance Intelligence Module|FRCIM]].

The normalized permit/obligation catalog is [[13_FDG_Legal_Intelligence_System/15_Regulatory_Compliance_Intelligence_Module/FLIS-RCIM-1504 - Philippine Permit and Obligation Catalog|FLIS-RCIM-1504]], while the existing Compliance Obligation Register remains reusable rather than duplicated.
