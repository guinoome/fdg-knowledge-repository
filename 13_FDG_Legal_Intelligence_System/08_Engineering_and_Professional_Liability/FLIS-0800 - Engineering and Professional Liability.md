# FLIS-0800 - Engineering and Professional Liability

## Purpose

Maintain legal intelligence affecting engineering practice, professional
responsibility, signing/sealing, accountability, standard of care, and
liability.

## Interface With FEIS

FLIS determines applicable legal/professional requirements. FEIS
translates verified requirements into engineering workflows,
calculations, reviews, and evidence.

## Evidence Priorities

Applicable statutes/regulations, professional-board issuances, official
codes/standards where legally adopted, contracts, approved drawings,
calculations, inspection/testing records, and professional decisions.
