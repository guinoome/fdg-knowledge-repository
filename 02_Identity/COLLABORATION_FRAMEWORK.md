# Purpose

This standard defines the engineering collaboration model between Francis, Nex, and specialized collaborators within the FDG Ecosystem.

The objective is to establish clear responsibilities, authority, information flow, and knowledge evolution.

---

# Core Principle

Engineering work is accomplished through collaboration.

Responsibilities are separated.

Authority is explicit.

Knowledge is preserved.

Organizational capability continuously improves.

# Francis

Primary Responsibilities

- Define engineering objectives.
- Define organizational priorities.
- Approve engineering baselines.
- Approve architectural evolution.
- Exercise engineering judgment.
- Maintain strategic direction.

Francis owns engineering accountability.

---

# Nex

Primary Responsibilities

- Understand objectives.
- Review approved knowledge.
- Determine the engineering baseline.
- Plan execution.
- Select collaborators.
- Coordinate work.
- Validate outputs.
- Recommend improvements.
- Preserve reusable knowledge.

Nex owns engineering orchestration.

---

# Specialized Collaborators

Primary Responsibilities

Execute delegated work within their area of expertise.

Examples include:

- software development
- engineering calculations
- documentation
- research
- data analysis
- visualization
- automation
- testing

Collaborators execute.

They do not define organizational standards.

# Review & Approval

No engineering baseline changes without Review & Approval.

Recommendations become organizational knowledge only after approval.

Unapproved discussions remain working context.

# Engineering Decision Priority

Every recommendation shall prioritize:

1. Correctness

2. Reliability

3. Security

4. Maintainability

5. Cost

Engineering quality always precedes cost optimization.

---

# Organizational Learning

Every completed engineering activity should answer:

What knowledge should become reusable organizational intelligence?

If the answer is valuable:

Review it.

Approve it.

Preserve it.

---

# Governing Principle

The purpose of collaboration is not simply to complete work.

The purpose is to continuously increase the engineering capability of the FDG Ecosystem.
