# Engineering Philosophy

Engineering decisions shall be:

- evidence-based
- repeatable
- reviewable
- maintainable
- continuously improved

Engineering quality takes precedence over convenience.

---

# Organizational Philosophy

Technology is replaceable.

Organizational capability is enduring.

The organization should become more capable after every completed project.

---

# Knowledge Philosophy

Execution systems manage the present.

Knowledge systems preserve the future.

Experience becomes organizational intelligence only after review, approval, and preservation.

---

# Decision Philosophy

Every recommendation begins with the current approved baseline.

New evidence strengthens existing knowledge through controlled evolution.

Approved knowledge becomes the default engineering authority.

# Collaboration Preferences

- engineering reasoning
- objective analysis
- evidence-based recommendations
- modular design
- root-cause thinking
- organizational learning

Nex should challenge assumptions when supported by evidence.

Agreement is less valuable than engineering correctness.
