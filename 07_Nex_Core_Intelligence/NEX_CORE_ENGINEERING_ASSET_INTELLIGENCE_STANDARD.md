# Purpose

This standard defines how Nex Core Intelligence creates, maintains, and utilizes engineering asset knowledge throughout the asset lifecycle.

---

# Core Principle

Assets create value when their technical history becomes organizational intelligence.

---

# Asset Intelligence Objective

Nex shall transform asset information into:

- operational awareness
- maintenance intelligence
- reliability improvement
- lifecycle optimization

---

# Asset Intelligence Model

```

↓
```

# Asset Knowledge Structure

Each engineering asset shall have a structured intelligence record.

---

# Asset Identity

Required information:

```
Asset ID

Asset Name

System

Location

Manufacturer

Model

Serial Number

Installation Date

Owner

Criticality

```

---

# Technical Information

Capture:

- specifications
- design parameters
- operating limits
- manuals
- drawings
- approved settings

---

# Operational Information

Capture:

- operating condition
- usage pattern
- performance data
- abnormalities

---

# Maintenance Intelligence

Record:

```
Maintenance Activity

Date

Finding

Action Taken

Parts Used

Result

```

---

# Failure Intelligence

Capture:

```
Failure Event

Symptom

Detection Method

Root Cause

Corrective Action

Prevention Method

```

---

# Asset Criticality Classification

Assets should be classified based on:

## Safety Impact

Effect on people and compliance.

---

## Operational Impact

Effect on business continuity.

---

## Financial Impact

Repair and replacement cost.

---

## Availability Impact

Effect on system uptime.

# Asset Intelligence Quality Requirements

Every asset record should be:

## Accurate

Correct technical information.

---

## Current

Updated lifecycle information.

---

## Connected

Linked to related knowledge.

---

## Actionable

Useful for decisions.

---

# Asset Review Frequency

Review based on:

- criticality
- operational importance
- lifecycle stage

---

# Anti-Pattern Prevention

## Equipment Database Only

Knowing what exists without knowing what happened.

---

## Missing History

Repeated troubleshooting.

---

## Data Collection Without Use

Information without intelligence.

---

## Asset Isolation

Equipment knowledge disconnected from experience.

---

# Responsibilities

## Francis

Defines critical asset priorities.

Approves lifecycle strategies.

---

## Nex

Maintains asset intelligence model.

Connects operational knowledge.

---

## Engineering Teams

Update asset information.

Capture field experience.

---

# Governing Principle

The best asset management system does not only answer:

"What equipment do we have?"

It answers:

"What do we know about this equipment, and how can we improve it?"
