# Purpose

This standard defines how Nex Core Intelligence, FDG standards, processes, and knowledge structures are changed, improved, and maintained.

---

# Core Principle

Change creates progress only when it preserves what already works.

---

# Evolution Objective

Nex evolution shall improve:

- capability
- reliability
- usability
- intelligence
- scalability

while preserving:

- history
- reasoning
- accountability

---

# Impact Assessment

Evaluate impact on:

## Knowledge

Will existing knowledge remain valid?

---

## Systems

Will integrations be affected?

---

## People

Will responsibilities change?

---

## Operations

Will workflows change?

---

## Risk

Will new risks appear?

---

# Change Approval Levels

## Minor Change

Limited impact.

May follow normal maintenance process.

---

## Major Change

Affects systems, processes, or standards.

Requires review.

---

## Strategic Change

Affects FDG direction.

Requires owner approval.

---


---

# Evolution History

Nex shall preserve:

- previous versions
- reasons for change
- lessons learned
- rejected alternatives when valuable

---

# Change Validation

After implementation verify:

## Intended Result

Was the objective achieved?

---

## Unintended Effects

Did new problems appear?

---

## Adoption

Is the change being used correctly?

---

# Technology Evolution

New technology shall be evaluated by:

- capability improvement
- integration impact
- security
- lifecycle value
- migration risk

---

# Retirement Management

Capabilities may be retired when:

- obsolete
- replaced
- no longer valuable
- maintenance exceeds benefit

---

# Retirement Process

```
Identify Replacement

↓

Preserve Knowledge

↓

Migrate Dependencies

↓

Deactivate

↓

Archive History

```

---

# Anti-Pattern Prevention

## Uncontrolled Change

Changing systems without impact review.

---

## Change Without History

Losing why decisions were made.

---

## Technology Chasing

Adopting tools without strategic value.

---

## Legacy Attachment

Keeping ineffective practices forever.
