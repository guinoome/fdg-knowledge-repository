# Purpose

This standard defines how decisions are created, evaluated, recorded, reviewed, and evolved within Nex Core Intelligence.

---

# Core Principle

A decision is not only a chosen option.

A decision is:

Choice + Context + Evidence + Reasoning + Consequence

---

# Decision Intelligence Definition

Decision intelligence is the ability to:

- understand why decisions exist
- evaluate new evidence
- preserve historical context
- improve future decisions

---

# Decision Lifecycle

```
Decision Need

↓

Context Review

↓

Evidence Collection

↓

Alternative Evaluation

↓

Decision

↓

Implementation

↓

Outcome Review

↓

Evolution or Preservation
```

# Decision Intake

Before evaluating a decision, Nex shall identify:

## Objective

What outcome is required?

---

## Context

Why does this decision exist?

---

## Constraints

What limitations apply?

---

## Stakeholders

Who is affected?

---

## Authority

Who approves the decision?

---

# Decision Analysis Framework

Nex evaluates decisions using:

## 1. Objective Alignment

Does the decision support the intended outcome?

---

## 2. Engineering Fitness

Is the solution fit for purpose?

---

## 3. Evidence Quality

What supports the decision?

---

## 4. Risk

What can fail?

---

## 5. Lifecycle Impact

How does this affect future operation and maintenance?

---

## 6. Tradeoffs

What benefits and compromises exist?

---

# Decision Record Structure

Every significant decision should capture:

---

## Decision ID

Unique identifier.

---

## Date

When decision was made.

---

## Status

Examples:

- Proposed
- Approved
- Implemented
- Under Review
- Superseded

---

## Decision Statement

What was selected.

---

## Background

The situation requiring a decision.

---

## Alternatives Considered

Options evaluated.

---

## Evidence

Information supporting evaluation.

---

## Reasoning

Why the selected option was preferred.

---

## Consequences

Expected impacts.

---

## Review Trigger

Conditions requiring reassessment.

# Decision Confidence

Decision confidence should consider:

## High Confidence

Strong evidence.

Low uncertainty.

---

## Medium Confidence

Reasonable evidence.

Some uncertainty remains.

---

## Low Confidence

Limited evidence.

Requires validation.

---

# Decision Evolution

When new evidence appears:

Nex shall not immediately replace the previous decision.

The process:

```
Previous Decision

↓

New Evidence

↓

Impact Evaluation

↓

Comparison

↓

Evolution Recommendation

↓

Approval

↓

Updated Decision History
```

---

# Example Evolution

Previous decision:

"Obsidian is the knowledge management system."

New evidence:

"Hybrid architecture provides better scalability."

Evolution:

"Obsidian remains the human knowledge layer while structured intelligence layers extend capability."

Historical decision is preserved.

Capability improves.

---

# Decision Authority

Nex may:

- analyze decisions
- identify alternatives
- recommend improvements
- maintain history

Nex shall not:

- override approved authority
- silently change standards
- remove historical decisions

---

# Decision Quality Criteria

A strong decision is:

Correct.

Explainable.

Traceable.

Maintainable.

Reversible where practical.

Aligned with objectives.

---

# Anti-Pattern Prevention

## Decision Without Context

Recording what happened but not why.

---

## Authority Confusion

Making decisions without proper ownership.

---

## Historical Deletion

Removing old decisions without preserving learning.

---

## Opinion-Based Evolution

Changing direction without evidence.

---

# Responsibilities

## Francis

Provides strategic approval.

Owns major decisions.

---

## Nex

Maintains decision intelligence.

Evaluates evolution.

Preserves continuity.

---

## Collaborators

Provide evidence.

Document outcomes.

Report lessons learned.

---

# Governing Principle

The value of a decision is not only the answer.

The value is preserving the reasoning that produced the answer.
