# Purpose

This standard defines the maturity assessment framework used to measure Nex Core Intelligence capability development and guide future evolution.

---

# Core Principle

Maturity is demonstrated capability, not technology quantity.

---

# Maturity Objective

Nex Core maturity evaluation shall measure:

- organizational discipline
- knowledge capability
- engineering intelligence
- automation maturity
- strategic value

---

```

↓
```

---

# Level 0 — Unstructured Intelligence

## Condition

Knowledge and decisions exist primarily through individuals.

---

## Characteristics

- inconsistent processes
- limited documentation
- repeated investigation
- low visibility

---

# Level 1 — Documented Intelligence

## Condition

Basic knowledge capture exists.

---

## Characteristics

- repository created
- procedures documented
- templates available

---

# Level 2 — Managed Intelligence

## Condition

Processes become repeatable.

---

## Characteristics

- workflows established
- decisions recorded
- reviews performed
- knowledge maintained

---

# Level 3 — Integrated Intelligence

## Condition

Systems and knowledge become connected.

---

## Characteristics

- platform integration
- relationship intelligence
- shared context
- improved collaboration

---

# Level 4 — Intelligent Engineering System

## Condition

Nex actively supports engineering decisions.

---

## Characteristics

- advanced analysis
- predictive insights
- automation support
- optimization

---

# Level 5 — Adaptive Engineering Organization

## Condition

The organization continuously improves itself.

---

## Characteristics

- self-improving workflows
- advanced intelligence ecosystem
- organizational learning at scale

# Anti-Pattern Prevention

## Technology-Based Maturity

Assuming more tools mean higher capability.

---

## Artificial Scores

Assigning maturity without evidence.

## Roadmap Without Reality
