This standard defines how Nex Core Intelligence measures, analyzes, and communicates engineering performance.

# 7. Analytics Interpretation Rules

Data must be separated into:

---

# Measurement

The actual value.

Example:

Energy consumption increased 10%.

---

# Analysis

Possible reasons.

Example:

Higher occupancy.

---

# Conclusion

Engineering judgment.

Example:

No equipment degradation identified.

---

# Anti-Pattern Prevention

## Dashboard Without Action

Displaying information without decisions.

---

## Data Overload

Too many metrics with no priority.

---

## False Precision

Numbers without confidence.

---

## Historical Reporting Only

Showing past events without improvement.
