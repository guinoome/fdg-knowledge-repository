# Purpose

This standard defines how Nex Core Intelligence captures, validates, transforms, and applies engineering knowledge gained from FDG activities.

---

# Core Principle

Experience becomes capability only when it is captured and reused.

---

```
Experience

↓

Information

↓

Knowledge

↓

Standard Practice

↓

Organizational Capability

```

# Knowledge Acquisition Sources

Knowledge may originate from:

---

# 1. Engineering Activities

Examples:

- design work
- troubleshooting
- calculations
- commissioning

---

# 2. Operational Experience

Examples:

- equipment behavior
- maintenance activities
- recurring issues

---

# 3. Project Execution

Examples:

- successes
- failures
- improvements

---

# 4. Reviews and Decisions

Examples:

- design choices
- risk evaluations
- optimization decisions

---

# 5. External References

Examples:

- standards
- technical publications
- industry practices

# Knowledge Capture Trigger

Knowledge should be captured when:

- a new solution is discovered
- a problem is solved
- a failure creates learning
- a decision affects future work
- a process improves

---

# Knowledge Capture Process

```
Observe

↓

Capture

↓

Structure

↓

Validate

↓

Store

↓

Connect

↓

Reuse

```

---

# 1. Observation

Identify:

- event
- condition
- problem
- opportunity

---

# 2. Capture

Record:

```
What happened?

Why did it happen?

What was done?

What was learned?

```

---

# 3. Structure

Convert information into:

- knowledge notes
- lessons learned
- procedures
- standards

---

# 4. Validation

Evaluate:

## Accuracy

Is it technically correct?

---

## Applicability

Can it be reused?

---

## Evidence

Is the basis clear?

---

# 5. Storage

Store according to:

- category
- project
- system
- relationship

---

# 6. Connection

Link knowledge to:

- assets
- projects
- decisions
- standards

---

# 7. Reuse

Apply knowledge to:

- future analysis
- design decisions
- operational improvement

---

# Knowledge Maturity Levels

---

# Level 1 — Captured

Information recorded.

---

# Level 2 — Structured

Organized and searchable.

---

# Level 3 — Validated

Reviewed and reliable.

---

# Level 4 — Reusable

Applied successfully.

---

# Level 5 — Standardized

Converted into organizational practice.

# Anti-Pattern Prevention

## Knowledge Hoarding

Information remaining with individuals.

---

## Capture Without Validation

Saving unreliable information.

---

## Lessons Without Action

Recording problems without improvement.

---

## Knowledge Without Retrieval

Creating information nobody uses.
