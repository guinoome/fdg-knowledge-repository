# Purpose

This standard defines how FDG captures, validates, and transforms experience into reusable organizational knowledge.

---

# Core Principle

Experience becomes valuable only when converted into reusable capability.

---

# Knowledge Transformation Model

```
Experience

↓

Observation

↓

Lesson Learned

↓

Validated Knowledge

↓

Standard

↓

Organizational Capability
```

---

# Knowledge Capture Objectives

Knowledge capture shall:

- preserve important learning
- prevent repeated mistakes
- improve future decisions
- strengthen organizational capability

---

# Capture Triggers

Knowledge capture should occur after:

## Project Completion

Examples:

- successful implementation
- failures
- major changes

---

## Problem Resolution

Examples:

- troubleshooting
- root cause analysis
- corrective actions

---

## Major Decisions

Examples:

- technology selection
- design decisions

---

## Process Improvement

Examples:

- workflow changes
- efficiency improvements

---

## Significant Events

Examples:

- operational incidents
- unusual conditions

---

# Lesson Learned Structure

Every lesson learned should answer:

```
What happened?

Why did it happen?

What was learned?

What should be repeated?

What should be avoided?

How should FDG improve?

```

## Operational Knowledge

- procedures

## Decision Knowledge

## Organizational Knowledge

# Validation Requirement

Lessons learned should be reviewed before becoming standards.

Validation should confirm:

- accuracy
- applicability
- repeatability
- usefulness

# Failed Experience Handling

Failures shall be treated as learning sources.

A failure record should capture:

## Event

What happened?

---

## Impact

What was affected?

---

## Root Cause

Why did it occur?

---

## Corrective Action

What was done?

---

## Preventive Action

How will recurrence be reduced?

---

# Knowledge Reuse Principle

Before solving a recurring problem:

Search existing knowledge.

Reuse validated solutions.

Improve when necessary.

---

# Knowledge Quality Criteria

Good knowledge should be:

## Clear

Understandable by others.

---

## Relevant

Useful for future work.

---

## Validated

Based on evidence.

---

## Connected

Linked to related knowledge.

---

## Maintainable

Able to evolve.

---

# Knowledge Ownership

Every significant knowledge item should have:

- source
- owner
- validation status
- related systems
- review date

---

# Anti-Pattern Prevention

## Experience Loss

Problems solved but lessons forgotten.

---

## Blame Culture

Hiding failures instead of learning.

---

## Unvalidated Lessons

Turning assumptions into standards.

---

## Knowledge Hoarding

Important knowledge existing only in individuals.

---

# Responsibilities

## Francis

Defines strategic lessons.

---

## Nex

---

## Contributors

Capture experiences.

Provide evidence.

---

# Governing Principle

Every completed challenge should make FDG stronger.

The goal is not merely solving today's problem.

The goal is reducing tomorrow's uncertainty.
