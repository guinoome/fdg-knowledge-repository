# Purpose

This standard defines the measurement framework used by Nex Core Intelligence to evaluate engineering performance, knowledge growth, system effectiveness, and organizational capability.

---

# Core Principle

Measure what matters.

Do not optimize what does not create value.

---

# Analytics Objective

Nex analytics shall support:

- better decisions
- improved reliability
- reduced waste
- increased capability

---

# Analytics Model

```
Data Collection

↓

Data Validation

↓

Analysis

↓

Insight Generation

↓

Decision Support

↓

Improvement

```

---

# Metric Categories

Nex Core metrics are grouped into:

```
Engineering Metrics

Knowledge Metrics

Decision Metrics

Automation Metrics

Risk Metrics

Capability Metrics

```

---

# 1. Engineering Performance Metrics

Measure engineering execution quality.

---

## Work Completion

Examples:

- completed work packages
- overdue activities
- resolution time

---

## Reliability

Examples:

- failure frequency
- repeat failures
- corrective action effectiveness

---

## Efficiency

Examples:

- response time
- resource utilization
- process cycle time

---

# 2. Knowledge Metrics

Measure organizational intelligence growth.

---

## Knowledge Creation

Examples:

- new validated records
- lessons captured

---

## Knowledge Reuse

Examples:

- reused solutions
- referenced standards

---

## Knowledge Quality

Evaluate:

- completeness
- accuracy
- relevance

---

# 3. Decision Intelligence Metrics

Measure decision quality.

---

## Decision Traceability

Evaluate:

- recorded assumptions
- evidence availability
- reasoning clarity

---

## Decision Effectiveness

Evaluate:

- expected outcome
- actual result
- improvement achieved

---

# 4. Automation Metrics

Measure automation value.

---

## Reliability

Evaluate:

- successful execution rate
- failure frequency

---

## Efficiency

Evaluate:

- time saved
- manual effort reduced

---

## Control

Evaluate:

- auditability
- human oversight
- exception handling

---

# 5. Risk Metrics

Measure uncertainty management.

---

## Risk Identification

Evaluate:

- detected risks
- emerging risks

---

## Risk Control

Evaluate:

- mitigation effectiveness
- unresolved high risks

---

# 6. Capability Maturity Metrics

Measure long-term development.

---

## Foundation Capability

Examples:

- documentation discipline
- workflow adoption

---

## Integration Capability

Examples:

- system connectivity
- information flow

---

## Intelligence Capability

Examples:

- analysis support
- automation maturity

---

# Metric Quality Requirements

Good metrics must be:

## Relevant

Connected to objectives.

---

## Reliable

Based on accurate data.

---

## Comparable

Able to show change over time.

---

## Actionable

Able to influence decisions.

---

# Data Quality Requirements

Before analysis verify:

- source reliability
- completeness
- consistency
- calculation method

---

# Analytics Limitations

Metrics should not replace:

- engineering judgment
- professional review
- contextual understanding

---

# Anti-Pattern Prevention

## Metric Collection Without Purpose

Gathering data without decisions.

---

## Vanity Metrics

Numbers that look good but provide no value.

---

## False Precision

Showing accuracy beyond available evidence.

---

## Ignoring Qualitative Knowledge

Removing human expertise from decisions.
