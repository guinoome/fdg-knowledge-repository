# Purpose

This standard defines how FDG measures, evaluates, and improves engineering performance and organizational capability.

---

# Core Principle

Performance improvement is not simply doing more work.

Performance improvement is creating better outcomes with increasing reliability.

---

# Performance Objective

Nex shall support improvement by measuring:

- effectiveness
- efficiency
- quality
- reliability
- learning
- capability growth

---

# Performance Dimensions

---

# 1. Engineering Effectiveness

Measures:

"Did we achieve the intended result?"

Examples:

- requirement satisfaction
- problem resolution
- project success

---

# 2. Engineering Efficiency

Measures:

"How much effort was required?"

Examples:

- time reduction
- workflow improvement
- resource utilization

---

# 3. Engineering Quality

Measures:

"How reliable was the output?"

Examples:

- error reduction
- review findings
- rework frequency

---

# 4. Engineering Reliability

Measures:

"Can the capability consistently perform?"

Examples:

- system availability
- process stability
- failure frequency

---

# 5. Knowledge Growth

Measures:

"Is FDG becoming smarter?"

Examples:

- validated lessons captured
- improved standards
- reusable solutions

---

# 6. Automation Value

Measures:

"Did automation create meaningful improvement?"

Examples:

- time saved
- errors reduced
- visibility improved

---

# Performance Indicators

Metrics should be:

## Relevant

Connected to objectives.

---

## Measurable

Based on evidence.

---

## Actionable

Able to drive improvement.

---

## Sustainable

Practical to maintain.

---

# Engineering Capability Maturity

FDG capability can evolve through:

---

## Level 1 — Reactive

Problems solved after occurrence.

---

## Level 2 — Managed

Processes documented.

---

## Level 3 — Standardized

Methods consistently applied.

---

## Level 4 — Optimized

Performance continuously improved.

---

## Level 5 — Intelligent

Knowledge-driven adaptive capability.

# Anti-Pattern Prevention

## Measuring Activity Instead of Value

Counting work instead of outcomes.

---

## Metric Overload

Collecting data without purpose.

---

## Short-Term Optimization

Improving speed while reducing quality.

---

## Improvement Without Standardization

Making gains that disappear.
