# Purpose

This standard defines the maturity model used to assess the development and capability level of Nex Core Intelligence.

---

# Core Principle

Maturity is not complexity.

Maturity is reliable capability.

---

# Maturity Assessment Objective

The assessment identifies:

- current capability level
- strengths
- weaknesses
- improvement priorities
- future development direction

---

# FDG Intelligence Maturity Model

Nex Core evolves through five maturity levels:

```
Level 1

Reactive


↓

Level 2

Structured


↓

Level 3

Integrated


↓

Level 4

Intelligent


↓

Level 5

Adaptive
```

---

# Level 1 — Reactive Capability

## Characteristics

Knowledge exists mainly in individuals.

Processes depend on personal experience.

Problems are solved after occurrence.

---

## Typical Condition

- limited documentation
- inconsistent methods
- low reuse

---

# Level 2 — Structured Capability

## Characteristics

Basic systems and standards exist.

Knowledge begins to become organized.

---

## Typical Condition

- documented procedures
- defined workflows
- repeatable practices

---

# Level 3 — Integrated Capability

## Characteristics

Systems, knowledge, and processes are connected.

---

## Typical Condition

- shared repository
- linked information
- coordinated workflows

---

# Level 4 — Intelligent Capability

## Characteristics

Knowledge actively supports decisions.

---

## Typical Condition

- intelligent retrieval
- analytical support
- automation
- predictive improvement

---

# Level 5 — Adaptive Capability

## Characteristics

The organization continuously learns and optimizes itself.

---

## Typical Condition

- self-improving systems
- advanced analytics
- adaptive processes

---

## Gap

## Improvement Action

# Improvement Prioritization

Prioritize based on:

## Strategic Value

Contribution to FDG objectives.

---

## Capability Impact

How much improvement it creates.

---

## Effort

Resources required.

---

## Risk Reduction

Problems prevented.

---

# Assessment Frequency

Review maturity:

- periodically
- after major evolution
- after major system changes

---

# Anti-Pattern Prevention

## Complexity as Maturity

Assuming more technology means higher capability.

---

## Measurement Without Improvement

Assessing without action.

---

## Artificial Scoring

Creating numbers without evidence.

---

## Premature Optimization

Improving advanced capabilities before foundations exist.
