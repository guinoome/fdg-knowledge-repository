# Purpose

This standard defines the relationship between Nex Core Intelligence and the FDG Knowledge Repository.

---

# Core Principle

Knowledge is the foundation of intelligence.

Nex does not replace the knowledge repository.

Nex operates through it.

---

# FDG Knowledge Architecture

The FDG Ecosystem separates:

Knowledge Management

Execution Management

Operational Systems

Intelligence Orchestration

---

# System Roles

## Obsidian

Role:

Human Intelligence Layer.

Purpose:

Preserve:

- principles
- standards
- decisions
- engineering reasoning
- organizational memory

Characteristics:

Long-term.

Human-readable.

Strategic.

---

## Notion

Role:

Work Management Layer.

Purpose:

Manage:

- tasks
- projects
- schedules
- execution tracking

Characteristics:

Operational.

Action-oriented.

Current work focused.

---

## Structured Databases

Role:

Operational Data Layer.

Purpose:

Manage:

- equipment records
- calculations
- transactions
- inventory
- structured information

Characteristics:

Queryable.

Precise.

System-oriented.

---

## Nex Core Intelligence

Role:

Intelligence Orchestration Layer.

Purpose:

Connect:

- objectives
- knowledge
- decisions
- collaborators
- execution

Characteristics:

Reasoning-oriented.

Evolution-oriented.

Context-aware.

# Obsidian Governance

Obsidian shall contain:

## Constitutional Knowledge

Examples:

- FDG Constitution
- Nex Constitution
- Engineering Principles

---

## Organizational Standards

Examples:

- operating standards
- workflows
- governance documents

---

## Decision Intelligence

Examples:

- decision records
- evolution history

---

## Engineering Knowledge

Examples:

- methods
- calculations
- lessons learned

---

# Obsidian Shall Not Become

Obsidian shall not become:

- a task manager
- a transaction database
- an uncontrolled document dump
- a replacement for specialized systems

---

# Knowledge Flow

The approved knowledge cycle:

```
Experience

↓

Capture

↓

Validate

↓

Structure

↓

Store

↓

Retrieve

↓

Apply

↓

Improve

```

# Repository Structure Principle

The repository shall be organized by intelligence value.

Not by:

- random file accumulation
- application limitations
- temporary projects only

# Knowledge Promotion

Information should move through maturity levels:

```
Note

↓

Structured Knowledge

↓

Validated Knowledge

↓

Standard

↓

Organizational Capability
```

---

# Knowledge Ownership

Every important knowledge item should have:

- owner
- status
- version
- review date
- source

---

# Synchronization Principle

Knowledge should synchronize between systems according to purpose.

Not all systems require identical copies.

Each system maintains its intended role.

---

# Future Intelligence Extension

Future systems may include:

- semantic retrieval
- vector databases
- knowledge graphs
- automated indexing
- engineering reasoning systems

These extend the repository.

They do not replace the knowledge foundation.

---

# Anti-Pattern Prevention

## Tool-Centric Knowledge

Designing knowledge around software limitations.

---

## Conversation Memory Dependency

Allowing important decisions to remain only in chats.

---

## Duplicate Truth Sources

Creating multiple competing knowledge repositories.

---

## Unstructured Accumulation

Saving information without classification.

---

# Responsibilities

## Francis

Defines strategic knowledge.

Approves major structure.

---

## Nex

Maintains knowledge continuity.

Identifies missing intelligence.

Ensures decisions become reusable.

---

## Collaborators

Contribute validated knowledge.

Follow repository standards.

---

# Governing Principle

Obsidian preserves intelligence.

Systems execute work.

Nex connects them into a learning engineering organization.
