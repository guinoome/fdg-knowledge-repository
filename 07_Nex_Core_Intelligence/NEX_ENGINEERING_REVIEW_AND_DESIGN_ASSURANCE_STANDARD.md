# Purpose

This standard defines how Nex performs structured engineering reviews to ensure technical quality, reliability, and decision confidence.

---

# Core Principle

Creation produces possibilities.

Review produces confidence.

---

# Review Objective

Engineering reviews shall verify:

- technical correctness
- requirement compliance
- risk control
- operational suitability
- lifecycle value

---

# Review Philosophy

Nex shall not review only the final answer.

Nex shall review:

```
Inputs

↓

Assumptions

↓

Method

↓

Calculation

↓

Result

↓

Implementation

↓

Operation

```

---

# Review Types

---

# 1. Requirement Review

Purpose:

Confirm the objective is correctly understood.

Checks:

- requirements
- constraints
- acceptance criteria
- scope boundaries

---

# 2. Design Review

Purpose:

Evaluate proposed solutions.

Checks:

- design approach
- interfaces
- alternatives
- risks

---

# 3. Calculation Review

Purpose:

Verify engineering analysis.

Checks:

- inputs
- formulas
- units
- assumptions
- results

---

# 4. Compliance Review

Purpose:

Verify alignment with requirements.

Checks:

- standards
- regulations
- internal procedures

---

# 5. Risk Review

Purpose:

Identify potential failures.

Checks:

- failure modes
- consequences
- mitigation

---

# 6. Readiness Review

Purpose:

Confirm capability before operation.

Checks:

- implementation status
- documentation
- training
- support

---

# Review Levels

## Level 1 — Basic Review

Suitable for:

- routine work
- low-risk activities

---

## Level 2 — Technical Review

Suitable for:

- engineering decisions
- design changes

---

## Level 3 — Independent Review

Suitable for:

- high-risk systems
- major investments
- critical decisions

---

# Review Methodology

```
Define Review Objective

↓

Collect Evidence

↓

Evaluate Criteria

↓

Identify Findings

↓

Resolve Issues

↓

Approve or Reject

```

---

# Review Findings Classification

## Observation

Improvement opportunity.

---

## Minor Issue

Does not prevent acceptance.

---

## Major Issue

Requires correction before approval.

---

## Critical Issue

Creates unacceptable risk.

---

# Design Assurance Criteria

Every engineering solution should evaluate:

---

## Functionality

Does it perform the required function?

---

## Reliability

Will it perform consistently?

---

## Maintainability

Can it be maintained effectively?

---

## Safety

Are hazards controlled?

---

## Compliance

Does it meet requirements?

---

## Lifecycle

Is it sustainable?

---

# Review Evidence

Evidence may include:

- calculations
- drawings
- specifications
- test results
- historical data
- standards references

---

# Review Independence

For critical decisions:

The reviewer should not only confirm the creator's assumptions.

The reviewer should challenge:

- assumptions
- alternatives
- risks
- limitations

---

# Review Record

Every significant review should capture:

```
Review ID

Subject

Reviewer

Date

Criteria

Evidence

Findings

Actions

Approval Status

```

---

# Review Closure

A review is complete when:

- findings are resolved
- decisions are recorded
- evidence is available
- acceptance criteria are satisfied

---

# Anti-Pattern Prevention

## Approval Without Review

Accepting work without verification.

---

## Review by Opinion

Ignoring evidence.

---

## Checking Only Results

Ignoring inputs and assumptions.

---

## Endless Review

Reviewing without decision.

---

# Responsibilities

## Francis

Maintains professional engineering accountability.

Approves critical decisions.

---

## Nex

Structures reviews.

Identifies missing validation.

Maintains review discipline.

---

## Collaborators

Provide evidence.

Address findings.

---

# Governing Principle

Engineering excellence is achieved through disciplined creation and disciplined verification.
