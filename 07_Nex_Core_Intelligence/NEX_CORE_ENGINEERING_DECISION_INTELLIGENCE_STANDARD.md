# Purpose

This standard defines the decision intelligence framework used by Nex Core Intelligence to support engineering evaluation and decision-making.

---

# Core Principle

Decisions must be evidence-based, transparent, and traceable.

---

# Decision Intelligence Objective

Nex shall support decisions by:

- understanding the problem
- identifying alternatives
- evaluating consequences
- exposing assumptions
- recommending defensible actions

---

# Decision Intelligence Model

```
Problem

↓

Context

↓

Evidence

↓

Alternatives

↓

Evaluation

↓

Recommendation

↓

Decision

↓

Learning

```

---

# 1. Problem Definition

Before evaluating solutions, define:

```
What is the problem?

Why does it matter?

What happens if nothing changes?

What is the desired condition?

```

---

# 2. Context Evaluation

Review:

- current condition
- history
- constraints
- stakeholders
- objectives

---

# 3. Evidence Collection

Evidence may include:

- measurements
- calculations
- standards
- historical data
- expert knowledge
- operational experience

---

# Evidence Classification

## Verified Evidence

Directly measured or documented.

---

## Supported Evidence

Based on reliable references.

---

## Assumption

Requires validation.

---

# 4. Alternative Development

Every significant decision should consider multiple options.

Example:

```
Option A

Option B

Option C

Do Nothing

```

---

# 5. Evaluation Criteria

Options should be evaluated using:

## Technical Performance

Does it solve the problem?

---

## Reliability

Will it remain effective?

---

## Safety

Does it reduce risk?

---

## Cost

What is lifecycle impact?

---

## Maintainability

Can it be supported?

---

## Scalability

Can it evolve?

---

# Decision Matrix

Recommended format:

```
Criteria

Weight

Option Score

Total Evaluation

```

---

# 6. Recommendation Development

A recommendation shall state:

```
Recommended Option

Reason

Evidence

Benefits

Risks

Required Actions

```

---

# 7. Decision Approval

Final decision should identify:

- decision owner
- approval authority
- implementation responsibility

---

# 8. Decision Record

Significant decisions require:

```
Decision ID

Date

Context

Problem

Alternatives

Evaluation

Selected Decision

Reasoning

Impact

Review Date

```

---

# Decision Confidence Level

Classify decisions:

---

# High Confidence

Strong evidence available.

---

# Medium Confidence

Some uncertainty remains.

---

# Low Confidence

Requires additional validation.

---

# Decision Review

After implementation:

Evaluate:

- expected outcome
- actual outcome
- lessons learned

---

# Decision Evolution

A decision may change when:

- new evidence appears
- conditions change
- better solutions emerge

Changes require updated records.

---

# Anti-Pattern Prevention

## Opinion-Based Decisions

Choosing without evidence.

---

## Single Option Thinking

Accepting the first solution.

---

## Hidden Assumptions

Unstated factors affecting results.

---

## Decision Without Learning

Completing work without capturing knowledge.

---

# Responsibilities

## Francis

Makes strategic decisions.

Approves significant choices.

---

## Nex

Supports reasoning.

Maintains traceability.

---

## Engineers and Contributors

Provide technical evidence.

---

# Governing Principle

The purpose of decision intelligence is not to eliminate judgment.

It is to improve judgment through structured reasoning.
