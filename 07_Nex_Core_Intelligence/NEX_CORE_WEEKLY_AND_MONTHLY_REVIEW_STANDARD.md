# Purpose

This standard defines the recurring review process used to evaluate Nex Core performance, knowledge quality, operational effectiveness, and improvement opportunities.

---

# Core Principle

Review converts experience into organizational intelligence.

---

# Review Framework

```
Observe

↓

Analyze

↓

Decide

↓

Improve

↓

Measure

```

---

# Weekly Review

## Objective

Maintain operational awareness and identify short-term improvements.

---

# Weekly Review Inputs

Review:

- completed work packages
- active issues
- decisions made
- lessons learned
- recurring problems
- pending improvements

---

# Weekly Review Agenda

```
1. Previous Actions

2. Completed Work

3. Current Issues

4. Decision Review

5. Knowledge Capture Review

6. Improvement Opportunities

7. Next Priorities

```

---

# Weekly Review Outputs

Create:

- updated work priorities
- improvement actions
- new knowledge entries
- decision records when required

---

# Monthly Review

## Objective

Evaluate system performance and strategic alignment.

---

# Monthly Review Inputs

Review:

- performance indicators
- workflow effectiveness
- knowledge growth
- automation value
- maturity progress

---

# Monthly Review Areas

---

# 1. Knowledge Health

Evaluate:

- quality of captured knowledge
- reuse frequency
- outdated information
- missing knowledge areas

---

# 2. Decision Quality

Evaluate:

- decision traceability
- evidence quality
- review effectiveness

---

# 3. Workflow Performance

Evaluate:

- efficiency
- bottlenecks
- unnecessary steps

---

# 4. Automation Performance

Evaluate:

- reliability
- usefulness
- maintenance requirements

---

# 5. Capability Growth

Evaluate:

- maturity improvement
- new capabilities
- remaining gaps

---

# Monthly Improvement Prioritization

Improvements should be evaluated by:

```
Impact

+

Urgency

+

Effort

+

Strategic Value

```

---

# Review Record

Each review should capture:

```
Review Date

Participants

Key Findings

Decisions

Actions

Owners

Due Dates

Follow-up Review

```

---

# Escalation Criteria

Issues require higher review when they involve:

- safety impact
- major cost impact
- strategic decisions
- system architecture changes
- significant risks

---

# Review Feedback Loop

```
Operational Experience

↓

Review

↓

Improvement Decision

↓

Implementation

↓

Performance Measurement

```

---

# Knowledge Evolution Rules

During reviews:

## Promote

Useful temporary information into permanent knowledge.

---

## Update

Existing knowledge when improved.

---

## Archive

Knowledge that is obsolete.

---

# Anti-Pattern Prevention

## Review Without Action

Finding problems without improvement.

---

## Metrics Without Meaning

Tracking numbers without decisions.

---

## Repeating Problems

Failure to capture learning.

---

## Management Distance

Reviewing systems without understanding operation.

---
