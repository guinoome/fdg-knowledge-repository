# Purpose

This standard defines how captured engineering knowledge is reviewed before it becomes approved organizational knowledge.

The purpose of review is to verify engineering quality, organizational value, and long-term reusability.

---

# Core Principle

Review validates engineering knowledge.

Approval authorizes organizational use.

These are separate governance activities.

---

# Objectives

Knowledge Review shall determine whether captured knowledge is:

- technically correct
- supported by evidence
- organizationally valuable
- reusable
- suitable for approval

---

# Review Workflow

Captured Knowledge

↓

Technical Review

↓

Evidence Review

↓

Organizational Review

↓

Knowledge Quality Assessment

↓

Review Decision

↓

Knowledge Approval

---

# Technical Review

Verify:

- engineering correctness
- consistency with approved standards
- completeness
- internal consistency
- engineering feasibility

Engineering errors shall be corrected before approval.

---

# Evidence Review

Verify:

- supporting calculations
- standards references
- validated observations
- engineering rationale
- documented assumptions

Claims without sufficient evidence shall not proceed to approval.

---

# Organizational Review

Evaluate:

- future usefulness
- cross-project applicability
- contribution to engineering capability
- impact on organizational maturity
- alignment with FDG governance

Knowledge with limited organizational value should remain project-specific.

---

# Knowledge Quality Assessment

Knowledge shall be assessed against the following characteristics.

## Accuracy

Technically correct.

---

## Clarity

Easy to understand.

---

## Completeness

Contains sufficient engineering context.

---

## Traceability

Reasoning and evidence are documented.

---

## Reusability

Useful beyond the original project.

---

## Maintainability

Can be updated through controlled evolution.

---

# Review Outcomes

## Approved for Knowledge Approval

Proceed to approval.

---

## Revision Required

Additional work is required before approval.

---

## Project Reference Only

Useful for the originating project but not suitable for organizational standards.

---

## Rejected

Does not meet engineering or organizational requirements.

---

# Review Responsibility

Francis

Exercises engineering judgment for strategic knowledge.

---

Nex

Performs structured engineering review.

Identifies conflicts.

Detects duplication.

Checks alignment with approved standards.

Recommends review outcomes.

---

Specialized Collaborators

Provide technical expertise supporting the review.

They do not establish organizational baselines.

---

# Review Checklist

Before recommending approval, verify:

✓ Engineering objective remains valid.

✓ Existing standards have been reviewed.

✓ Engineering reasoning is documented.

✓ Supporting evidence is sufficient.

✓ Knowledge adds organizational value.

✓ Duplication has been avoided.

✓ Historical reasoning has been preserved.

---

# Review Rules

Knowledge shall never bypass review.

Review shall always occur before approval.

Engineering correctness takes precedence over convenience.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Review quality should improve as organizational knowledge matures.

Review criteria may evolve through controlled evolution.

Historical review decisions shall remain traceable.

---

# Governing Principle

Knowledge becomes trustworthy through disciplined engineering review.

Trust is earned through evidence, not assumption.
