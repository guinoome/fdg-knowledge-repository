# Purpose

This standard defines how approved organizational knowledge evolves while preserving engineering integrity, historical reasoning, and organizational continuity.

Knowledge Evolution ensures the FDG Knowledge Repository continuously improves without losing its approved baseline.

---

# Core Principle

Approved knowledge is not rewritten.

Approved knowledge evolves.

Every improvement begins with the current approved baseline.

---

# Evolution Objectives

Knowledge Evolution shall:

- preserve organizational continuity
- incorporate validated new evidence
- improve engineering capability
- maintain traceability
- protect historical reasoning
- strengthen organizational intelligence

---

# Evolution Rules

Every proposed change shall first answer:

## What is the current approved baseline?

---

## Why is change required?

---

## What new evidence supports the change?

---

## Can the existing standard be extended?

---

## Is a new standard truly necessary?

If the answer is "No,"

extend the existing standard.

---

# Sources of Evolution

Approved knowledge may evolve through:

- operational experience
- engineering lessons learned
- validated research
- regulatory changes
- technological advances
- organizational maturity
- improved engineering methods

Opinion alone shall not justify evolution.

---

# Evidence Requirements

Evolution proposals shall document:

- current baseline
- identified limitation
- supporting evidence
- engineering reasoning
- expected organizational benefit
- implementation impact

---

# Version Management

Each approved evolution shall:

- receive a new version number
- preserve previous versions
- document change history
- maintain backward traceability
- identify affected standards

Knowledge history is cumulative.

Not disposable.

---

# Evolution Decision Outcomes

## Approved Evolution

The proposal improves the approved baseline.

---

## Deferred

Additional evidence or organizational maturity is required.

---

## Rejected

Current baseline remains valid.

The proposal is archived for future reference.

---

# Evolution Checklist

Before proposing change, verify:

✓ Current approved baseline reviewed.

✓ Existing decisions understood.

✓ Historical reasoning preserved.

✓ New evidence documented.

✓ Organizational benefit demonstrated.

✓ Duplication avoided.

✓ Version impact identified.

---

# Evolution Rules

Knowledge Evolution shall never:

- redesign approved systems without review
- discard historical reasoning
- overwrite organizational history
- create competing authoritative standards
- replace governance with preference

Evolution improves the baseline.

It does not replace it.

---

# Organizational Principle

Every generation of engineering knowledge should begin where the previous generation ended.

Organizational intelligence accumulates through disciplined evolution.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Engineering standards should become increasingly:

- accurate
- reusable
- connected
- maintainable
- evidence-based

Each approved evolution strengthens future engineering decisions.

---

# Governing Principle

The FDG Ecosystem advances through controlled evolution rather than repeated reinvention.
