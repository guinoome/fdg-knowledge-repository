# Purpose

This standard defines how reviewed engineering knowledge becomes approved organizational knowledge within the FDG Ecosystem.

Approval establishes the official engineering baseline for future work.

---

# Core Principle

Review determines technical quality.

Approval establishes organizational authority.

Only approved knowledge becomes part of the FDG engineering baseline.

---

# Approval Objectives

The approval process shall:

- establish engineering authority
- maintain a single approved baseline
- prevent conflicting standards
- preserve organizational consistency
- support controlled evolution

---

# Approval Workflow

---

# Approval Evaluation

Before approval, verify:

- technical review is complete
- engineering objectives remain valid
- organizational value is demonstrated
- duplication has been eliminated
- governance requirements are satisfied

---

# Baseline Verification

Approval shall confirm that the proposed knowledge:

- aligns with existing standards, or
- intentionally evolves an approved standard.

If an approved standard already exists:

Extend it.

Do not create competing standards.

---

# Approval Decisions

## Approved

The knowledge becomes part of the official FDG Knowledge Repository.

---

## Approved with Revision

Minor modifications are required before publication.

The engineering decision is approved.

Editorial improvements remain.

---

## Deferred

The knowledge has value but requires additional evidence, validation, or organizational maturity.

---

## Rejected

The knowledge shall not become an organizational standard.

Historical records remain for traceability.

---

# Authority

Strategic engineering standards shall be approved by Francis.

Nex shall recommend approval decisions but shall not independently establish organizational baselines.

Specialized collaborators may recommend improvements but do not possess approval authority.

---

# Version Assignment

Every approved document shall receive:

- Document ID
- Version number
- Approval status
- Approval date
- Change history

Version control shall comply with the Document Control Standard.

---

# Repository Integration

Approved knowledge shall be integrated into:

- Maps of Content
- Cross-references
- Related standards
- Templates (when applicable)
- Architecture Decision Records (when applicable)

Knowledge becomes discoverable, not merely stored.

---

# Approval Checklist

Before approval, verify:

✓ Technical review completed.

✓ Engineering reasoning documented.

✓ Supporting evidence available.

✓ Existing standards reviewed.

✓ Baseline conflicts resolved.

✓ Version assigned.

✓ Cross-references updated.

✓ Repository integration completed.

---

# Approval Rules

Approval shall never:

- bypass engineering review
- overwrite approved history
- create duplicate standards
- remove historical reasoning
- weaken governance

Approval establishes a new baseline.

It does not erase previous baselines.

---

# Organizational Principle

A repository grows through contribution.

An engineering organization grows through approved standards.

Approval transforms knowledge into organizational authority.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Approval decisions shall remain traceable.

Future improvements shall occur through controlled evolution rather than replacement.

Every approved standard strengthens the engineering maturity of the FDG Ecosystem.

---

# Governing Principle

Organizational intelligence is defined by what the organization has approved, not by what it has discussed.
