# Purpose

This standard defines how engineering knowledge progresses from experience to reusable organizational intelligence.

The lifecycle ensures engineering knowledge is validated, governed, preserved, and continuously improved.

---

# Core Principle

Knowledge is not created by writing.

Knowledge is created through disciplined engineering review and approval.

Information becomes organizational intelligence only after governance.

---

# Knowledge Lifecycle

Engineering Experience

↓

Knowledge Capture

↓

Engineering Review

↓

Engineering Approval

↓

Knowledge Preservation

↓

Controlled Evolution

↓

Organizational Intelligence

↓

Improved Engineering Capability

Every stage adds value.

No stage may be bypassed.

---

# Stage 1 — Engineering Experience

Purpose

Generate new engineering observations through execution.

Sources include:

- engineering projects
- operational experience
- calculations
- failures
- successes
- research
- design decisions
- lessons learned

Output

Raw engineering experience.

---

# Stage 2 — Knowledge Capture

Purpose

Document engineering experience before it is forgotten.

Capture shall focus on:

- decisions
- reasoning
- assumptions
- evidence
- constraints
- lessons learned

Output

Captured engineering knowledge.

---

# Stage 3 — Engineering Review

Purpose

Validate technical correctness and organizational relevance.

Review verifies:

- correctness
- completeness
- consistency
- applicability
- evidence

Output

Reviewed knowledge.

---

# Stage 4 — Engineering Approval

Purpose

Determine whether reviewed knowledge becomes an approved organizational standard.

Possible outcomes:

Approved

Revision Required

Rejected

Only approved knowledge becomes part of the organizational baseline.

Output

Approved organizational knowledge.

---

# Stage 5 — Knowledge Preservation

Purpose

Protect approved knowledge for long-term organizational reuse.

Preservation includes:

- version control
- document control
- repository integration
- cross-referencing
- traceability

Output

Reusable organizational intelligence.

---

# Stage 6 — Controlled Evolution

Purpose

Improve approved knowledge using validated new evidence.

Evolution shall:

review the current baseline

preserve historical reasoning

document justification

assign a new version

maintain traceability

Evolution strengthens knowledge.

It does not replace history.

Output

Improved engineering standards.

---

# Stage 7 — Organizational Intelligence

Purpose

Integrate approved knowledge into the engineering capability of the FDG Ecosystem.

Organizational intelligence exists when approved knowledge is consistently reused to improve future engineering decisions.

Output

Improved engineering maturity.

---

# Lifecycle Rules

Knowledge shall never move directly from experience to organizational standards.

Every transition requires governance.

---

# Engineering Principles

Knowledge shall be:

reviewed

approved

versioned

traceable

reusable

continuously improved

---

# Knowledge Flow

Raw Information

↓

Engineering Context

↓

Engineering Knowledge

↓

Approved Standard

↓

Organizational Intelligence

↓

Engineering Capability

Information alone has limited value.

Engineering capability is the objective.

---

# Organizational Learning

Every completed engineering activity shall answer:

What should future engineers never need to rediscover?

That knowledge should enter the lifecycle.

---

# Success Measures

The lifecycle succeeds when:

- repeated mistakes decrease
- engineering consistency increases
- onboarding time decreases
- design quality improves
- organizational capability grows
- approved standards evolve with evidence

---

# Governing Principle

The value of engineering knowledge is measured not by how much is stored, but by how consistently it improves future engineering decisions.
