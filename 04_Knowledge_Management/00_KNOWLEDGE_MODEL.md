# Purpose

This document defines the knowledge management architecture of the FDG Ecosystem.

Knowledge Management governs how engineering experience becomes reusable organizational intelligence.

It does not define repository structure.

It defines the lifecycle of engineering knowledge.

---

# Core Principle

Knowledge is the primary engineering asset of the FDG Ecosystem.

Engineering capability improves through disciplined knowledge management rather than accumulated information.

# Engineering Principle

Knowledge Management governs engineering intelligence.

Knowledge Architecture governs repository organization.

The two shall remain independent.

---

# Organizational Principle

Experience has value once.

Approved knowledge has value repeatedly.

Organizational intelligence is the accumulated result of governed engineering knowledge.
