# FDG-ML-DEP-STD-015
# Latest Digital Invitation Design Modernization Instruction
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Engineering Instruction  
**System:** ML Digital Event Platform (ML-DEP)  
**Owner:** FDG Ecosystem  
**Priority:** High  
**Scope:** All invitation templates, preview thumbnails, music backgrounds, and template navigation layout

---

## 1. Objective

Modernize the full digital invitation template library so it feels current, premium, and easy to browse on both desktop and mobile. The library must be updated toward the latest visual language used in modern virtual invitations, including photo-based, animated, floral, gold foil, luxury-styled, kids birthday, milestone birthday, wedding, debut, and other event-driven designs.

The template library must be inspired by Canva-style invitation ecosystems, including virtual invitation templates, birthday invitation templates, invitation size guidance, and ready-made customizable templates for any occasion. The platform must also support clear audio/background music for invitation videos and provide a thumbnail-first browsing experience for fast selection. ## 2. Design Direction

All templates shall be refreshed into one or more of the following style families:

- Photo-based invitation templates
- Animated video invitation templates
- Floral invitation templates
- Gold foil and metallic-accent templates
- Luxury-styled invitation templates
- Kids birthday invitation templates
- Fairy theme birthday invitation templates
- 25th birthday / milestone birthday invitation templates
- Wedding and debut invitation templates
- Elegant corporate and formal celebration templates
- Modern minimalist celebration templates

Each template must look intentional, polished, and market-ready. No template should appear generic, low-effort, overcrowded, or visually outdated.

---

## 3. Library Standard

The template library shall be organized as a curated showcase, not a cluttered asset dump.

Required qualities:

1. Every template must have a strong thumbnail.
2. The thumbnail must clearly communicate the event type and visual style.
3. The browsing flow must allow rapid visual scanning.
4. The user must not need to open many templates to understand the collection.
5. Template names must be short, clear, and searchable.
6. Templates must be arranged by category, style, and event type.
7. The collection must feel premium from the first screen.

---

## 4. Top 50 Template Strategy

The library shall contain a curated “Top 50” group. These must be the most visually competitive and commercially attractive designs in the system.

Selection rule:
- Prioritize designs that are highly usable, visually strong, and easy to customize.
- Prefer designs that look modern in current market conditions.
- Remove weak, repetitive, or visually dated templates.
- Retain only designs that would impress a client at first glance.

Recommended Top 50 distribution:
- 10 photo-based templates
- 10 animated/video templates
- 8 floral templates
- 8 gold foil / luxury templates
- 6 kids birthday templates
- 4 milestone birthday templates
- 2 debut / quinceañera templates
- 2 wedding templates

This distribution may be adjusted if the library demand changes, but the final output must still feel balanced and premium.

---

## 5. Thumbnail Navigation Standard

The template browser shall use thumbnail-first navigation.

Requirements:
- Thumbnails must be large enough to recognize at a glance.
- Thumbnails must be visually consistent in grid alignment.
- Template category labels must appear directly under each thumbnail.
- Hover or tap preview must show the key motion or visual highlight.
- Sorting must support occasion, style, color, and format.
- Search must support event type, theme, and keyword matching.
- Users must be able to move from thumbnail grid to full preview in one action.

The goal is fast browsing with minimal friction.

---

## 6. Music Background Standard

For animated invitation templates, the music layer must be treated as part of the template experience.

Requirements:
- Music must match the emotional tone of the template.
- Audio must be clear, balanced, and not overpower the visuals.
- Provide at least one music option per template.
- Include soft, elegant, celebratory, cinematic, or playful background music as appropriate.
- Avoid harsh, distorted, or distracting audio tracks.
- The default music should be emotionally aligned with the design style.

Example pairing:
- Fairy theme birthday: soft magical music
- Luxury gold foil: elegant cinematic music
- Kids birthday: bright playful music
- Wedding: refined romantic music
- Milestone birthday: classy celebratory music

---

## 7. Visual Style Rules

### Photo-Based Templates
- Use strong hero images.
- Keep text contrast high.
- Preserve subject clarity.
- Avoid clutter around the photograph.
- Use modern framing and composition.

### Animated Templates
- Motion must support the message, not distract from it.
- Animation timing must feel smooth and premium.
- Transitions should be clean and intentional.
- Use subtle effects where elegance is required.

### Floral Templates
- Use balanced floral framing.
- Keep floral decorations tasteful.
- Avoid overcrowding the central message area.
- Use modern botanical styling.

### Gold Foil / Luxury Templates
- Use metallic accents with restraint.
- Maintain premium contrast and spacing.
- Avoid cheap-looking glitter effects.
- Use refined typography and clean hierarchy.

### Kids Birthday Templates
- Use clear visual fun without visual chaos.
- Preserve readability.
- Use character/theme elements with discipline.
- Keep the design joyful, not noisy.

---

## 8. Template Naming Standard

Template names must follow a simple naming structure:

`[Event Type] | [Theme] | [Style Indicator]`

Examples:
- Kids Birthday Invitation | Fairy Theme Birthday Invitation
- Birthday Invitation Video | 25th Birthday | Customizable
- Wedding Invitation | Floral Gold Foil | Animated
- Debut Invitation | Luxury Rose Theme | Video Template

Names must be concise enough for browsing and search.

---

## 9. Template Quality Gate

Before a template is approved for the Top 50 collection, it must pass the following checks:

- Strong first impression
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Readable on mobile
- Easy to customize
- Good thumbnail appearance
- Appropriate background music
- Style consistency
- No outdated design language
- No weak spacing or awkward composition
- Event message is immediately recognizable

Any template failing these checks shall be removed or redesigned.

---

## 10. Preferred Template Experience

The ideal user experience is:

1. Open template library.
2. See clean category groups.
3. Scan premium thumbnails quickly.
4. Identify the event style immediately.
5. Preview motion and music.
6. Customize fast.
7. Export without confusion.

The system should feel like a curated design gallery, not a raw folder of assets.

---

## 11. Implementation Notes

The template update shall be done in phases:

Phase 1: Audit all existing templates  
Phase 2: Remove outdated or weak designs  
Phase 3: Rebuild visual hierarchy and thumbnails  
Phase 4: Upgrade music and animation behavior  
Phase 5: Reorganize into top 50 showcase format  
Phase 6: Validate mobile and desktop browsing  
Phase 7: Final quality review and publish

---

## 12. Final Output Requirement

The final template library must be:
- visually current,
- easy to navigate,
- premium in presentation,
- clear in thumbnail selection,
- and strong enough to impress users immediately.

The result should feel like a world-class invitation design catalog.
