# Engineering Operations Center (EOC)

# PART 09
# Engineering Workforce Intelligence Center (Manpower Management)

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

The Engineering Workforce Intelligence Center manages engineering workforce capability, workload, competency, productivity, and performance.

The module does not replace HR attendance systems.

Its purpose is to ensure that engineering resources are effectively deployed to support hotel operations.

The focus is engineering capability rather than payroll.

---

# 2. Objectives

The module shall

Monitor daily manpower.

Track technician competency.

Balance workloads.

Measure productivity.

Measure work quality.

Identify training needs.

Track certifications.

Support workforce planning.

Generate engineering KPIs.

Generate executive summaries.

# 24. Acceptance Criteria

The module shall be considered complete when

Every technician has a competency profile.

Workload is measured using engineering complexity.

Performance reflects productivity and quality.

Training requirements are automatically identified.

Management can make staffing decisions using objective engineering data.

The module becomes the workforce intelligence platform for the Engineering Department.
