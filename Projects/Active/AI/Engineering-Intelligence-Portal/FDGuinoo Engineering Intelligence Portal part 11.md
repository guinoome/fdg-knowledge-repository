# Engineering Operations Center (EOC)

# PART 11
# Engineering Knowledge & Reports Repository

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

The Engineering Knowledge & Reports Repository serves as the centralized document intelligence platform for the Engineering Operations Center.

Its purpose is to automatically collect, classify, index, relate, summarize, and preserve all engineering documents throughout their lifecycle.

The repository is not a file storage system.

It is an engineering knowledge base.

Every document shall contribute operational knowledge.

---

# 2. Objectives

The repository shall

Automatically collect reports.

Classify documents.

Extract engineering information.

Create searchable knowledge.

Generate AI summaries.

Link documents to operations.

Preserve engineering history.

Support investigations.

Support audits.

Support management reporting.

The repository shall be considered complete when

Historical knowledge is preserved.

The Chief Engineer can retrieve any engineering information from a single search.

The repository becomes the engineering knowledge center for the hotel.
