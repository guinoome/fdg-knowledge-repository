# Engineering Operations Center (EOC)

# PART 10
# Contractor & Project Control Center

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

The Contractor & Project Control Center manages all engineering contractors, renovation projects, warranty work, annual maintenance contracts (AMC), and capital improvement projects.

The module provides complete visibility of project progress, contractual obligations, engineering coordination, safety compliance, procurement dependencies, and operational impact.

Every contractor engagement shall have one complete operational record.

---

# 2. Objectives

The module shall

Manage contractors.

Monitor project progress.

Track milestones.

Track delays.

Track permits.

Track engineering coordination.

Track inspections.

Track punch lists.

Track warranty.

Track payments.

Track operational impacts.

Generate executive summaries.

# 25. Acceptance Criteria

The module shall be considered complete when

Every contractor engagement is fully traceable.

Every project displays operational and financial status.

Engineering can immediately identify project risks.

Management can understand contractor performance without separate reports.

The module becomes the engineering project control center for all contractor activities.
