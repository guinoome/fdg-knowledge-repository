# Appendix A
# Engineering Tracking Catalog

## Purpose

The FDGuinoo Engineering Intelligence Portal shall serve as the single source of truth for all engineering operational information. Every tracked item shall have a defined source, owner, update frequency, and attention level.

---

The system shall track:

- Occupancy (%)
- VIP Arrivals
- VIP In-House Guests
- Long Staying Guests
- Early Arrivals
- Late Check-Outs
- Total Available Rooms
- Occupied Rooms
- Vacant Rooms
- Out of Order (OOO) Rooms
- Out of Service (OOS) Rooms
- Rooms Returned Today
- Rooms Waiting for Housekeeping
- Rooms Waiting for Front Office Release
- Revenue Loss per OOO Room
- Average Room Recovery Time
- Repeat Room Failures
- Rooms Under Monitoring
