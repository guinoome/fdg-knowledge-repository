# DOCUMENT PURPOSE

This document defines the complete breakdown of the Engineering Intelligence Portal (EIP) specification.

The portal is not intended to be a simple dashboard or spreadsheet replacement. It is designed to become a personal Engineering Intelligence System that continuously collects, consolidates, validates, prioritizes, and summarizes engineering operational data into a single decision-making platform.

The portal shall minimize manual work and enable the Chief Engineer to prepare for daily operations in less than five minutes.

---

# PROJECT PHILOSOPHY

## Current Workflow

Engineering currently requires opening multiple systems every morning:

- Microsoft Outlook
- Engineering Shared Folder
- FCS
- SCM
- OOO Tracker
- Inventory
- PM Reports
- Viber Engineering GC
- Contractor Reports

The Chief Engineer manually consolidates information before meetings.

Problems include:

- Duplicate information
- Missed follow-ups
- No centralized tracking
- Difficult prioritization
- Time-consuming report preparation
- High dependency on memory

---

## Future Workflow

The Engineering Intelligence Portal continuously synchronizes engineering information throughout the morning.

At exactly **07:45 AM**, the system automatically generates the official Daily Engineering Snapshot while continuing to update the live operational dashboard.

The Chief Engineer only reviews priorities instead of collecting information.
