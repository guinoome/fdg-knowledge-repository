The “Pattern Interrupt” Hook
“I am creating a YouTube Short about [Your Topic]. Generate 5 powerful 3 second opening hooks that instantly break the viewer’s attention pattern and force them to stop scrolling.
Each hook must: • Be extremely unexpected, bold, or slightly controversial
• Avoid overused phrases like “Did you know” or “Have you ever”
• Start in a way that feels like the viewer joined mid conversation or mid action
• Create instant curiosity or confusion that demands continuation
• Be short, punchy, and natural for spoken delivery in Shorts
Focus on hooks that feel like interruptions in reality, not introductions to content.”

The “Retain & Reward” Script
“Write a 50 second YouTube Shorts script about [Topic].
Structure it using the Gap Theory of curiosity:
• First 5 seconds: Open with a burning question that creates a strong knowledge gap and makes viewers feel they need the answer immediately
• Middle section: Deliver exactly 3 fast, high value points. Each point should be sharp, practical, and easy to understand. Keep pacing intense and remove all filler
• Final 5 seconds: Reveal the main answer, insight, or conclusion that resolves the curiosity gap created at the start
Rules: • Keep every sentence under 10 words
• Maintain high energy and fast rhythm
• No long explanations or storytelling fluff
• Every line must push retention forward
• Make the viewer feel they must watch until the end to understand the full idea”

 The “Contrarian Take”
“Identify the most widely accepted piece of advice in the [Your Niche] industry.
Now create a YouTube Shorts script titled:
‘Why [Common Advice] Is Actually Ruining Your Progress’
Requirements:
• Start by clearly stating the common advice everyone follows
• Immediately challenge it with a bold contrarian statement
• Explain why it seems right but fails in real execution
• Use simple, logical reasoning instead of emotional claims
• Provide one clear alternative approach that works better
• End with a provocative line that invites disagreement, debate, or comments
Tone: • Confident, direct, slightly provocative
• Not emotional or clickbait
• Back every claim with logic or practical reasoning
Goal: Spark discussion, disagreement, and high engagement while still sounding credible and intelligent.”


4️⃣ The “Infinite Loop” Closer
“Script a 45 second YouTube Short about [Topic].
The script must be designed for maximum rewatchability using an infinite loop structure.
Requirements:
• Open with a strong hook that introduces [Topic] in a curious or unresolved way
• Build the middle with clear, fast paced value delivery (no filler, no slow explanations)
• Keep the tone simple, direct, and highly engaging for short form video
• Focus on maintaining curiosity and momentum throughout
Critical rule: • The final sentence must directly connect back to the opening hook
• It should feel like the video never truly ‘ends’
• The ending must naturally lead the viewer to restart the video without realizing it
Goal: Create a seamless loop effect that increases watch time, replays, and algorithm performance by making the ending and beginning feel like one continuous cycle.”


5️⃣ The “Visual Storyboarder”
“Take this YouTube Shorts script: [Paste Script]
Break it down into precise 3 second intervals.
For each interval, create a high retention visual plan designed to maximize watch time and stop viewers from scrolling.
For every 3 second segment, include:
• On screen visual (B roll, stock footage, screen recording, or meme style visual)
• Text overlay (short, bold, attention grabbing words)
• Editing direction (zoom, cut, speed change, glitch, shake, etc.)
• Attention goal (what emotion or curiosity this moment triggers)
Rules: • No generic visuals
• Every scene must feel dynamic and fast paced
• Prioritize pattern interrupts and visual change every few seconds
• Avoid repetition in visuals or pacing
• Design for mobile first viewing and silent autoplay
Goal: Turn a simple script into a high energy visual experience that forces retention and prevents swiping.”

6️⃣ The “Viral Title & Thumbnail” Duo
“I have a YouTube video about [Topic].
Generate 10 high performing title ideas and matching thumbnail concepts designed for maximum CTR and curiosity.
For each idea:
• Title must be under 50 characters
• Use Negative Constraints or pattern interrupt framing such as:
‘Stop doing X’
‘The truth about Y’
‘You are wasting time on X’
‘Nobody tells you this about Y’
• Titles must feel bold, controversial, or revealing without being misleading
For each title, also include:
• A high contrast thumbnail concept
• No words or text on the thumbnail at all
• Strong visual storytelling using facial expressions, symbols, arrows, contrast, or shocking comparison
• Clear emotional trigger (shock, confusion, curiosity, urgency)
• Simple composition that is instantly understandable in 1 second
Goal: Maximize click through rate by combining curiosity driven titles with visually powerful, wordless thumbnails that communicate instantly.”
