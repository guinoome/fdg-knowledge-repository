## 3. Engineering Infographic / Carousel (Canva) — 7 Slides

**Title:** "Net-Billing vs Net-Metering: What's the Real Difference?"

| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| 1. Cover | "Which One Actually Saves You More Money?" / subtitle: "An FDG Solar Visayas Engineering Explainer" |
| 2. Net Metering | Plain-language definition: excess solar power is exported to the grid and credited, typically at a rate lower than your retail purchase rate. |
| 3. Net Billing / Self-Consumption | Plain-language definition: system is sized so your home uses most of the solar power it generates in real time, offsetting electricity you'd otherwise buy at full retail rate. |
| 4. The Engineering Reason | Because exported energy is credited below your buying rate, power you consume directly is worth more than power you export. This is arithmetic, not opinion. |
| 5. What This Means for Sizing | We size systems around your actual daytime load profile, not around maximum panel count. Bigger isn't automatically better. |
| 6. Trust Detail | Every string we design stays within manufacturer voltage limits (typically 450V DC max) with dedicated DC breakers and surge protection per string. Standard engineering practice, not an upsell. |
| 7. CTA | "Get a Free Engineering-Based System Assessment" + full contact block |
