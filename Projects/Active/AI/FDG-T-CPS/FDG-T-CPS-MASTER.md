# ENGINEERING REQUIREMENTS

Every engineering calculation shall:

* Display the governing equation.
* Show variable definitions.
* Show units.
* Show substituted values.
* Show intermediate calculations.
* Show the final result.
* Display engineering remarks.
* Include validation status.
* Reference the applicable formula ID.

No hidden calculations.

---

# VALIDATION REQUIREMENTS

Implement a rule-based validation engine.

The engine shall evaluate:

* Hydraulic calculations
* Pump selection
* Pressure limits
* Velocity limits
* Pipe sizing
* Tank sizing
* Pressure vessel sizing
* Equipment completeness
* Report completeness

Warnings shall never silently modify user inputs.

---

# RECOMMENDATION ENGINE

Provide transparent engineering recommendations.

Every recommendation shall include:

* Engineering basis
* Trigger condition
* Expected benefit
* Possible trade-offs
* Suggested action

The engineer shall always remain responsible for accepting or rejecting recommendations.
