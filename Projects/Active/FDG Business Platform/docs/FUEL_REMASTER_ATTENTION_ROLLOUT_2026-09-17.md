## Protected behavior and corrections

New positive deductions require a dated calibration register record. Unchanged deductions on legacy closeout corrections remain compatible; missing historical evidence is not fabricated. Unconfirmed/unreturned tests block posting. Preserve FIFO, monthly expenses, owner-only capacity, consecutive dates, approval before posting, revisions, failed-save rollback and cache isolation.

Utility intervals are inclusive; overlapping active approvals are rejected. Correct an approved record by proposing the same utility/start date and selecting the original. Approval supersedes it without deleting original dates, values, source or review. To end an open-ended rate, approve its corrected end date before approving the next nonoverlapping interval. No automatic expense changes occur.

Review states describe the local review, not verified source repair. Evidence references are user-entered; workbook repair and revalidation must precede eligibility changes. No sample utility values were seeded.
