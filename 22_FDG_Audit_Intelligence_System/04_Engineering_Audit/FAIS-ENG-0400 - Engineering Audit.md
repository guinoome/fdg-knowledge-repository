# Engineering Audit

## Purpose

Provide independent assurance of engineering deliverables without replacing engineering authority.

## Audit Areas

Design basis, requirements, assumptions, calculations, units, references, code/standard citations, equipment data, drawings, specifications, interfaces, constructability, maintainability, testing, commissioning, safety, lifecycle considerations, revisions, and approvals.

## Evidence-Driven Engineering

Engineering conclusions must be traceable to inputs, assumptions, methods, evidence, and applicable requirements. Calculation checking should be reproducible.

## Boundary

FAIS identifies assurance gaps; FEIS or the responsible engineer owns engineering content and corrective design.
