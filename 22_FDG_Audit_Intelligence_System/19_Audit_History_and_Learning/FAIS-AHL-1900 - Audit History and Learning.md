# Audit History and Learning

## Purpose

Turn completed audits into organizational learning without erasing historical decisions or findings.

## Learning Capture

Capture recurring findings, root-cause patterns, effective controls, ineffective controls, successful corrective actions, false positives, audit-method improvements, and emerging risks.

## Trend Analysis

Compare periods only when definitions and data are sufficiently consistent. Record methodology changes that affect comparability.

## Institutional Memory

Retain superseded conclusions and closure history according to governance rather than rewriting old records to match the present state.
