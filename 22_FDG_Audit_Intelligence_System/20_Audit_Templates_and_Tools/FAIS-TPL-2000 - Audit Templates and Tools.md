# Audit Templates and Tools

## Purpose

Define the minimum reusable templates required for repeatable FAIS operation before software automation.

## Required Templates

Audit Plan; Audit Program/Checklist; Evidence Register; Working Paper; Finding Record; CAPA Record; Verification Record; Audit Report; Audit Baseline; Lessons Learned.

## Tool Philosophy

Start with portable Markdown/CSV or similarly stable formats. Add local scripts, databases, dashboards, and integrations only after validated workflow need.

## Template Control

Templates may evolve, but completed records should preserve the template/version context used at the time.

This document is part of the initial FAIS baseline. It may be expanded through controlled future milestones. Recommendations do not become FDG policy until approved through the applicable governance authority.
