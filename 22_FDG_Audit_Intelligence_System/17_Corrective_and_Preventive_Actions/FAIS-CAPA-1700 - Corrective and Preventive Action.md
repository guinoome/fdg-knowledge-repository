# Corrective and Preventive Actions

## Purpose

Control the response to findings from containment through root cause, correction, corrective action, prevention, and effectiveness verification.

## CAPA Flow

Contain where necessary → determine cause → define correction → define corrective action → assign owner/date → implement → collect evidence → verify effectiveness → close or reopen.

## Root Cause Discipline

Avoid defaulting to 'human error' when process design, workload, training, tools, governance, communication, or control design contributed.

## Ownership

The audited domain owns remediation unless governance assigns otherwise. FAIS tracks and verifies rather than silently implementing changes.

## Control Note

This document is part of the initial FAIS baseline. It may be expanded through controlled future milestones. Recommendations do not become FDG policy until approved through the applicable governance authority.

## Maintenance Readiness Assurance Extension — 2026-10-09

FAIS may independently audit maintenance-readiness controls defined by:

- [[08_FEIS_Engineering_Intelligence_Systems/06_Maintenance_and_Reliability_Intelligence/FEIS-MNT-0001 - Maintenance Work Readiness Planning and Scheduling Standard|Maintenance Work Readiness, Planning & Scheduling]]
- [[08_FEIS_Engineering_Intelligence_Systems/06_Maintenance_and_Reliability_Intelligence/FEIS-MNT-0002 - Asset Maintenance Readiness and Project Handover Standard|Asset Maintenance Readiness & Project Handover]]

Potential control failures include:
- jobs marked Ready while mandatory constraints remain unresolved;
- readiness overrides without authority/evidence;
- repeated schedule break-ins caused by preventable planning defects;
- critical material shortages not converted into corrective action;
- failed post-maintenance tests followed by unsupported return-to-service;
- new assets transferred without required master data, PM setup, spares, documents or training.

Where systemic, verified failures may create CAPA rather than being treated as isolated work-order exceptions.
