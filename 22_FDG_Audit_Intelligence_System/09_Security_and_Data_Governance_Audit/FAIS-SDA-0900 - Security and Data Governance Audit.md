# Security and Data Governance Audit

## Purpose

Evaluate whether approved security, access, confidentiality, data-governance, backup, recovery, and information-handling controls operate as intended.

## Audit Areas

Access authorization, least privilege, classification, secrets handling, audit logs, backups, restore tests, retention, data movement, third-party dependencies, incident records, change controls, and privileged operations.

## Interface

FSIS owns security intelligence and security architecture; FAIS independently evaluates conformance and control effectiveness.

## Sensitive Evidence

Collect the minimum sensitive information required. Audit records must not become an unnecessary repository of credentials, secrets, or personal data.

This document is part of the initial FAIS baseline. It may be expanded through controlled future milestones. Recommendations do not become FDG policy until approved through the applicable governance authority.
