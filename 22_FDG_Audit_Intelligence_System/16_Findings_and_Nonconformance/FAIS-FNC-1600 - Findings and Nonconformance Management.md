# Findings and Nonconformance

## Purpose

Create a consistent lifecycle and classification model for audit findings.

## Finding Structure

Finding ID; audit; criterion; observed condition; evidence; analysis; impact/risk; classification; severity; owner; required response; due state; related actions; verification; closure.

## Classification

Use categories such as conformity, observation, opportunity for improvement, minor nonconformance, major nonconformance, critical control failure, or unresolved evidence gap. Final taxonomy should be governed and calibrated through use.

## Severity Principle

Severity reflects consequence, exposure, control weakness, recurrence, detectability, and scope—not the auditor's preference.

## No Silent Closure

A finding closes only when closure criteria are satisfied and verification evidence is recorded.
