# FAIS Archive Governance

## Purpose

Preserve superseded or inactive FAIS knowledge without confusing it with current authority.

## Archive Rule

Archive rather than delete when historical value, traceability, or authority history may matter.

## Metadata

Archived material should identify prior status, replacement/superseding reference when known, archival reason, and archival date.

## Authority

Presence in the archive does not make content current or authoritative.

This document is part of the initial FAIS baseline. It may be expanded through controlled future milestones. Recommendations do not become FDG policy until approved through the applicable governance authority.
