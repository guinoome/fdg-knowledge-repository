# AI Agent and Collaborator Audit

## Purpose

Audit human and machine collaborators for authority, scope, evidence use, output quality, traceability, security boundaries, and review requirements.

## Audit Areas

Identity/role, assigned authority, accessible context, task definition, model/tool used where relevant, evidence sources, assumptions, output verification, handoff, approval, errors, escalation, and lessons.

## Model Independence

Audit the work product and process rather than assuming capability from model branding. Model selection evidence may be reviewed where cost, risk, or capability matters.

## Self-Audit Limitation

An agent may perform first-pass checks on its own work, but high-risk assurance should use an independent reviewer, method, model, or human authority where practical.
