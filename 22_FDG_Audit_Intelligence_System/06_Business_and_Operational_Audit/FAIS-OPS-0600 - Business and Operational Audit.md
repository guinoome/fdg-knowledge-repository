# Business and Operational Audit

## Purpose

Evaluate operational controls, process reliability, service performance, asset/process records, and organizational execution.

## Audit Areas

SOP adherence, responsibility, handoffs, records, exceptions, approvals, service levels, asset controls, maintenance evidence, inventory/process integrity, business continuity, and recurring failure patterns.

## Operational Evidence

Prefer system records, work records, observations, controlled logs, approved procedures, and reconciled data over recollection alone.

## Improvement

Separate isolated error, systemic control weakness, process-design defect, training gap, resource constraint, and governance failure.
