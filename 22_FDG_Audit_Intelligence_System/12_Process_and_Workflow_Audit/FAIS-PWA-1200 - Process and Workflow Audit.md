# Process and Workflow Audit

## Purpose

Evaluate whether workflows are defined, repeatable, controlled, measurable, and aligned with intended outcomes.

## Audit Areas

Inputs, triggers, owners, sequence, handoffs, decisions, controls, outputs, evidence, exceptions, failure modes, cycle time, rework, and definition of done.

## Root Cause

Do not treat repeated symptoms as isolated failures when evidence indicates a workflow or organizational control problem.

## Automation Readiness

Automate only after the workflow is sufficiently stable, rules are understood, outputs are reviewable, and failure handling is defined.
