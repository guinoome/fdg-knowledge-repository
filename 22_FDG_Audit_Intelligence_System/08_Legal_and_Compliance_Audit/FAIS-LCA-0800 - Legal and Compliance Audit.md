# Legal and Compliance Audit

## Purpose

Evaluate evidence of compliance against identified approved legal, regulatory, contractual, and internal requirements.

## Interface with FLIS

FLIS should own legal intelligence and authoritative legal-source management. FAIS tests whether applicable controls and records demonstrate conformance.

## No Legal Fabrication

Do not infer an obligation merely because it appears prudent. Every compliance finding should identify its source or state that legal authority requires confirmation.

## Audit Areas

Permits, licenses, contractual obligations, required records, approval evidence, renewal controls, compliance registers, and unresolved legal authority.
