# Audit Governance

## Purpose

Define governance for audit authority, independence, planning, approval, escalation, confidentiality, records, and quality.

## Governance Controls

Maintain an audit universe, risk-informed priorities, authorized scope, assigned auditor, declared conflicts, review/approval authority, escalation path, and retention requirements.

## Three Responsibility Boundaries

**Domain owner:** owns the process/control. **FAIS:** independently evaluates it. **Governance authority:** accepts risk, approves policy, resolves authority disputes, and authorizes material change.

## Audit Interference

Auditors must not alter evidence to improve apparent conformance. Evidence preservation and audit trail integrity take priority.
