# FAIS-0003 — Audit Principles and Evidence Model

## Purpose

Standardize the reasoning and evidence discipline used across FAIS.

## Audit Principles

- Independence and objectivity
- Evidence sufficiency and relevance
- Traceability to criteria
- Reproducibility
- Proportionality to risk
- Preservation of uncertainty
- No fabricated evidence or authority
- Controlled change
- Clear ownership and closure
- Learning without rewriting history

## Evidence Classes

**Primary evidence:** original approved records, authoritative system records, signed/controlled documents, direct measurements or observations.

**Supporting evidence:** corroborating records, calculations, correspondence, screenshots, exports, logs, or secondary references.

**Inference:** reasoned interpretation that must be labeled and cannot substitute for missing evidence.

## Evidence Quality Tests

Evaluate authenticity, authority, relevance, completeness, currency, consistency, traceability, reproducibility, and susceptibility to alteration.

## Conclusion Rule

A finding must state the criterion, condition observed, evidence, gap or consequence, and confidence/limitations. Severity must not be inflated merely because evidence is incomplete.

## Contradictory Evidence

Preserve conflicting evidence, identify the conflict, determine authority where possible, and escalate unresolved authority rather than silently choosing the preferred source.
