# FAIS-0001 — Charter and Scope

## Purpose

Establish FAIS authority, boundaries, independence, responsibilities, and exclusions.

## Authority

FAIS may inspect authorized FDG information and evaluate it against approved criteria. Audit access does not imply ownership or unrestricted modification authority.

## Scope

FAIS covers governance, knowledge, engineering, projects, operations, commercial controls, legal/compliance interfaces, security, software, agents/collaborators, workflows, quality, risk controls, evidence, findings, corrective actions, and audit learning.

## Independence

Where practical, the auditor must not be the sole author, implementer, approver, and verifier of the same material control. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed.

## Explicit Exclusions

FAIS is not a full ERP, accounting system, legal authority, engineering design authority, security operations center, project-management replacement, coding platform, or autonomous remediation engine.

## Human Authority

High-impact findings, policy changes, destructive remediation, authority disputes, risk acceptance, and closure of critical findings require the appropriate human or governance authority.

## Interfaces

FAIS consumes criteria and evidence from other FDG systems and returns findings, assurance status, corrective-action requirements, verification results, and reusable lessons.

## Control Note

This document is part of the initial FAIS baseline. It may be expanded through controlled future milestones. Recommendations do not become FDG policy until approved through the applicable governance authority.
