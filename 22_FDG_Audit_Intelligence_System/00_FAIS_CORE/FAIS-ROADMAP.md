# FAIS — Roadmap

## Purpose

Define staged development without turning FAIS prematurely into a large software platform.

## Milestone 0 — Foundation

Freeze charter, architecture, terminology, audit record schema, evidence model, severity model, finding lifecycle, and cross-system interfaces.

## Milestone 1 — Manual Repeatable Audit

Validate repository, engineering, project, process, and collaborator audits using controlled Markdown templates and evidence registers.

## Milestone 2 — Standardized Assurance

Introduce reusable checklists, audit programs, sampling guidance, CAPA verification, recurring finding analytics, and management reporting.

## Milestone 3 — Assisted Intelligence

Add local tools for indexing, broken-link detection, duplicate detection, traceability checks, completeness checks, evidence inventory, and report assistance.

## Milestone 4 — Continuous Controls

Introduce selected recurring control tests where criteria are stable, measurable, low-risk, and reviewable.

## Milestone 5 — Integrated Assurance

Connect FAIS through replaceable interfaces to other FDG Intelligence Systems and operational data sources.

## Deferred Scope

Do not yet build a full GRC platform, accounting audit suite, autonomous enforcement system, full observability platform, or universal compliance engine. These require validated need and future approval.

## Control Note

This document is part of the initial FAIS baseline. It may be expanded through controlled future milestones. Recommendations do not become FDG policy until approved through the applicable governance authority.
