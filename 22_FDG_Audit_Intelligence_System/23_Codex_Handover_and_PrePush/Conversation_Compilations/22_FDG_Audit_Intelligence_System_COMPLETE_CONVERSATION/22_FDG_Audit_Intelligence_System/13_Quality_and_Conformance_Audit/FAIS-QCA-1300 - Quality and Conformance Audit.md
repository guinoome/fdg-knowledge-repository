# Quality and Conformance Audit

## Purpose

Provide a common framework for checking deliverables and controls against defined acceptance criteria.

## Conformance Test

Compare the actual condition to an approved requirement. Record conforming evidence, exceptions, missing evidence, and limitations.

## Quality Dimensions

Correctness, completeness, consistency, traceability, usability, maintainability, reliability, reproducibility, and fitness for intended purpose.

## Acceptance

A deliverable is not automatically acceptable because no defect was reported. Acceptance should be based on defined criteria and evidence.
