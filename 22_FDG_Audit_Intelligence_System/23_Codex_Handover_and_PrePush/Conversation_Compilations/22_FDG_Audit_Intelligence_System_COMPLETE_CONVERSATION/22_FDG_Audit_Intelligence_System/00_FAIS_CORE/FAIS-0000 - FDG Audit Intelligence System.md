# FAIS-0000 — FDG Audit Intelligence System

## Purpose

Define FAIS as the independent assurance, verification, and continuous audit intelligence layer of the FDG Ecosystem.

## Mission

FAIS determines whether FDG knowledge, systems, engineering outputs, projects, business processes, collaborators, controls, and evidence conform to approved requirements and remain complete, traceable, defensible, maintainable, and fit for purpose.

## Operating Position

FAIS audits domains; it does not replace or own them. Domain Intelligence Systems remain responsible for their subject matter. FAIS provides independent assurance across system boundaries.

## Primary Capabilities

- Repository and knowledge architecture audit
- Engineering and technical audit
- Project assurance
- Business and operational audit
- Financial/commercial control audit
- Legal/compliance assurance
- Security and data-governance audit
- Software/system audit
- AI-agent and collaborator audit
- Process, quality, risk, evidence, finding, CAPA, reporting, and learning intelligence.

## Canonical Audit Cycle

**DISCOVER → AUDIT → ANALYZE → REPORT → REMEDIATE BY AUTHORIZED OWNER → VERIFY → CLOSE → LEARN**.

FAIS preserves separation between finding and remediation. It may recommend or verify corrective action but must not silently alter the audited subject.

## Evidence Discipline

Every material conclusion should distinguish verified fact, repository evidence, inference, audit analysis, recommendation, and matters requiring human approval. Unknowns remain unknown until evidence resolves them.

## Design Principles

FAIS is local-first where practical, technology-independent, vendor-independent, evidence-driven, reviewable, reproducible, auditable, modular, and designed for long-term organizational capability.

## Success Condition

FAIS succeeds when FDG can explain what was audited, against which requirement, using what evidence, by whom or what, what was found, what action occurred, whether closure was verified, and what organizational learning was retained.
