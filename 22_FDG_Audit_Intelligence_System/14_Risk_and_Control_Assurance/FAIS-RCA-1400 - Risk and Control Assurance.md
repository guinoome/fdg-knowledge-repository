# Risk and Control Assurance

## Purpose

Link identified risks to controls and determine whether controls are designed, implemented, evidenced, and effective.

## Control Model

For each material risk identify preventive/detective/corrective controls, control owner, frequency or trigger, evidence, test method, exceptions, and residual risk.

## Effectiveness

Distinguish control existence, design adequacy, implementation, operating effectiveness, and sustained effectiveness.

## Risk Acceptance

FAIS may report residual risk but does not accept material organizational risk unless explicitly delegated that authority.
