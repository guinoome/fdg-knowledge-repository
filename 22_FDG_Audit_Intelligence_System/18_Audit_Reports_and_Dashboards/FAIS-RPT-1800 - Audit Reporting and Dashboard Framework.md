# Audit Reports and Dashboards

## Purpose

Standardize decision-useful audit communication while keeping detailed evidence available for reviewers.

## Report Structure

Objective and scope; criteria; methodology; limitations; executive conclusion; findings; severity; evidence references; systemic themes; actions/owners; unresolved matters; verification status.

## Dashboard Measures

Open findings by severity/age/system, overdue CAPA, repeat findings, closure cycle time, evidence gaps, control-test results, recurring root causes, and audit coverage.

## Metric Warning

Do not optimize teams to make audit metrics look good. Measures should expose control health, not create incentives to hide findings.
