# Financial and Commercial Audit

## Purpose

Provide control-oriented commercial assurance while preserving the authority of qualified accounting, finance, tax, and legal functions.

## Audit Areas

Approval trails, quotations, procurement comparisons, purchase records, commercial assumptions, budget/CAPEX/OPEX traceability, invoice/supporting records, segregation of duties, reconciliations, and contract-commercial interfaces.

## Boundary

FAIS does not certify financial statements or provide statutory audit opinions unless performed under the legally required qualified authority.

## Control Focus

Trace transactions and decisions to authorized evidence and identify missing, inconsistent, duplicated, unsupported, or anomalous records.

## Control Note

This document is part of the initial FAIS baseline. It may be expanded through controlled future milestones. Recommendations do not become FDG policy until approved through the applicable governance authority.
