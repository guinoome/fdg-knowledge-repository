# FBIS System Architecture

## Commercial Lifecycle

Customer
→ Product/Service
→ Pricing
→ Order
→ Voucher/Discount
→ Payment
→ Validation
→ Fulfillment
→ Receipt
→ Refund/Cancellation
→ Settlement
→ Reconciliation
→ Revenue Intelligence

## Platform Pattern

FDG Platform
→ FBIS Commercial Interface
→ Reusable Business/Payment Service
→ External Provider

Platforms must not become the authority for duplicated commercial rules.

## FBIS Owns
- commercial rules
- pricing intelligence
- order intelligence
- sales intelligence
- payment intelligence
- voucher/discount intelligence
- refund intelligence
- reconciliation intelligence
- revenue intelligence
- commercial analytics
