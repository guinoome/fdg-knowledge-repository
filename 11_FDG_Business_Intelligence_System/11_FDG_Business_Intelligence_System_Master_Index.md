## One-System Rule

> **All folders above are domains of ONE FBIS. None is an independent intelligence system.**

The folder Master Indexes provide domain-level organization.

The individual Markdown documents contain the actual business intelligence.

## Document Connectivity Rule

Every FBIS Markdown document must be connected to:

1. This system-level Master Index.
2. Its own folder Master Index.

This creates a continuous Obsidian graph from the FDG Ecosystem to every FBIS document.

```text
FDG Ecosystem
      ↓
FBIS Master
      ↓
Domain Master
      ↓
Document
```

## Source-of-Truth Principle

FBIS is the authoritative business intelligence layer of the FDG Ecosystem.

Do not create duplicate business knowledge merely to make it accessible from another FBIS domain. Use Obsidian links to the authoritative document.

## Structural Integrity

- Exactly one FBIS system-level Master Index.
- Exactly one Master Index per FBIS folder.
- Every FBIS Markdown document belongs to one FBIS folder.
- Every FBIS Markdown document links to its folder Master Index.
- Every FBIS Markdown document links to this system-level Master Index.
- Every folder Master Index links to this system-level Master Index.
- The FDG Ecosystem Master Index links to this system-level Master Index.

## Maintenance

When adding a new document:

1. Place it in the correct FBIS domain.
2. Add it to the domain Master Index.
3. Add the standard FBIS System and FBIS Domain links to the document.
4. Add cross-domain links only when the relationship is substantively useful.

> **One FBIS. One system-level Master Index. One Master Index per domain. Every document connected.**
