# FBIS Payment Validation Standard

1. Client-side claims are not authoritative.
2. Provider events are authenticated where supported.
3. Order and transaction identifiers are matched.
4. Amount and currency are checked.
5. Duplicate events are idempotent.
6. Invalid or ambiguous events become exceptions.
7. Fulfillment occurs only after controlled validation.
8. Financially material events are auditable.
9. Credentials remain outside the Knowledge Repository.
10. Provider-specific implementation remains behind abstraction.
