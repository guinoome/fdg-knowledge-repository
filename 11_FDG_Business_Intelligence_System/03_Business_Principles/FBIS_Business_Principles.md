# FBIS Business Principles

1. Business rules before automation.
2. Evidence before conclusions.
3. One source of truth per commercial concept.
4. Provider independence.
5. Auditability for financially material events.
6. Deterministic pricing and voucher rules.
7. Historical transaction integrity.
8. Visible exceptions.
9. Minimum necessary data.
10. Reusable capability over duplicated platform logic.
