# Payment Intelligence

Payment Intelligence governs the business intelligence surrounding payment transactions.

Capabilities:
- provider abstraction
- payment method catalog
- transaction lifecycle
- payment attempts
- server-side validation
- webhook/event validation
- duplicate detection
- failed payment analysis
- payment success analysis
- refunds
- settlement
- reconciliation
- provider performance

Critical rule:
A client-side success page is not authoritative evidence of payment completion.
