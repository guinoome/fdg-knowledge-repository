# FBIS Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Architecture
Canonical domain structure, commercial boundary, entity model, KPI dictionary, governance.

## Phase 1 — Commercial Core
Products/services, pricing, orders, voucher engine, discount engine, executive dashboard.

## Phase 2 — Payments
Provider abstraction, PayMongo adapter, checkout, verified webhooks, idempotency, payment states, receipts.

## Phase 3 — Financial Operations
Refunds, settlement tracking, reconciliation, exception dashboard.

## Phase 4 — Intelligence
Revenue analytics, customer commercial intelligence, campaign intelligence, voucher ROI, provider performance.

## Phase 5 — Continuous Learning
Anomaly detection, rule effectiveness, pricing intelligence, provider optimization, knowledge capture.
