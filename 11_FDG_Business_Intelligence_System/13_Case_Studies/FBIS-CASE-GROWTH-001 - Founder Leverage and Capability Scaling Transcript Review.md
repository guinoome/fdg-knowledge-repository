The strongest transferable proposition is **skills → market evidence → working workflow → reproducible service → delegated outcome/decision → organization-owned adaptive capability**. This is a hypothesis about scalable organizational design, not guaranteed revenue growth.

## 3. Source details worth preserving, and corrections

1. **Value-skill test:** What are you good at, what have you done, what do you enjoy? Supplement with direct buyer willingness to pay, problem cost, legal/professional competence, available capacity, and evidence from authentic job postings/actual buyer requests. Job ads indicate hiring demand, not necessarily demand for FDG products.
2. **Practice with demonstrable outputs:** A 90-day “learn and do” cycle can be split into bounded projects. FDG should first exploit existing PME/engineering and operations competence; learn new skills only where delivery reveals a capability gap. A public portfolio must use original, sanitized or authorized materials.
3. **Audit → Transfer → Fill:** Record baseline activity, delegate narrow safe tasks and refill capacity with sales, engineering review, customer research, service packaging and organizational design. Never claim savings equal transferred hours before counting oversight/defects/expense.
4. **Record tasks to teach them:** A consented walkthrough can become a versioned work package, checklist, good/bad example, role/authority, exception triggers, quality criteria and controlled procedure. A recording alone is **unreviewed raw working data**, not automatically a standard or safe machine-readable skill.
5. **Shared “second brain”:** Reuse [[10_FDG_CORE_Intelligence/FDG-CORE-STD-007_MEMORY_AND_CONTEXT_ENGINE_STANDARD|FDG-CORE-STD-007]], [[04_Knowledge_Management/KNOWLEDGE_CAPTURE_STANDARD|Knowledge Capture Standard]], and existing GitHub/Obsidian repository. Enforce access **before retrieval** at source/identity/index boundaries; test document-level authorization, isolation, audit, consent, retention and deletion. A prompt saying “sanitize sensitive things” is **not** an access-control mechanism. Public GitHub must not receive client, personnel, security, contracts, financial or unconsented meeting materials.
6. **Decision ownership:** Reuse [[06_Organizational_Architecture/DECISION_AUTHORITY_STANDARD|NEX-STD-040]] and [[06_Organizational_Architecture/DELEGATION_STANDARD|NEX-STD-043]]; assign reversible routine decisions with predefined thresholds and escalation, while qualified humans retain engineering/legal/financial/release authority. “Never take the work back” is not absolute: suspend delegation on safety, security, compliance, quality or authority failures; correct the process and retest.
7. **The 10–80–10 expression:** Speaker uses “108010” without a fully specified tested delegation contract. Do not codify numerical percentages as FDG standard. Instead preserve role split: founder provides objective/constraints, operators execute within authority, independent reviewer accepts output where required.
8. **Hackathon:** Teams of 2–3, a real work problem, short protected development window, demonstrations and peer review are useful. For FDG this is sandbox/local/offline only unless FPJIS release approvals exist. The metric is verified quality-adjusted time recovered and reusability, not number of scripts/apps generated.
9. **Empowerment:** Evolving staff/agents should reduce founder bottleneck while maintaining resilience when any individual/model/vendor is absent. Do not equate eliminating work with removing necessary safety checks or human professional sign-off.
10. **Client-side commercialization:** Internal automation savings do not themselves prove an externally saleable product. External offers need independent buyer pain, transparent scope, willing payer, an approved price, payment, delivery evidence and economic repeatability.

## 6. Three FDG horizons

**Now (0–30 days):** qualify one customer problem, issue a controlled quote after approval, manually deliver one reviewed pilot where feasible, capture task effort, economics, knowledge, results, review gates, and actual customer willingness to pay. No new SaaS platform required.

**Next (31–90 days):** turn a demonstrated manual workflow into a versioned operator pack with exception handling, reviewer handoff and small non-production automation tests. Delegate one low-risk task **only if authority is granted and acceptance passed**; measure net founder time recovered and service quality. Skill sprint focuses the missing commercial or delivery competency; not generic prompt proficiency.

**Redesigned constraints (later):** customer contracts, knowledge, verification evidence, reusable workflows and decision-authority maps stay independent of collaborator/model/provider. Productize only repeated paid workflows with stable outcomes, support economics, tenant-safe security, predictable release/rollback and organizational handover.

## 7. Portable evidence and acceptance criteria

- **LC-01:** Never equate personal income with business revenue, bookings, gross receipts, margin or profit; disclose dates, currency and accounting definition.
- **LC-02:** Validate skill → paid outcome through actual external customer evidence, not speaker anecdotes, portfolio screenshots or enthusiastic reactions.
- **LC-03:** Verify founder time baseline, transfer cost, oversight, rework and residual decision interruptions before asserting “time bought back.”
- **LC-04:** Every delegated task has work-package owner, data classification, expected output, accepted example, decision limit, escalation and acceptance evidence.
- **LC-05:** Context retrieval must enforce permissions independently of generation-time redaction and include adversarial access tests (wrong employee, wrong client, revoked permissions, sensitive meeting notes).
- **LC-06:** Engineering conclusions, signing/sealing, money movement, publication and production deployment retain independent human/organizational approvals; no implied autonomy.
- **LC-07:** Internal hackathon demos use authorized synthetic/sanitized fixtures, documented rollback and FPJIS quality gates before any operational deployment.
- **LC-08:** Commercial signals, client acceptance and recurring paid renewals remain separate from usage, marketing engagement and internal productivity claims.
- **LC-09:** The source case cannot be promoted to an approved engineering/business standard without independent validation and appropriate founder review.
- **LC-10:** Preserve vendor-neutral, local-first, offline-capable, agent-neutral, provider-replaceable knowledge/operating assets.
