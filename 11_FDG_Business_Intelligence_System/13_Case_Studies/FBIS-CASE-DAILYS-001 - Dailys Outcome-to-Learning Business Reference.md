## Independently visible commercial characteristics

The website at https://dailys.ph/ (reviewed 2026-10-09) advertises a brief assessment, Philippine-licensed doctor consultation and a prescription hair-regrowth plan delivered to the customer, with marketed Starter and Pro monthly prices of PHP 2,499 and PHP 2,997 respectively. It explicitly identifies clinical review by licensed professionals. Its refund-policy page, https://dailys.ph/pages/refund-policy, describes cancellations, refunds and a 90-day satisfaction guarantee, subject to stated conditions. These are **website representations**, not verified fulfillment records or validated clinical outcomes. Prices and policies may change.

A particularly useful boundary: automate commercial intake and coordination while leaving regulated professional approval with the competent human. Adapt that principle to FDG engineering review, signed technical deliverables, critical operational decisions, and legal/compliance obligations.

## Transferable business mechanisms

1. **Outcome-first positioning**: make one customer result legible before explaining software or assistants.
2. **Guided qualification**: a short, progressive assessment reduces ambiguity and routes the client toward an appropriate *proposed* next step.
3. **Approved offer matching**: map needs to supported services and exclusions; assess professional/legal requirements before commitments.
4. **Payment-to-order continuity**: signed provider confirmation, reconciliation, auditable state transitions and exceptions; not browser redirects.
5. **Single operating view**: real records for opportunities, orders, financial exceptions, fulfillment, support and retention, permissioned by tenant/branch.
6. **Specialist advisory agents**: finance, growth and customer support produce scoped recommendations/drafts, not self-issued executive authority.
7. **Measured feedback loop**: capture observations, trace sources, propose improvements, authorize changes, compare cohort performance, preserve failures.
8. **Recurring value**: subscription or recurring service can compound retention if its delivered outcome is measured.
9. **Operator leverage**: systematically reduce founder interventions while maintaining accountable control and customer-quality gates.

## What not to copy or assume

- Do not translate the video's self-reported 905k into a profit, valuation, ROI, product-market-fit or AI-causation claim.
- Do not create three ungoverned AI departments or grant them treasury, pricing, advertising or customer-data permissions.
- Do not claim the payment gateway, dashboard and agents were technically demonstrated or independently tested.
- Do not copy a medical assessment/prescribing flow for engineering without appropriate professional validation.
- Do not manufacture reviews, testimonials, founder-time metrics, campaign performance, dashboard data or conversion benchmarks.
- Do not make the user experience dependent on a named AI model, automation vendor, website builder or payment provider.
- Do not launch production subscriptions if customer entitlement and operational records are still demo-only.

## Source-backed opportunity assessment

- **High potential, low duplication:** couple the existing service catalog and FBIS sales funnel to a short approved-quote journey.
- **High potential, high control sensitivity:** finance recommendations from actual invoicing, settlement, fulfillment cost and contract records.
- **High potential, measurable:** marketing lead-to-approved-quote conversion and customer-support resolution vs case load.
- **Low evidence, defer:** unrestricted executive agents, predictive profit claims, automatic price/policy changes and unsupervised customer promises.
