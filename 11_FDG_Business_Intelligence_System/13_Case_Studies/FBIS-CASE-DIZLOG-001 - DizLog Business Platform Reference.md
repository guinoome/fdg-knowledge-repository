# FBIS-CASE-DIZLOG-001 --- DizLog Business Platform Reference

**Classification:** External reference / case study\
**Status:** Research evidence; not an FDG standard

## Purpose

Preserve the DizLog analysis as reusable FBIS competitive and
architectural intelligence.

## Reference

DizLog demonstrates an SMB-oriented integrated business platform
spanning POS/sales, inventory, purchasing, customers, employees,
accounting, projects, online commerce, and reporting. The principal
architectural lesson is not the screen design; it is the use of shared
business transactions to feed a common operational and financial
information layer.

## FDG Extraction

### ADOPT

-   common customer and supplier masters
-   product/service catalog
-   inventory and stock movement
-   sales and purchasing transactions
-   invoicing and payments
-   accounting linkage
-   multi-location concepts
-   projects and reporting

### ADAPT

-   online store and B2B capabilities
-   HR/payroll according to the applicable business and Philippine
    requirements
-   vertical business templates

### ENHANCE

-   business intelligence
-   operational intelligence
-   engineering/asset intelligence
-   evidence and assumption tracking
-   decision support
-   reusable cross-project data architecture

### DO NOT COPY

Do not create a screen-by-screen DizLog clone. Treat DizLog as evidence
for common business primitives and integrated workflows.

## Architectural Lesson

`Business activity → transactions → business data → financial meaning → analytics → decisions`
