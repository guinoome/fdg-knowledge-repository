# FDG First-Payout Commercial Validation Portfolio

> **Knowledge path:** [[11_FDG_Business_Intelligence_System/11_Business_Frameworks/11_Business_Frameworks_Master_Index|Business Frameworks]] → this portfolio.

## 1. Founder direction

For this experiment, freeze new major software/platform development unless a paid pilot exposes a workflow limitation that cannot reasonably be handled using existing FDG capability.

Commercial sequence:

```text
Existing FDG Knowledge
→ Specific Pain
→ Specific Offer
→ Paid Pilot
→ Structured Delivery Evidence
→ Measured Economics
→ Repeatable Service / Product
→ Standardization
→ Selective Automation
→ Software / Subscription only when justified
```

The immediate objective is not maximum theoretical revenue. It is a credible first paid transaction followed by repeatability evidence.

## 4. Two validation lanes

### Lane A - Assisted services

Use direct outreach to businesses with visible operational pain.

Initial sequence:
1. Maintenance Evidence + Monthly Engineering Reporting.
2. Construction Progress / Billing Evidence.
3. QTO / BOQ support.
4. Workflow / SOP / Reporting automation.
5. Specialist engineering services after scope, authority and liability checks.

### Lane B - Digital professional capability packs

Use existing governed knowledge to create products without building a new application.

Initial sequence:
1. FDG Construction Management Professional Toolkit.
2. Maintenance / Facility Management Toolkit.
3. QTO / BOQ Estimator Pack.
4. Testing & Commissioning Evidence Pack.
5. SOP / Operations System Pack.
6. Solar Pre-Sales / Quotation Pack.
7. Asset-specific PM packs.
8. Philippine business-operations vertical packs.

Each pack must be original FDG intellectual property or legally reusable content, with provenance retained.

## 5. Pricing experiment

### Flagship list price
**PHP 4,999** for a professional system/package or tightly scoped assisted pilot.

This price is not suitable for a generic single spreadsheet. A PHP 4,999 pack should provide a coherent professional capability: multiple connected templates, workflow guidance, examples, dashboards/calculations where appropriate, acceptance guidance, update/version information and clear scope.

### Founding Five promotion
For the first five paid customers of a launched flagship offer:

- List price: PHP 4,999
- Founding Five price: PHP 2,499.50
- Discount: 50%
- Quantity cap: exactly five successful paid orders for that launch campaign
- Promotion ends when the fifth qualifying payment is verified or when the campaign is explicitly closed.
- Historical list and promotional pricing must be preserved under FBIS pricing/version rules.

Do not use fake scarcity. The counter must be based on verified paid orders.

### Product ladder hypothesis

| Level | Indicative experiment | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Free sample | PHP 0 | Demonstrate quality and capture demand signal |
| Single practical tool | PHP 499-999 | Low-friction entry |
| Specialist mini-pack | PHP 1,499-2,499 | Role/task-specific value |
| Professional system pack | PHP 4,999 | Flagship digital product |
| Assisted pilot/service | From PHP 4,999 | Prove outcome and willingness to pay |
| Monthly managed service | From PHP 4,999/month subject to scope | Recurring revenue test |
| Custom implementation | Quote after discovery | Only after bounded requirements and margin review |

These are pilot hypotheses, not permanent price policy.

## 7. Differentiation rule

FDG shall not compete as a generic template seller.

Target differentiation:

```text
Profession-specific
+ Philippines-first where useful
+ Engineering semantics
+ Capture Once / Reuse Everywhere
+ Evidence and provenance
+ Connected lifecycle workflows
+ Worked examples
+ Management outputs
+ Versioned updates
+ Clear boundaries and professional-review gates
```

A template should increasingly behave like an executable knowledge object, consistent with [[08_FEIS_Engineering_Intelligence_Systems/03_Digital_Construction_Knowledge_Library/FEIS-DCKL-0000 - Digital Construction Knowledge Library Architecture|FEIS-DCKL-0000]].

## 10. Commercial validation measures

Per offer track:
- qualified prospects contacted
- conversations
- offers issued
- payments
- first-payment time
- conversion rate by source
- gross revenue
- delivery hours
- founder hours
- revenue per founder hour
- direct delivery cost
- revision/rework count
- refund/complaint rate
- repeat purchase / renewal
- next-service opportunity
- buyer role / segment
- most-used and unused components
- reason lost / reason bought

A transaction is proof of willingness to pay only for the tested scope, price, buyer and channel.

## 11. Stop / continue / scale rules

**Continue** when paid evidence exists and delivery can be completed acceptably.

**Improve** when customers pay but rework, founder hours or support burden are too high.

**Pause** when repeated outreach produces no qualified demand, or economics are structurally weak.

**Scale** only after repeatability, evidence quality, economics, and handover are demonstrated.

**Automate** only the repetitive validated bottlenecks.

## 12. Governance

This is a commercial experiment. It does not change legal entity status, tax/invoicing authority, professional sign-off authority, or existing approved engineering standards.

FAIS shall distinguish marketing claims from verified outcome evidence.

FMCIS collaborators may review or contribute only within assigned work packages; no silent rewrite of another collaborator's work.
