## Social Demand-Generation Extension — 2026-10-04

The canonical sales funnel remains:

Lead → Qualified → Opportunity → Quote → Order → Payment → Fulfillment → Completed.

For social-media and platform-launch campaigns, a pre-funnel demand-generation layer may feed the Lead stage:

~~~text
Social Content
→ CTA / Keyword / Link
→ Resource Delivery / Landing Page / Trial
→ Captured Lead
→ Marketing Qualification
→ Lead
→ Qualified
→ Opportunity
→ Quote / Subscription
→ Order / Payment
→ Fulfillment / Product Use
→ Completed / Retained
~~~

The social acquisition layer is defined in:

[[11_FDG_Business_Intelligence_System/11_Business_Frameworks/FBIS-BP-002_FDG_Social_Platform_Launch_Content_Engine_Blueprint|FDG Social Platform Launch & Content Engine Blueprint]]

Marketing engagement metrics do not redefine Sales Funnel stages. Marketing owns demand generation and MQL handoff; Commercial owns qualified opportunities and deal progression under [[06_Organizational_Architecture/NEX-STD-125_ENTERPRISE_FUNCTION_AND_DEPARTMENT_STANDARD|FDG Enterprise Function & Department Standard]].
