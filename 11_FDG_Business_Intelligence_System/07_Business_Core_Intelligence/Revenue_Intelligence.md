# Revenue Intelligence

Responsibilities: gross sales, discounts, refunds, applicable fees, net revenue, revenue by platform/product/channel, trends, forecasting, and campaign revenue impact.

Conceptual formula: Net Revenue = Gross Sales - Discounts - Refunds - Applicable Fees/Adjustments. Accounting treatment must be validated before formal financial reporting.
