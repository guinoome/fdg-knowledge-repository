# Business Intelligence Framework

FBIS transforms commercial data into decision-support intelligence.

1. Descriptive — what happened
2. Diagnostic — why it happened
3. Predictive — what is likely
4. Prescriptive — what should be done
5. Continuous Learning — what should become reusable knowledge

Every material metric identifies source, period, formula, freshness, quality, and owner.
