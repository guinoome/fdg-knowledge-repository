## 2026-10-19 — Qualified revenue and marketing efficiency standard

Marketing and sales agent reports must distinguish **impressions → legitimate inquiries → qualified leads → commercially accepted opportunities → quotes issued → genuine settled payments → completed/accepted deliverables → renewals**. Each metric has a named **period, eligible denominator, source, evidence timestamp and exclusions**; no inferred conversions, manipulated screenshots or fictitious pipeline.

- **Marketing** owns audience/source, compliant approved copy, inquiry/MQL and spend quality; **Commercial/Sales** owns accepted opportunities, discovery, quote and close; **Finance** owns actual collections, refunds and costs; **Engineering/Service** owns accepted product/service proof.
- Report **qualified conversion and attributable paid-customer acquisition cost**, contribution after acquisition, refund/churn, customer approval and average founder/reviewer effort **alongside** reach/followers. No vanity metric substitutes for sustainable sales.
- Distinguish scenario-based ROI from actual collected business return; unknown counts/denominators → **UNKNOWN/UNDEFINED** rather than zero or fabricated high performance.
- Audience research and reference harvesting are restricted to legitimate in-scope FDG sales use; never copy personal contact lists, customer engineering data or creator media into public reports.
- Periodic loss review must preserve "not interested", "missing functionality", "price", "security", "failed delivery" and "not yet qualified" as distinct cause codes; only evidence-linked product gaps move into FPJIS.
- Reuse [[11_FDG_Business_Intelligence_System/11_Business_Frameworks/FBIS-BP-003_FDG_First_Payout_Commercial_Validation_Portfolio|FBIS-BP-003 commercial strategy]] and [[11_FDG_Business_Intelligence_System/11_Business_Frameworks/FBIS-BP-002_FDG_Social_Platform_Launch_Content_Engine_Blueprint|approved claim handling]], not an additional analytics platform.
