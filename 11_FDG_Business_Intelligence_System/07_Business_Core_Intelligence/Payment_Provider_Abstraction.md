# Payment Provider Abstraction

Provider-neutral capabilities: Create Payment, Get Payment Status, Validate Payment Event, Refund Payment, Get Settlement, Get Provider Health.

Provider-specific APIs, credentials, payloads, SDKs, and webhook formats remain behind adapters. No provider becomes a permanent architectural dependency.
