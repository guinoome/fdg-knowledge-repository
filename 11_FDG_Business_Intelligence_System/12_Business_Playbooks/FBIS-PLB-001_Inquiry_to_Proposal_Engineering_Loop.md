# FBIS-PLB-001 — Inquiry-to-Proposal Engineering Loop

**Status:** Draft  
**Source Authority:** Tier E — Exploratory Knowledge

Operating loop: DISCOVER → UNDERSTAND → PLAN → BUILD → TEST → VERIFY → IMPROVE KNOWLEDGE.

DISCOVER maps the as-is process. UNDERSTAND identifies the measurable bottleneck. PLAN defines trigger, transformation, output, failure handling, cost, data ownership, and success criteria. BUILD implements the approved scope in a sandbox. TEST covers normal and edge cases. VERIFY measures the target metric and records acceptance. IMPROVE KNOWLEDGE captures reusable patterns, superseded approaches, client-specific customization, and deferred improvements.

Non-negotiables: no automation claim without verification; no unapproved recurring-cost tool; no silent scope expansion; no unapproved transfer of client data; reusable FDG capability remains distinguishable from client-specific customization.
