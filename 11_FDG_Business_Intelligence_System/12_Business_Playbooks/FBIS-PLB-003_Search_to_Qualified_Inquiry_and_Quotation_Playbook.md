## First smallest revenue experiment — Maintenance Evidence & Monthly Engineering Reporting

- **Intended audience:** Cebu-area and other actually serviceable property/facility managers with incomplete maintenance evidence and monthly reporting needs; do not imply regional capacity or engineering teams that have not been verified.
- **Public landing page:** one real service page on an existing authorized FDG marketing site, only after current site and source-code review; describe real deliverables, client inputs, exclusions, professional review, a representative **sample** clearly distinguished from client outputs, and a working inquiry CTA.
- **Discoverability:** inspect approved 5–10 URLs per FEXIS-SEO-0001; fix unintended blocking, misleading titles, broken CTA and mobile capture before publishing more content.
- **Lead intake:** request minimum customer fields with appropriate privacy notice/consent: name/organization, role, preferred reply channel, facility/asset group, current record availability, requested month. No facility-security layouts, asset vulnerabilities, private files, bank details or employee records in the public inquiry form.
- **Qualification:** real decision-maker authority, reporting burden, supplyable maintenance records, engineering feasibility and explicit budget/timing; record lead source and reason not qualified.
- **Quote:** freeze scope, service version, deliverables, pricing/promo validity, payment terms and excluded services; require approvals. Pricing from FSvIS-OM-0001, NOT whatever SEO content suggests.
- **Outcome:** actual verified payment, engineering-reviewed delivered report, formal client acceptance and complete direct cost/founder-time ledger; publish only sanitized and consented case studies.
- **Learning:** trace which actual search queries/pages generated a qualified buyer, whether the offer was suitable and where conversion failed, while protecting lead identities.

## Minimum funnel records and KPI denominator contracts

| Stage / event | Evidence required | What it is NOT |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Search visibility | Authorised Search Console site/query/page report, impressions, clicks, date | Not a client or ranking guarantee |
| Landing visit | Privacy-appropriate analytics sessions where lawfully available | Not identical to Search Console clicks |
| Inquiry | One verifiably stored unique request with timestamp, source, consent status | Not a paid order |
| Qualified lead | Human qualification with buyer-fit and authorized contact decision | Not every contact form request |
| Versioned proposal | Issued approved quotation with revision and identified prospect | Not automatic sale |
| Verified receipt | Independent payment-source verification and reversal/refund links | Not estimate, screenshot or promise |
| Delivery/acceptance | Controlled engineering report with evidence + signed/recorded client acceptance | Not "we generated PDF" |
| Renewal | Separate actual repeat contract/payment within eligible cohort | Not "interested in working again" |

Define timeframe, cohort, source, privacy and denominators before rates. Report missing stats N/A. Reconcile gross verified receipts, direct delivery cost, transaction fees, founder time and separately allocated acquisition cost.

## Small-scale launch workflow

1. **Preflight and permission:** Confirm real service readiness and existing business site; no deployment if code acceptance/privacy/auth issues remain unresolved. Verify domain/brand ownership and market-facing claims.
2. **Source & buyer mapping:** Pick one specific buyer problem and 3–5 candidate questions/queries based on real buyer interviews; no fabricated keyword-volume data or ranking position. Create a search-to-offer evidence ledger.
3. **Fix underlying path:** Approved SEO work → real mobile landing page → real inquiry storage and next action. If integration is demo-only, label it, and do not solicit information as though it were processed.
4. **Human review and publish separately:** Security check for forms, engineering check for claims, marketing review, commercial offer/price gate, explicit FPJIS release as applicable. New GitHub docs do not deploy a webpage.
5. **Capture feedback:** Monitor Search Console signals when verified; log actual inquiries, objections, qualifying conversations, approved proposals, verified receipts, service delivery hours, accepted outcomes.
6. **Decision after measurable window:** CONTINUE / IMPROVE / REPOSITION / PAUSE / STOP, recording evidence quality and uncertainty; repeated paid outcomes required before automation or broad cloning to all FDG platforms.
