# FBIS Security and Audit

Requirements: least privilege, secret isolation, server-side provider credentials, webhook authentication, idempotency, audit logging, transaction traceability, administrative action logging, environment separation, and backup/recovery.

Avoid storing payment-card data unless specifically required and architected for applicable compliance obligations. Prefer provider-hosted or tokenized mechanisms.
