# FBIS Audit Model

Audit material events: order creation, price calculation, voucher application, payment creation, provider event, validation result, fulfillment activation, refund, cancellation, settlement, reconciliation, and administrative changes.

Audit records identify event, timestamp, actor/system, entity, previous state, new state, and evidence.
