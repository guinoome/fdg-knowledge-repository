# FBIS Governance

Principles:
1. Business rules are explicit.
2. Financially material events are auditable.
3. Provider-specific logic remains replaceable.
4. Historical transactions preserve historical commercial context.
5. Client-side payment claims are not authoritative.
6. Voucher rules are deterministic and versioned.
7. Metrics require formal definitions.
8. Exceptions are visible.
9. Security controls protect sensitive information.
10. Validated organizational learning is preserved.

Material changes to pricing, payment validation, voucher rules, refunds, reconciliation, or revenue definitions require documented review.
