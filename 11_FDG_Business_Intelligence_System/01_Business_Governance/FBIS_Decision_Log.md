# FBIS Decision Log

FBIS-DEC-001 — FBIS owns business and commercial intelligence.

FBIS-DEC-002 — Payment intelligence belongs inside FBIS, not as a separate top-level Intelligence System.

FBIS-DEC-003 — Payment execution may be a reusable service while FBIS remains commercial authority.

FBIS-DEC-004 — Provider abstraction is required to prevent vendor lock-in.

FBIS-DEC-005 — Client-side payment success is not authoritative evidence.

FBIS-DEC-006 — Zero-value orders may bypass external payment.

FBIS-DEC-007 — Commercial rules and KPI definitions require versioning and auditability.

FBIS-DEC-008 — PayMongo is the Phase 1 payment execution provider behind the provider abstraction; it does not own FBIS commercial logic.
