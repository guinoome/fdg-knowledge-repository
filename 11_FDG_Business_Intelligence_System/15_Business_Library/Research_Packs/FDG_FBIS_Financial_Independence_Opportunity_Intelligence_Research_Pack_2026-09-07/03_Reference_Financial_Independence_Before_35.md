Claims such as immediate income improvement are seller marketing claims, not verified performance evidence.

## FDG extraction

The useful idea is not "get rich before 35." The useful abstraction is:

`Stabilize → Create Surplus → Build Productive Capacity → Reinvest → Compound → Reduce Dependence on Labor`

FDG strengthens this into:

`Labor → Knowledge → Process → Automation → Product → Platform → Capital`

and:

`Earn → Capture Knowledge → Standardize → Productize → Automate → Delegate → Recur → Scale → Reinvest → Compound → Owner-Independent Assets`

## Why the term "passive income" is insufficient

"Passive" obscures support load, maintenance, security, compliance, customer retention, capital risk, and human accountability. FBIS should prefer the more testable concepts:
- owner-independent revenue;
- recurring revenue;
- founder-independent operating coverage;
- productive assets;
- revenue quality;
- institutional continuity.
