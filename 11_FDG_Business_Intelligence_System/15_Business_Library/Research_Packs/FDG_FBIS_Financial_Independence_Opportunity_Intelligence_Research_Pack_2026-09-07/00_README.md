# Purpose

This pack compiles the full financial-independence discussion that began from the photographed Weupbooks advertisement and strengthens it with external evidence, quantitative models, repository integration guidance, templates, schemas, and Obsidian ``[[Wikilinks]]``.

It is designed for **additive integration into the existing FDG Knowledge Repository**, not as a replacement repository and not as a new standalone intelligence system.

## Canonical direction

> **The FDG Ecosystem continues creating engineering, commercial, intellectual, and financial value even when its founder is not personally executing each transaction.**

The design target is not literal "passive income." The target is:

> **An owner-independent, recurring-revenue-producing portfolio of intellectual property, platforms, services, systems, businesses, organizational capabilities, and productive assets.**

This capability belongs inside [[11_FDG_Business_Intelligence_System/11_FDG_Business_Intelligence_System_Master_Index|FDG Business Intelligence System (FBIS)]] and must connect to [[FDG Ecosystem]], [[10_FDG_CORE_Intelligence/10_FDG_CORE_Intelligence_Master_Index|FDG CORE]], [[08_FEIS_Engineering_Intelligence_Systems/08_FEIS_Engineering_Intelligence_Systems_Master_Index|FEIS]], [[17_FDG_Platform_Intelligence_System/00_FPI_Home|FDG Platform Intelligence]], [[19_FWAIS — FDG Workflow Automation Intelligence System]], and [[20_FPJIS_FDG_Project_Intelligence_System]].
