# Canonical principle

> **The FDG Ecosystem continues creating engineering, commercial, intellectual, and financial value even when its founder is not personally executing each transaction.**

## Interpretation

This principle does **not** mean "zero human involvement" and does not authorize unattended safety-critical engineering. It means the organization is designed so that value creation is carried by repeatable capability rather than by one person's constant presence.

Owner independence requires a combination of:

- documented and teachable processes;
- transferable decision rights;
- qualified delegation;
- automation where appropriate;
- institutional knowledge and evidence;
- recurring and repeatable revenue mechanisms;
- resilient technical and commercial infrastructure;
- controlled IP ownership and reuse;
- continuity and succession planning;
- auditable governance and quality controls.

## Design target

> **FDG becomes an owner-independent, recurring-revenue-producing portfolio of intellectual property, platforms, services, systems, businesses, organizational capabilities, and productive assets.**

## Anti-patterns

A business is **not** owner-independent merely because:
- it earns high revenue;
- it uses AI;
- it has subscriptions;
- it has automated payments;
- the founder can temporarily step away.

Owner dependency remains high if the founder is the only person who can sell, approve, diagnose, price, design, resolve exceptions, retain customer trust, access critical credentials, or reconstruct organizational knowledge.

## Project test

Every project/opportunity should answer:

> **Does this project merely produce revenue, or does it also create a reusable asset, system, capability, data advantage, IP, recurring income stream, or organizational capability that compounds over time?**

A project can still be accepted for immediate cashflow, but FBIS must record whether it compounds capability or merely consumes founder time.
