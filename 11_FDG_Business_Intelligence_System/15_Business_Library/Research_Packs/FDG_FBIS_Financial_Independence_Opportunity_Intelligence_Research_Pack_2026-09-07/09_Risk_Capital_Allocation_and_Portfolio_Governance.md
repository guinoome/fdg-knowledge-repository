# Why this matters

A financial-independence strategy fails if all surplus capital is deployed into attractive-looking projects without an explicit risk, liquidity, and opportunity-cost framework.

## Capital hierarchy

Before high-risk expansion, separate:
- operating liquidity;
- emergency/continuity reserves;
- committed liabilities;
- near-term CAPEX;
- experimental capital;
- growth capital;
- long-horizon investment capital.

Founder reserves and business reserves must remain legally/accountingly distinct.

## Economic evaluation

Use the method appropriate to the decision:

- payback: liquidity/recovery speed;
- ROI: simple return;
- NPV: value created after discounting cash flows;
- IRR: implied project return;
- ROIC: return on invested capital;
- scenario analysis: uncertainty;
- sensitivity analysis: key drivers;
- decision trees: staged uncertainty;
- real options: value of delay, expansion, contraction, or abandonment.

CFA Institute guidance treats NPV and IRR as core project-evaluation methods and highlights real options for managerial flexibility.

## Portfolio approach

Do not optimize all projects for the same horizon.

### Core
Known demand, proven economics, supports current cash engine.

### Adjacent
Extends proven capabilities to nearby customers/markets.

### Frontier
High uncertainty, potentially asymmetric option value.

## Experiment rule

Frontier ideas should begin as **small, bounded, reversible experiments** unless safety/regulation requires more formal qualification.

Record:
- hypothesis;
- maximum capital loss;
- maximum founder time;
- learning objective;
- success threshold;
- kill threshold;
- next option unlocked.

## Risk taxonomy

At minimum:
- market/demand;
- technical;
- delivery;
- financial;
- liquidity;
- legal/regulatory;
- safety/professional;
- security/privacy;
- IP;
- customer concentration;
- supplier/platform dependence;
- founder/key-person;
- reputational;
- operational continuity.

Align the process with [[10_FDG_CORE_Intelligence/FDG-CORE-STD-006_DECISION_INTELLIGENCE_STANDARD|CORE Decision Intelligence]] and evidence/provenance requirements.
