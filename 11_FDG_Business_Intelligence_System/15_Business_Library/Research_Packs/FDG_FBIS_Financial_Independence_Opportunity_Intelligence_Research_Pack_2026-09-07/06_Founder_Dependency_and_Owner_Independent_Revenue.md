# Core distinction

High revenue is not equivalent to independence.

A business can earn substantial revenue and still have catastrophic key-person risk if the founder is the only person who can execute or authorize critical work.

## Core metrics

### Revenue leverage

`Revenue Leverage = Revenue / Founder Hours`

Track by product/service and over time.

### Founder dependency ratio

`Founder Dependency Ratio = Founder Hours Required / Total Human Operating Hours`

This is useful but incomplete because one high-risk founder-only approval may matter more than many routine hours. Pair it with critical-process dependency metrics.

### Owner-independent revenue ratio

`Owner-Independent Revenue Ratio = Revenue Expected to Continue Without Direct Founder Execution / Total Revenue`

Revenue counts as owner-independent only if continued delivery does not require routine founder execution and necessary governance/quality control is transferable.

### Founder-only critical process count

Count critical processes where:
- only the founder possesses required knowledge;
- only the founder has authority;
- only the founder has credentials;
- only the founder has trusted customer access;
- no tested substitute exists.

### Documentation coverage

`Documentation Coverage = Critical Processes with Approved Current SOPs / Total Critical Processes`

### Delegation readiness

Score whether role, authority, competence, evidence, acceptance criteria, escalation path, and backup are defined.

## Key-person risk reduction sequence

`Identify → Document → Standardize → Train → Delegate → Test → Automate where justified → Audit → Improve`

Automation should not come before process understanding.

## Safety-critical exception

Engineering judgment, professional sign-off, statutory approvals, safety decisions, and controlled technical releases must retain competent human accountability. Owner independence means **replaceable organizational capability**, not removal of engineering responsibility.

## Continuity tests

- Can the founder be unavailable for 1 day?
- 1 week?
- 1 month?
- 3 months?
- permanently?

At each horizon, record which revenue streams, approvals, projects, credentials, and customer relationships fail.
