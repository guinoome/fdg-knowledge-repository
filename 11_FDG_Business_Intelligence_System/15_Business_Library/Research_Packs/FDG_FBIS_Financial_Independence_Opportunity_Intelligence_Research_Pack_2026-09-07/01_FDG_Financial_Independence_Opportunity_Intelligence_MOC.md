# Scope

[[FBIS Financial Independence & Opportunity Intelligence]] is a proposed canonical **capability within FBIS**, not a separate FDG intelligence system.

It joins four questions:

1. Is the founder financially resilient?
2. Is the business economically resilient?
3. Does an opportunity create only cash, or also reusable productive capability?
4. Can FDG continue producing value without requiring the founder to execute every transaction?

## Core model

`Labor → Knowledge → Process → Automation → Product → Platform → Capital`

The compounding operating loop is:

`Earn → Capture Knowledge → Standardize → Productize → Automate → Delegate → Recur → Scale → Reinvest → Compound → Create Owner-Independent Assets`
