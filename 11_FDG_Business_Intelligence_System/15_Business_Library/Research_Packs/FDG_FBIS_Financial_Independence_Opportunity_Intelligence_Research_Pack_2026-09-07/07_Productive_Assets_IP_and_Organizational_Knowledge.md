# FDG wealth model

A strategic—not accounting—view:

`FDG Wealth = Financial Capital + Intellectual Property + Software + Data + Processes + Brand + Customer Relationships + Recurring Contracts + Organizational Knowledge + Productive Assets`

## Critical accounting distinction

**Strategic/economic value is not the same as accounting recognition.**

WIPO describes intangible assets broadly, including IP, brands, software, data, and organizational know-how. IAS 38 imposes accounting recognition and measurement rules for intangible assets. Therefore FDG must maintain two related but distinct views:

1. **Accounting asset view** — what can be recognized under applicable accounting rules.
2. **Strategic capability/asset view** — resources and capabilities that may create value even when they are not recognized as balance-sheet assets.

Never inflate financial statements by treating every useful process, dataset, brand effect, or internal know-how item as an accounting asset.

## Productive asset classes for FDG

### Intellectual property
- copyrights;
- trademarks;
- patents where applicable;
- trade secrets;
- proprietary methods;
- licensed know-how.

### Software/platform assets
- reusable services;
- modules;
- APIs;
- calculation engines;
- workflow engines;
- governed schemas.

### Data assets
- engineering datasets;
- pricing intelligence;
- benchmark datasets;
- anonymized performance data where lawful;
- equipment/product mappings.

### Organizational knowledge
- standards;
- decision records;
- lessons learned;
- evidence packages;
- troubleshooting trees;
- templates;
- training packages;
- handover protocols.

### Commercial assets
- brand;
- customer relationships;
- distribution channels;
- recurring contracts;
- partner ecosystems.

## Reuse principle

An engineering engagement should be designed to produce two outputs:

1. client/project deliverable;
2. reusable FDG organizational learning, stripped of confidential/client-specific material as required.

## IP commercialization options

- direct use in FDG services;
- subscription access;
- enterprise licensing;
- white-label/tenant deployment;
- royalties/licensing;
- embedded capability in broader platform products.

Ownership, licensing, confidentiality, and client-created IP boundaries must be governed by [[13_FDG_Legal_Intelligence_System]].
