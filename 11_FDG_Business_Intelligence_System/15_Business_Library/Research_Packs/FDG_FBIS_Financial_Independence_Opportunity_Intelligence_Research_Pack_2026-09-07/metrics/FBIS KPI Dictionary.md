# KPI extensions

| KPI | Formula / definition | Primary use |
|---|---|---|
| Personal Runway | liquid emergency reserves / essential monthly expenses | founder resilience |
| Business Runway | unrestricted cash / net monthly burn, only when burn > 0 | liquidity |
| FI Coverage Ratio | sustainable non-salary cashflow / required lifestyle expenses | personal FI scenario |
| Revenue Leverage | revenue / founder hours | labor decoupling |
| Founder Dependency Ratio | founder hours / total human operating hours | dependency trend |
| Owner-Independent Revenue Ratio | owner-independent revenue / total revenue | continuity economics |
| Documentation Coverage | critical processes with approved current SOP / total critical processes | knowledge continuity |
| Delegation Coverage | critical processes with competent authorized backup / total critical processes | continuity |
| Recurring Revenue Share | recurring revenue / total revenue | revenue structure |
| Customer Concentration | revenue from top N customers / total revenue | concentration risk |
| Gross Revenue Retention | retained recurring revenue excluding expansion / starting recurring revenue | revenue durability |
| Net Revenue Retention | recurring revenue after churn/contraction/expansion / starting recurring revenue | cohort economics |
| CAC Payback | acquisition cost / monthly gross-profit contribution per acquired customer | growth efficiency |
| Reinvestment Rate | capital reinvested into approved productive assets/capabilities / distributable surplus | compounding |
| Knowledge Capture Rate | qualifying projects with reusable post-project knowledge captured / qualifying projects | organizational learning |
| IP Reuse Count | count of distinct approved reuse events for an IP/capability asset | capability leverage |
| Cross-Module Leverage | number/value of modules using a shared capability | platform leverage |
| Institutional Continuity Index | proposed composite; see [[16_Institutional_Continuity_Index]] | resilience |
| Revenue Quality Index | proposed composite; see [[17_Recurring_Revenue_Quality_Model]] | recurring revenue quality |
| Time to Learning | elapsed time from experiment authorization to decision-grade evidence | innovation efficiency |
