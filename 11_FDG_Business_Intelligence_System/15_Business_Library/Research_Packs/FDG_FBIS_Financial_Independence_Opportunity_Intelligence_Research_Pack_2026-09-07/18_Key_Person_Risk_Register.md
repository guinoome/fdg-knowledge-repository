# Objective

Identify operational, commercial, technical and governance functions that fail when one individual becomes unavailable.

| Risk ID | Critical capability | Sole holder? | Impact | Recovery time | Backup person/system | Documentation | Tested? | Mitigation |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---:|---|
| KP-001 | Example: pricing approval | Yes/No | H/M/L | hours/days | role/system | link | Yes/No | action |

## Categories

- customer relationship;
- sales/pricing;
- engineering approval;
- architecture;
- credentials/access;
- finance/payments;
- legal/compliance;
- repository knowledge;
- deployment/operations;
- incident response;
- supplier/provider relationship;
- strategic decision rights.

## Risk treatment priority

1. eliminate unnecessary founder-only controls;
2. document decision logic;
3. assign competent backup;
4. grant least-privilege access;
5. test handover;
6. automate repetitive parts where justified;
7. retain human approval for regulated/safety-critical decisions.
