# Research conclusion

The owner-independent FDG direction is strongly supported by established management disciplines, but it should be framed as a **multi-system organizational capability**, not as a financial shortcut.

The strongest external support comes from six bodies of knowledge:

1. business continuity;
2. knowledge management;
3. risk management;
4. innovation/opportunity management;
5. asset and intellectual-property management;
6. recurring-revenue economics.

## 1. Business continuity strengthens the founder-independence requirement

ISO 22301:2019 provides a business-continuity management framework for planning, operating, reviewing and continually improving an organization's ability to withstand and recover from disruption.

FDG implication:

Founder absence, collaborator loss, provider outage, repository migration, payment failure, or internet outage should be treated as **continuity scenarios**, not merely inconveniences.

This validates:
- [[06_Founder_Dependency_and_Owner_Independent_Revenue]]
- [[16_Institutional_Continuity_Index]]
- [[18_Key_Person_Risk_Register]]

**Current-status note (2026-09-07):** ISO 22301:2019 remains published, but ISO shows it at stage 90.92 and due for revision. The FDG repository should store the edition/year and periodically check supersession.

## 2. Knowledge management is a continuity mechanism, not documentation overhead

ISO 30401:2018 establishes requirements/guidance for knowledge-management systems.

FDG implication:

Knowledge must survive changes in:
- people;
- models/agents;
- software;
- vendors;
- platforms;
- organizational structure.

The repository therefore is not merely an archive; it is part of FDG's continuity architecture.

**Current-status note:** ISO 30401:2018 remains the published edition, while ISO/DIS 30401 Edition 2 is under development in 2026.

## 3. Opportunity intelligence should follow innovation-management discipline

ISO 56007:2023 specifically addresses tools and methods for managing **opportunities and ideas**, including identifying opportunities, creating concepts and validating them.

ISO 56008:2024 addresses measurement of:
- innovation processes;
- innovation initiatives;
- innovation portfolios;
- evidence-based go/no-go/pivot/refine decisions.

ISO 56006:2021 addresses strategic intelligence for collecting, processing and using knowledge for strategic decisions.

FDG implication:

[[05_FDG_Opportunity_Intelligence_Model]] should not be a static scorecard. It should be a managed evidence loop:

`Opportunity → Hypothesis → Evidence → Experiment → Measurement → Decision → Learning → Portfolio Reallocation`

This substantially strengthens [[15_Decision_Rules_and_Stage_Gates]].

## 4. IP must be managed deliberately

ISO 56005:2020 provides guidance on IP management within innovation. WIPO also identifies IP, software, data, brands and organizational know-how as economically important intangible assets and explains licensing/assignment as commercialization routes.

FDG implication:

Every project should ask:
- What IP is created?
- Who owns it?
- What can legally be reused?
- What must remain confidential?
- Can it be licensed?
- Can it become shared platform capability?
- What evidence is required to establish provenance?

This supports:
[[07_Productive_Assets_IP_and_Organizational_Knowledge]]

**Current-status note:** ISO 56005:2020 is marked to be revised, with Edition 2 work underway in 2026.

## 5. Asset management strengthens lifecycle economics

ISO 55001:2024 is the current edition of the asset-management-system requirements standard. Its 2024 revision explicitly strengthens decision-making and value.

FDG implication:

Productive assets should be managed through their lifecycle using value, performance, risk and expenditure—not merely acquisition cost.

This applies to:
- software;
- infrastructure;
- equipment;
- datasets;
- reusable engineering capability;
- commercial systems.

Important distinction: not every strategically valuable capability qualifies as an accounting asset.

## 6. Strategic asset value and accounting recognition must remain separate

WIPO treats software, data, brands and organizational know-how as important intangible assets in the economic sense. IAS 38 applies stricter accounting recognition and measurement criteria, and specifically limits recognition of many internally generated intangibles.

FDG implication:

Maintain two views:

`Strategic Capability Register ≠ Accounting Asset Register`

This prevents inflated financial claims while preserving visibility of real organizational capability.

## 7. Recurring revenue must be judged by quality, not only quantity

Stripe's 2026 SaaS metrics guidance includes CAC, churn, NRR, MRR, ARR, customer concentration, gross margin and LTV. These metrics are useful management primitives even if Stripe is not FDG's payment provider.

FDG implication:

Subscription revenue that is founder-heavy, low-margin, concentrated or high-churn is not high-quality owner-independent revenue.

## 8. Platform/network effects must be proven

Harvard Business School defines a network effect as a situation where product/platform value depends on the number of users/buyers/sellers.

FDG implication:

Do not claim a "network effect" merely because an FDG platform has many users. Classify whether value genuinely increases through:
- same-side participation;
- cross-side participation;
- complementors;
- shared learning/data effects.

This avoids inflated platform economics.

## 9. Philippine financial resilience is consistent with BSP education

BSP financial-education material emphasizes prudent planning, reserves, asset accumulation and debt management.

FDG implication:

The personal/founder financial layer should prioritize resilience before speculative return maximization.

## 10. 2026 standards-watch requirement

Several relevant standards are currently being revised. Therefore every FDG knowledge record that depends on an external standard should store:

- standard/reference number;
- edition/year;
- status;
- date checked;
- source;
- supersession/review trigger.

This should eventually be automated through [[10_FDG_CORE_Intelligence/FDG-CORE-STD-009_KNOWLEDGE_SURVEILLANCE_STANDARD|CORE Knowledge Surveillance]].

## New architectural insight

The strongest version of the original idea is not:

> "Create passive income."

It is:

> **Create an organization whose knowledge, processes, authority, recurring economics, IP, assets, and continuity mechanisms allow value creation to persist through changes in people, tools, providers, and technology.**

That is a much more durable FDG design objective.
