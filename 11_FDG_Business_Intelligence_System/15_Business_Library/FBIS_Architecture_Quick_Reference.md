# FBIS Architecture Quick Reference

FBIS owns Business → Sales → Orders → Pricing → Vouchers → Payments → Refunds → Reconciliation → Revenue → Commercial Intelligence. Reusable services execute capabilities. Platforms consume capabilities. Providers remain replaceable. Evidence remains auditable. Rules are versioned. Exceptions are visible.
