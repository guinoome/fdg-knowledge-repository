# FMCIS-MEDIA-001 — FDG Media Production System

## Founder direction

Develop Nex and the FDG collaborator team into a durable filmmaking and commercial media-production capability. This is not a mandate to buy subscriptions or become dependent on OpenArt, Higgsfield, Runway, Google Flow or any single model/provider.

**FDG trains and owns its people, role contracts, methods, examples, review standards, workflows and skills. External platforms and models are replaceable production instruments.** Nex is the production orchestrator and can request bounded help from FMCIS collaborators with relevant expertise. Collaborators must research, challenge, test, teach and return reusable knowledge to the FDG Knowledge Repository.

The commercial objective is repeatable, rights-aware, technically coherent media that helps FDG explain, market, sell and support its products and services. Measure accepted output, cost, time and customer outcomes—not the volume of generated clips or novelty of a tool.

## Architecture disposition

**EXTEND** the existing FMCIS coordination and capability registry, NEX-COLLAB-LEARN-001 controlled skill evolution, NEX-ROLE-ENGINEERING-001 role engineering, existing FCMIS social publishing/content operation, video source-fidelity review and FDG commercial claim/release controls.

Do not create a competing agent organization, media memory silo, permanent subscription stack or second publishing system. FMCIS owns collaborator coordination; Nex owns orchestration and consolidation; the project/domain owner approves product truth and content; the Founder retains final authority.

## Capability map

1. Creative development and direction: audience, customer problem, concept, story structure, hook, script, dialogue, visual storytelling, pacing and CTA.
2. Cinematography: composition, shot size, camera angle, camera movement, focal length/lens behavior, depth of field, exposure, lighting, color and continuity.
3. Production design and visual identity: character/host, wardrobe, environment, props, product geometry, brand identity, visual bible and reusable reference assets.
4. Generative-media engineering: text/image/video workflows, reference conditioning, image-to-video, video-to-video, start/end frames, multi-shot, motion control, model routing, prompt design, reproducibility and failure analysis.
5. Editing and post-production: shot selection, continuity editing, pacing, transitions, titles, typography, captions, color grading, sound design, narration, music, mix and platform exports.
6. Commercial production: product ads, direct-response ads, UGC, corporate films, explainers, training, case studies, social-first creative, campaign variants and controlled creative testing.
7. Production economics and operations: production planning, asset provenance, review effort, throughput, failure/rework, cost per approved shot and cost per approved finished minute.
8. Rights, privacy and release: source provenance, model/provider terms, commercial-use rights, copyright, likeness/voice consent, client confidentiality, data egress, publication authority and claims approval.

## Dynamic FMCIS production roles

Nex assembles only the roles needed for each assignment. A collaborator can cover multiple roles only where competence is evidenced and independent review remains intact for material claims.

| Role | Required output |
|---|---|
| Creative director / producer | Approved brief, objective, audience, concept and production constraints |
| Screenwriter / story editor | Script, hook, beat sheet, dialogue, claims register and CTA |
| Director of photography / cinematographer | Shot design, lens/camera/light plan, visual language and continuity |
| Storyboard / previs specialist | Storyboard, timing, reference frames and animatic where useful |
| Generative-media engineer | Model/tool routing, reference conditioning, prompts, settings, generation log and failure analysis |
| Production designer / art director | Character, environment, prop, product and brand reference system |
| Editor / post-production specialist | Editable sequence, pacing, continuity, color, titles, captions and exports |
| Sound designer / composer / voice specialist | Narration, dialogue, ambience, Foley, music rights and final mix |
| Commercial producer / marketing strategist | Customer/audience fit, offer, proof, platform-native creative and experiment design |
| Rights / privacy / security reviewer | Asset rights, consent, model terms, data processing and egress review |
| Independent quality reviewer | Artifact-based review against brief, technical truth and acceptance criteria |

**Delegation truth:** a role assignment is not proof of dispatch. Nex must use a real supported collaborator/subagent invocation and preserve its receipt. If no dispatch mechanism is available, record DISPATCH BLOCKED, prepare the work card, and do not claim that a collaborator researched or reviewed the work.

## End-to-end production lifecycle

BRIEF → RESEARCH → CONCEPT → SCRIPT → STORYBOARD / VISUAL BIBLE → MODEL ROUTING → PROOF SHOTS → GENERATION → SELECTS → EDIT → AUDIO / COLOR / CAPTIONS → QUALITY / RIGHTS REVIEW → OWNER APPROVAL → EXPORT → DISTRIBUTION → MEASUREMENT → LEARNING

1. Brief: target audience, customer problem, offer, supported proof, intended action, channel, duration, aspect ratios, language, brand, deadline, owner and budget.
2. Research: source-supported product facts, audience and channel context, reference work and claims register. Distinguish facts, assumptions, creative interpretation and unknowns.
3. Concept: alternatives, narrative arc, opening hook, emotional/educational payoff and CTA.
4. Pre-production: script, beat sheet, shot list, storyboard, visual bible, asset manifest, audio plan, budget and acceptance rubric.
5. Model routing: choose the least costly authorized workflow that can meet the specific shot requirement; distinguish platform, model, feature, adapter and editor.
6. Proof generation: create a few representative stills and short shots first. Obtain creative/product-owner acceptance before scaling the sequence.
7. Production: generate controlled variations, record model/version, inputs, settings, credits, failures and selected takes.
8. Post-production: assemble approved shots, repair continuity where possible, add narration/music/sound, color, titles, captions and channel variants.
9. Quality and rights: inspect the complete film, not just hero frames. Check technical claims, product fidelity, continuity, accessibility, licensing, consent, privacy and export.
10. Release: obtain the required owner/client/publication approval. Export only approved versions. Posting, ad spend, customer delivery and account actions remain separately authorized.
11. Learning: capture reusable, source-backed improvements, validate them and promote through NEX-COLLAB-LEARN-001.

## Provider/model landscape

Detailed source-backed snapshot: [[21_FDG_Multi_Collaborator_Intelligence_System/02_Capability_Registry/FMCIS-MEDIA-PLATFORM-LANDSCAPE-2026-11-11|AI Filmmaking Platform and Model Landscape — 2026-11-11]].

Keep categories distinct:
- OpenArt: multi-model creative platform/workspace, Director, character library, MCP/CLI and Smart Shot.
- Higgsfield: AI creative suite with Cinema Studio, Marketing Studio, Canvas, Supercomputer and MCP/CLI.
- Google Flow: creative workspace/orchestrator for Google's image/video models and editing/scene tools.
- Veo: Google's video model family.
- Gemini Omni: Google's multimodal video generation/editing model family; available through Flow and other distribution surfaces. A model name is not itself a separate platform.
- Nano Banana: Google's image-generation/editing model family; use for visual development and references, not as a video model.
- Runway: creative platform and separately documented developer API/model-routing service; API catalog includes models from Runway and external labs.
- Other candidates: Seedance, Kling, Luma, Flux, ComfyUI and local/open-source media workflows; evaluate when they solve a real capability gap.

No provider is selected as permanent winner. Tool fit is task-specific and version-sensitive. Vendor marketing claims are hypotheses until independently tested.

## No-subscription-first operating policy

- No new paid subscription is required to research, train the team or build FDG skills.
- First use official public documentation, existing authorized ChatGPT/Higgsfield/Runway integrations, genuinely free tiers/credits, existing user-authorized access, open-source tools and synthetic/public-rights test assets.
- Do not assume a free tier or existing connector is enabled for every collaborator or available in every region/account.
- Do not create accounts, accept terms, start trials, buy credits, incur spend or change a billing plan without explicit approval.
- No confidential FDG, customer, employee, facility or unreleased product media may be uploaded to a third-party service without data-owner authorization and provider-specific privacy/retention/terms review.
- Free access is not automatically privacy-safe, commercially licensed or operationally sufficient.
- Keep editable project files, original assets, exports, metadata and prompts in FDG-controlled storage where lawful and technically possible. Avoid vendor-only asset libraries as the sole record.

The skill family is a training and operating system for the human/collaborator team. It does not retrain underlying model weights. Every collaborator should receive scoped role contracts, examples, practice tasks, evaluation rubrics and feedback. Lessons should improve FDG-owned SOPs and skills, not become trapped in an individual agent session.

## Common benchmark and first pilot

Use one controlled benchmark pack and one identical brief across candidates. Use synthetic or public-rights assets initially.

Benchmark tasks:
1. Product still with exact text/logo/geometry preservation.
2. One character/host across at least three distinct scenes.
3. One environment across different camera angles and lighting.
4. A 20–30 second 4–6-shot commercial sequence.
5. Targeted edit of a source shot while preserving non-target content.
6. Narration, music, sound and captions.
7. Editable export and asset provenance retrieval.

FDG Solar Visayas is a candidate pilot, subject to product-owner approval of brief, actual product imagery and verified claims. Working concept from discussion: “Power your home. Own your energy future.” This is a creative direction, not an approved advertising claim. Candidate output: 20–30 second 16:9 master plus 9:16 variant, with 4–6 scenes, accurate FDG branding, narration, soundtrack and CTA.

Do not start full production before storyboards/reference frames and representative proof shots pass review. No public release or customer distribution is authorized by this capability record.

Evaluation dimensions: prompt/reference adherence; product and brand fidelity; character/environment consistency; cinematographic coherence; temporal quality; editability; audio quality; rights/privacy; usable-shot yield; first-pass acceptance; human effort/latency; and unit economics.

Primary economics:
- cost per approved shot = total attributable production cost / accepted shots
- cost per approved finished minute = total attributable production cost / accepted finished minutes
- human hours per finished minute = production, review and rework hours / accepted finished minutes

Include credit usage, failed generations, retries, subscriptions only when actually incurred, local compute/storage, post-production tools, collaborator time and review effort. For free credits, report cash cost and separately record consumption/opportunity cost; do not call the workflow zero-cost. A single showcase output is a demo, not a benchmark.

## Technical truth, brand and safety controls

AI-generated engineering footage is illustrative unless validated against approved source data and qualified domain review. Do not invent equipment models, electrical connections, ratings, installation practices, code compliance, savings, certifications, test results or operating conditions. A polished animation is not engineering evidence, a simulation result or proof of installed conditions.

For solar and engineering marketing, product owners approve specifications, savings assumptions, warranties, regulatory claims, imagery and CTA. Separate actual product photographs from generated concept imagery; label illustrative scenes where a reasonable viewer could otherwise mistake them for real installation evidence.

## Distribution and commercial measurement

Reuse existing [[FCMIS/FCMIS-Social-Publishing-Routine-Growth-Operations|FCMIS Social Publishing Routine and Growth Operations]] and [[FCMIS/FCMIS-REF-Video-Watch-Multimodal-2026-10-26|multimodal video source-fidelity review]]. Do not create a second publishing ledger or social automation system.

Track content/campaign ID, approved source claims, script/storyboard version, asset provenance, model/version, generation log, reviewer and owner approval, final export hashes, channel variants, publication authorization, actual publication receipt, metrics, qualified leads, paid conversions, collected revenue and contribution margin.

Impressions/views are not sales. A generated video is not an accepted client deliverable. A published post is not verified revenue. Publishing and external campaign spend require separate approval.
