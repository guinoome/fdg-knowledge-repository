## Mandatory software defect assurance

For any work package involving a software defect, bug, regression, unexpected behavior, failed test/acceptance, calculation/data discrepancy or runtime/integration failure, the assignment MUST cite and follow [[22_FDG_Audit_Intelligence_System/10_System_and_Software_Audit/FAIS-SSA-1001 - Evidence-First Software Defect Diagnosis and Minimal Repair Protocol|FAIS-SSA-1001 v1.1]]. Record the protocol version, repository/branch/commit baseline, defect severity, diagnosis owner, implementer, risk-triggered verifier, project/domain approval authority, exact repair scope and required evidence. The receiving collaborator must acknowledge the protocol before defect work. No collaborator may bypass the read-only diagnosis gate, perform unauthorized repair or claim unexecuted tests. Routine feature work remains governed by its approved FPJIS package; defects discovered during that work trigger FAIS-SSA-1001 for the affected defect.
