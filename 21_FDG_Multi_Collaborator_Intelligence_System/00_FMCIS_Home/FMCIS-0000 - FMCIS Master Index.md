# FMCIS-0000 — FMCIS Master Index

**System:** 21_FDG_Multi_Collaborator_Intelligence_System (FMCIS)

**Status:** Architecture Foundation — Draft

**Owner / Final Authority:** Francis

**Architecture / Intelligence Authority:** Nex

## Purpose

FMCIS is the FDG organizational intelligence layer for coordinating multiple external collaborators, native agent teams, specialist capabilities, evidence exchange, debate, iterative execution, and organizational learning.

Its purpose is to convert one person's intent into coordinated multi-collaborator execution while preserving authority, accountability, evidence, memory, and final human approval.

> **Nex is always on top, but Nex is never above learning.**

## Authority Model

Francis retains final authority.

Nex remains the FDG Architecture & Intelligence Authority.

FMCIS coordinates capability and execution. It does not become an authority above Nex.

When Nex is not yet continuously online, Francis performs the review and approval function. FMCIS prepares structured intelligence for later Nex review and learning.

A Founder decision may bypass a Nex recommendation when necessary. Such a decision must be recorded and made available to Nex as learning context.

## Design Principles

- Dynamic capability teams, not permanent fixed teams.
- Chief of Staff coordinates; Nex remains above it.
- Collaborator-native orchestration is reused rather than rebuilt.
- Chat collaboration is primarily used for independent planning, research, comparison, and debate.
- Agent environments are primarily used for tool-driven execution, repository work, testing, and iteration.
- Model selection is capability-weighted.
- Do not maximize agents; maximize useful intelligence per unit of time, token, and cost.
- Consensus is not truth; evidence quality determines resolution.
- Every failed loop must produce information that improves the next loop.
- FMCIS remembers the work; Nex remembers what the organization learned.
- Automate execution before automating authority.
