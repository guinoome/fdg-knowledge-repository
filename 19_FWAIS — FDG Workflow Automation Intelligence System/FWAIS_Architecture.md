# FWAIS Architecture

## System Definition

**FWAIS — FDG Workflow Automation Intelligence System**

FWAIS provides the organizational capability to transform validated workflows into repeatable, monitored, auditable, and continuously improving execution.

## Major Capabilities

1. Workflow Discovery Intelligence
2. Workflow Design Intelligence
3. Automation Intelligence
4. Workflow Orchestration
5. Decision & Approval Intelligence
6. Monitoring & Reliability Intelligence
7. Workflow Optimization Intelligence
8. Workflow Knowledge Intelligence
9. Capability Automation
10. Integration Intelligence

## 5R Automation Qualification

A workflow is a strong automation candidate when it is:

- Repetitive
- Rules-based
- Repeatable
- Reviewable
- ROI-positive

A sixth gate is mandatory for safety-critical or sensitive workflows:

- Safe

Therefore:

**5R + Safety → Automation Candidate**

## Architecture Relationship

Operational Systems perform domain work.

Intelligence Systems reason, analyze, optimize, and govern.

FWAIS operationalizes approved workflows.

Execution technologies are replaceable.

The FDG Knowledge Repository preserves durable organizational knowledge.
