## Recommended motion system

### 1. Workflow pulse
Active workflow edges glow or pulse lightly during execution.

### 2. Card lift / focus transitions
Selected workflow stages or KPI cards elevate subtly when focused.

### 3. Live execution ticker
Activity feed updates with subtle timed motion rather than aggressive animation.

### 4. Node expansion
Clicking a workflow node reveals a side drawer with configuration, test state, and outputs.

### 5. Confidence branching
Decision nodes can animate alternate paths when confidence thresholds trigger escalation.

### 6. ROI trend animation
Charts animate progressively to show outcome improvement over time.

### 7. Assistant onboarding motion
The assistant character appears as a calm contextual guide, not a hyperactive mascot.

## Suggested demo video sequence

### Demo 1 — Executive teaser (30–45 sec)
- show business problem

- highlight business impact metrics
- show lead → quotation → CRM → follow-up journey
- end with ROI value

### Demo 2 — Workflow builder walkthrough (45–90 sec)
- open workflow builder
- drag or activate nodes
- inspect language detection / intent classification / approval nodes
- show execution preview
- show human-in-the-loop branch

### Demo 3 — Business impact narrative (30–60 sec)
- show top-level KPIs
- show time saved / revenue recovered / automation coverage
- show live insights and bottlenecks
- close with “from idea to impact” narrative
