# FWAIS ROI Intelligence

## Purpose

FWAIS should evaluate automation by measurable business outcome.

## Core measures

- Hours saved
- Labor cost avoided
- Revenue recovered
- Revenue protected
- Error reduction
- Faster turnaround
- Customer response improvement
- Compliance effort reduced
- Capacity released
- Conversion improvement

## Simple business model

Annual Benefit
− Annual Operating Cost
− Implementation Amortization
= Net Annual Value

## Important discipline

All estimated benefits must be clearly labeled as assumptions until validated by real operational data.
