# FWAIS Opportunity Map — Visayas → Philippines → Global

## Geographic rollout rule

Start where the workflow pain can be directly observed and validated:

1. **Visayas**
2. **Philippines**
3. **Global**

## Visayas-first sectors

- Hotels and resorts
- Property management
- Construction
- Engineering contractors
- MEPF contractors
- Facilities management
- Restaurants and hospitality
- Automotive shops
- Fuel stations
- Solar / PV businesses
- Trading and distribution
- Retail
- Healthcare
- Dental clinics
- Professional services
- Accounting firms
- Real estate
- Logistics
- Education
- Tourism

## Rule for national promotion

Promote a workflow pattern to Philippine scope only when:

- the problem recurs across multiple organizations
- the workflow remains substantially similar
- the integration requirements are reusable
- ROI is measurable
- regulatory differences are understood

## Rule for global promotion

Promote globally only when:

- the core business problem is broadly recurring
- the workflow remains technology-neutral
- jurisdiction-specific controls are identified
- evidence supports demand
- value is demonstrable
