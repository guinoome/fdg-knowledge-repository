# FWAIS Enhancements We Should Not Forget

These are enhancement points added to strengthen the conversation package.

## 1. Workflow Registry
Maintain a formal registry of workflow definitions, status, owners, KPIs, and current implementation mechanisms.

## 2. Capability Registry
Maintain a technology-neutral record of what FDG can currently do.

## 3. Integration Registry
Maintain a record of how each capability is currently connected and validated.

## 4. Approval / Escalation templates
Build reusable escalation and human-in-the-loop templates across workflow families.

## 5. Outcome measurement discipline
Measure actual business effect after deployment, not just deployment completion.

## 6. Design authority alignment
Use FPIS as the authority for maturing the platform visual language.

## 7. Future motion demo assets
Convert the three demo modes into future short product videos or animated prototypes.

## 8. Visual workflow representation standard
Define an FDG-wide standard for how workflow triggers, decisions, agents, approvals, evidence, and errors are represented visually.

## 9. Local-first / provider-replaceable discipline
Preserve FDG’s organizational capability even when underlying tools change.

## 10. Handover discipline
Before token/time limits are reached, maintain concise handover notes so other collaborators can continue work without re-reading everything.
