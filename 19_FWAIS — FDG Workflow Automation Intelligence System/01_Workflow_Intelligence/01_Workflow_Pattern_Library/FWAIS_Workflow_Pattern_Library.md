# FWAIS Workflow Pattern Library

## Purpose

This library stores reusable workflow archetypes extracted from validated business needs. Patterns describe the business problem and workflow logic, not a permanent technology stack.

7. Voice-of-Customer Intelligence
8. Compliance Pre-Screening
9. Content/Data Transformation
10. Knowledge Capture
11. Predictive Intervention

## Pattern rule

A pattern is reusable only when its business intent, inputs, outputs, decision rules, failure conditions, controls, and measurable outcomes are understood.
