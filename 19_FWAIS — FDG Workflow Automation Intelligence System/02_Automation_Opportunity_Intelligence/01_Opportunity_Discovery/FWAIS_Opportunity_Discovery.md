# FWAIS Automation Opportunity Discovery

FWAIS should discover automation opportunities from actual business friction rather than from technology trends.

## Discovery sources

- Interviews
- Process observation
- Time studies
- Error records
- Customer complaints
- Revenue leakage
- Unpaid invoices
- Missed calls
- Lost leads
- Repetitive documentation
- Repeated data entry
- Compliance workload
- RFP/tender workload
- Support tickets
- Cancellation reasons
- Existing workflow logs
- Industry research
- Approved external intelligence

## Discovery question

> What work is repeatedly consuming time, money, attention, or revenue that can be standardized and safely improved?

## Demand-first rule

Validate demand locally first in the Visayas, then expand successful patterns to the Philippines and global markets.
