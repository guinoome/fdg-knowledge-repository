## Additional engineering lesson

A client-facing automation should have:

- Measurable baseline
- Clear scope
- Defined implementation boundary
- Human escalation
- Error handling
- Test scenarios
- ROI hypothesis
- Operational ownership

Claims such as specific project duration, pricing, or market demand must be independently verified before FDG uses them commercially.
