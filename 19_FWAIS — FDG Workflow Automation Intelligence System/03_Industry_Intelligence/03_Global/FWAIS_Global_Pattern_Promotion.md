# FWAIS Global Pattern Promotion

A workflow pattern may be promoted globally when:

- Business problem is broadly recurring
- Core workflow is technology-neutral
- Jurisdiction-specific controls are identified
- Integration adapters are replaceable
- Evidence supports demand
- ROI is demonstrable
- Security and compliance boundaries are explicit
