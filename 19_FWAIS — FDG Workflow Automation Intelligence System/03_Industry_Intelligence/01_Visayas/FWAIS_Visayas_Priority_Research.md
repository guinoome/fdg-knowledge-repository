# FWAIS Visayas Priority Research

## Objective

Build the initial automation demand intelligence base from businesses operating in the Visayas.

## Priority evidence

Prefer:

1. Direct client interviews
2. Process observations
3. Measured operational data
4. Local industry associations
5. Government/public datasets
6. Credible industry studies
7. Vendor claims only when independently verified

## Initial opportunity themes

- Missed customer inquiries
- Quotation/RFQ turnaround
- Accounts receivable follow-up
- Procurement and supplier coordination
- Hotel engineering and facilities workflows
- Documentation and SOP capture
- Customer feedback consolidation
- Sales-call and service QA
- Compliance pre-screening
- Inventory/reorder workflows
- Project reporting
- Tender/RFP response work
