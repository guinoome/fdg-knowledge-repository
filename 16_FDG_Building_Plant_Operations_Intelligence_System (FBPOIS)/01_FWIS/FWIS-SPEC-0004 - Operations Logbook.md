# Purpose

The Operations Logbook serves as the official chronological record of engineering operations.

It captures all operational events, whether manually entered by engineering personnel or automatically generated by integrated FWIS modules.

The Operations Logbook functions as the permanent engineering event ledger for the property.

---

# Objectives

The module shall:

- Preserve operational history.
- Record all engineering events.
- Improve accountability.
- Support investigations.
- Provide evidence for audits.
- Preserve engineering knowledge.
- Supply data for analytics.

---

# Scope

The Operations Logbook records events from all engineering operational activities.

It does not replace module-specific records.

Instead, it references them while maintaining a unified chronological timeline.

---

# Linked Records

Every event may reference another module.

Examples:

Incident

↓

Open Incident Record

Plant Alarm

↓

Plant Operations Record

Utility Reading

↓

Utilities Monitoring Record

OOO Room

↓

Room Status Record

No duplicate operational data shall be stored.

---

# Attachments

Each log entry may contain:

- Photographs
- Videos
- Documents
- Inspection Forms
- Equipment Readings
- Voice Notes (Future)

Attachments become permanent engineering records.

---

# User Actions

Authorized users may:

- Create Manual Entry
- View Events
- Search Events
- Attach Files
- Export Reports

Users cannot delete permanent records.

Corrections shall be recorded as amendments.

↓

↓

↓

---

# Business Rules

- Every entry receives a unique Event ID.
- Event timestamps are generated automatically.
- Original records cannot be deleted.
- Edits require audit logging.
- Critical events require acknowledgement.
- Linked records remain synchronized.
