# Purpose

The Data Lifecycle Management Architecture defines how information is created, validated, stored, retained, archived, restored, and disposed throughout its lifecycle.

The objective is to ensure that FBPOIS maintains accurate, auditable, secure, and efficient information management while preserving engineering knowledge and historical records.

---

# Objectives

The lifecycle framework shall:

- Preserve engineering records.
- Prevent uncontrolled data growth.
- Maintain historical traceability.
- Support enterprise audits.
- Enable disaster recovery.
- Protect organizational knowledge.
- Support future analytics and AI services.

---

# Lifecycle Principles

The platform shall follow these principles:

- Data is an enterprise asset.
- Information shall remain traceable.
- Historical records shall be preserved.
- Disposal shall follow organizational policy.
- Critical engineering records shall never be silently deleted.

---

# Data Lifecycle

```text
Planning

Creation

↓

↓

```

---

# Lifecycle Stages

## Planning

Business rules, ownership, and classification are defined before data is created.

---

## Creation

Information is generated by:

- Users
- Mobile Applications
- Windows Applications
- APIs
- IoT Devices
- SCADA
- Building Management Systems

Every record receives a globally unique identifier.

---

## Validation

Validation includes:

- Required fields
- Business rules
- Duplicate detection
- Referential integrity
- Security validation

Invalid records shall not be committed.

---

## Approval

Certain records require approval.

Examples:

- Incident Reports
- Operational Reports
- Plant Status Changes
- Asset Changes
- Master Data Updates

Approval workflows shall be configurable.

---

## Operational Use

Approved data becomes available for:

- Operations
- Maintenance
- Reports
- Analytics
- Dashboards
- APIs
- NEX

---

## Historical Reference

Historical records remain searchable and available for:

- Investigations
- Benchmarking
- Trend Analysis
- Engineering Reviews
- Lessons Learned

Historical records shall remain immutable unless formally corrected.

---

## Archive

Inactive information may be archived.

Archived records:

- remain searchable
- retain audit history
- preserve relationships
- support restoration

---

## Retention Review

Retention policies determine whether archived information should be:

- retained
- extended
- permanently preserved
- disposed

Policies shall be configurable by organization.

---

## Disposal

Disposal shall:

- follow organizational policy
- maintain audit records
- preserve referential integrity
- prevent unauthorized deletion

Permanent deletion shall require elevated authorization.

---

# Data Categories

The platform manages:

## Master Data

Examples:

- Organization
- Property
- Buildings
- Operational Assets
- Equipment

Retention:

Permanent unless retired.

---

## Transactional Data

Examples:

- Daily Operations
- Utility Readings
- Incidents
- Work Orders
- Plant Readings

Retention:

Organization configurable.

---

## Analytical Data

Examples:

- KPIs
- Trends
- Forecasts
- Benchmark Results

Retention:

Configurable.

Analytics may be regenerated from transactional data where appropriate.

---

## Knowledge Data

Examples:

- Engineering Notes
- Lessons Learned
- Standard Procedures
- Best Practices

Retention:

Permanent.

Knowledge represents organizational intellectual capital.

---

## Audit Data

Examples:

- Login History
- Record Changes
- Permission Changes
- API Access
- Synchronization Logs

Retention:

Long-term according to organizational policy.

---

# Attachments

Supported attachments include:

- PDF
- Images
- Videos
- Drawings
- CAD Files
- Manuals
- Reports

Attachments shall maintain links to associated records.

---

# Version Management

The platform shall support version history for:

- Master Data
- Documents
- Procedures
- Configuration
- Templates

Previous versions remain accessible.

---

# Backup Strategy

Supports:

- Full Backup
- Incremental Backup
- Point-in-Time Recovery
- Snapshot Backup
- Geo-Redundant Backup

Backup schedules shall be configurable.

---

# Disaster Recovery

Supports:

- Local Recovery
- Secondary Data Center
- Private Cloud Recovery
- Hybrid Recovery

Recovery objectives shall be organization configurable.

---

# Archival Storage

Archived information may be stored in:

- PostgreSQL Archive
- Object Storage
- Cold Storage
- Enterprise Archive

Storage implementation may vary by deployment.

---

# Business Rules

- Records shall not be permanently deleted without authorization.
- Audit history shall never be removed independently of the associated record.
- Archived records remain searchable.
- Knowledge records shall not be automatically disposed.
- Every lifecycle transition shall be recorded.

---

# Security

Lifecycle management shall support:

- Role-Based Access Control
- Audit Logging
- Encryption
- Secure Disposal
- Retention Policies
