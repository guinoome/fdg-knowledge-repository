# Zero-Value Orders

When:

Final Order Amount = 0

the system may confirm the order without creating an external payment transaction.

Example:

Product      ₱499
Voucher     -₱499
-----------------
Total          ₱0

This supports free campaigns, internal testing, promotional grants, and fully discounted products.
