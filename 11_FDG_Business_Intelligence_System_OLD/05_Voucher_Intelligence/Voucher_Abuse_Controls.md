# Voucher Abuse Controls

Controls may include:
- usage limits
- customer limits
- campaign limits
- expiration
- eligibility rules
- reservation before payment
- duplicate redemption prevention
- velocity monitoring
- suspicious usage detection

The system should distinguish confirmed abuse from statistical anomalies.
