# FBIS Terminology

Order — commercial request created by a customer or business.

Payment Attempt — attempt to obtain payment for an order.

Payment Event — provider-originated event associated with a transaction.

Payment Validation — controlled verification that a payment event satisfies required conditions.

Voucher — coded commercial entitlement changing transaction terms.

Discount — reduction applied to an eligible commercial amount.

Settlement — provider transfer/financial settlement associated with transactions.

Reconciliation — comparison of independent records to identify agreement and exceptions.

Net Revenue — revenue measure after defined deductions; exact accounting treatment requires approved financial definitions.
