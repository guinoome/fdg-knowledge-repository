# Revenue Intelligence

Responsibilities:
- gross sales
- discounts
- refunds
- applicable fees
- net revenue
- revenue by platform
- revenue by product
- revenue by channel
- revenue trends
- revenue forecasting
- campaign revenue impact

Conceptual formula:

Net Revenue = Gross Sales - Discounts - Refunds - Applicable Fees/Adjustments

Accounting treatment must be validated before using the metric as formal financial reporting.
