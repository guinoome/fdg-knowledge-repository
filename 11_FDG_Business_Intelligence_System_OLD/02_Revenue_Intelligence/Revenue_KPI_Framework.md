# Revenue KPI Framework

Core metrics:
- Gross Sales
- Net Revenue
- Average Order Value
- Revenue Growth
- Discount Rate
- Refund Rate
- Revenue per Customer
- Revenue by Product
- Revenue by Platform
- Revenue by Channel

Each KPI requires a controlled definition and source.
