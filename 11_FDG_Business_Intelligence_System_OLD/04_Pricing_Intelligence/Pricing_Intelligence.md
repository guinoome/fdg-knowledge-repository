# Pricing Intelligence

Responsibilities:
- product/service pricing
- price versions
- effective dates
- promotional pricing
- minimum/maximum rules
- channel-specific pricing
- customer-specific pricing where authorized
- historical pricing

Prices must be versioned. Historical orders must preserve the pricing context used at transaction time.
