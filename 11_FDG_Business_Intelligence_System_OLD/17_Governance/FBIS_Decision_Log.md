# FBIS Decision Log

Initial architectural decisions:

FBIS-DEC-001
FBIS owns business and commercial intelligence.

FBIS-DEC-002
Payment intelligence belongs inside FBIS rather than becoming a separate top-level Intelligence System.

FBIS-DEC-003
Payment execution may be implemented as a reusable service, while FBIS remains the commercial authority.

FBIS-DEC-004
Provider abstraction is required to prevent vendor lock-in.

FBIS-DEC-005
Client-side payment success is not authoritative evidence.

FBIS-DEC-006
Zero-value orders may bypass external payment.

FBIS-DEC-007
Commercial rules and KPI definitions require versioning and auditability.
