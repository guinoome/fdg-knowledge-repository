# Hospitality Commercialization and Service Ladder

## Principle

Do not wait for a complete hotel PMS to obtain hospitality-market evidence.

Commercialize existing FDG capability progressively.

## Ladder

```text
Hospitality Assessment / Templates
→ Managed Reporting / Operations Service
→ Department Workspaces
→ Property-Edge Operations
→ Integrated Hospitality Platform
→ Multi-Property / Enterprise
→ White-Label / Partner
```

## Early Sellable Offers

### 1. Hotel Engineering Maintenance & Monthly Reporting
Reuse:
[[14_FDG_Service_Intelligence_System/02_Service_Portfolio/Operations_and_Maintenance_Services/FSvIS-OM-0001 - Maintenance Evidence and Monthly Engineering Reporting Service|Maintenance Reporting Service]].

Hospitality specialization:
- guest-room concerns;
- OOO/OOS;
- room downtime;
- PM evidence;
- recurring room defects;
- plant/utility summary;
- management report.

### 2. Hotel Operations Control Toolkit
Possible professional pack:
- daily hotel operations report;
- arrivals/departures coordination;
- room status control;
- housekeeping board;
- guest request tracker;
- engineering concern tracker;
- shift turnover;
- incident register;
- management attention list.

This can validate demand before software.

### 3. Housekeeping + Engineering Room Readiness Pack
Specific pain:
- dirty/clean/inspected vs engineering restriction;
- room return-to-service;
- lost sellable room nights.

### 4. Hotel Shift Turnover / Daily Operations
Leverage FWIS-style shift/operations architecture.

### 5. Guest Request and Service Recovery Tracker
Small controlled tool/service before broad PMS.

### 6. Hotel PMS Readiness / Process Mapping
Service:
- map current systems;
- pain;
- duplicate entry;
- room state;
- billing;
- OTA;
- F&B;
- maintenance;
- reporting;
- migration readiness.

### 7. OTA / Direct Booking Process Audit
Assess:
- source mix;
- manual re-entry;
- mapping;
- overbooking exposure;
- direct booking process;
- channel costs.

Does not require FDG to be the channel provider.

## Business Platform Relationship

FDG Hospitality may:
- appear in the FDG Business Platform catalog/account/subscription experience; and/or
- operate as a standalone flagship sharing platform services.

The product boundary must be decided by FPJIS/FPIS based on validated target customers.

Do not force hotel workflows into the existing Fuel module or generic business UI.

## Subscription Hypothesis

Possible revenue:
- setup/onboarding;
- per-property subscription;
- room-band pricing;
- module add-ons;
- support/service;
- integration setup;
- data migration;
- engineering/facility service;
- white-label.

No pricing model becomes canonical without real customer validation.

## Differentiation

Avoid competing solely on:
- number of modules;
- number of OTAs;
- “AI-powered” label;
- cheapest price.

FDG target differentiation:
- hospitality + engineering operational intelligence;
- local/offline continuity;
- evidence and audit;
- Philippines capability packs;
- property-edge architecture;
- modular provider replacement;
- premium role experiences;
- connection to construction/turnover/asset lifecycle for new hotels.

## Hotel Lifecycle Advantage

FDG can ultimately connect:
```text
Hotel Design / Construction
→ Testing & Commissioning
→ Turnover / Asset Register
→ Property Operations
→ Maintenance
→ Guest Room Defects
→ Energy / Utilities
→ CAPEX / Renovation
→ New Project
```

This is a major strategic difference from a standalone PMS.

## First-Dollar Rule

Before building a complete hospitality platform, seek evidence from:
- hotel/resort operator interviews;
- current workflow mapping;
- paid assessment;
- paid toolkit;
- maintenance/reporting engagement;
- pilot department workflow.

Use [[20_FPJIS_FDG_Project_Intelligence_System/33_First_Payout_Commercial_Validation_Blueprints/00_Master_Index|First-Payout Commercial Validation]].

## Commercial Validation Metrics

- qualified hotel prospects;
- property type/room count;
- current PMS/tools;
- pain;
- manual hours;
- most expensive failure;
- willingness to pay;
- requested module;
- existing vendor satisfaction;
- offline/outage pain;
- OTA/channel pain;
- engineering/maintenance pain;
- paid pilot;
- delivery burden;
- productization evidence.

## Stop Rule

Do not build the entire platform merely because benchmark vendors have many modules.

Build the smallest connected capability that a real property validates.
