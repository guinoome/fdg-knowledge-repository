# FPJIS-COM-0001 --- FDG Business Platform Commercialization Thesis

**Status:** Candidate commercialization thesis

## Goal

Convert accumulated FDG operational and engineering knowledge into
reusable software/service capability that can generate near-term revenue
while compounding into a larger platform.

## Commercialization Logic

1.  Identify a recurring, measurable business problem.
2.  Capture the workflow and required data in FPJIS/FBIS.
3.  Validate willingness to pay before large implementation.
4.  Build the smallest useful vertical solution on the FDG Common
    Business Core.
5.  Measure outcome: time saved, revenue gained, downtime reduced, cost
    avoided, risk reduced, or visibility improved.
6.  Preserve reusable entities/workflows in the common core.
7.  Package the vertical capability for the next similar customer.
8.  Accumulate cross-customer learning without leaking confidential
    customer data.

## Revenue Paths to Validate

-   implementation/setup
-   subscription/license
-   support/maintenance
-   customization
-   data migration/integration
-   engineering/service package bundled with software
-   vertical capability packs

No revenue model is approved until validated against a real target
market.

## Architectural Guardrail

Do not allow short-term client customization to fork the core
architecture unnecessarily. Use configuration and extension points where
practical.
