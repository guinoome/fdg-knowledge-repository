# FPJIS-OPP-0001 --- Philippine Business Platform Opportunity

**Status:** Opportunity hypothesis; requires market validation

## Hypothesis

Many Philippine SMEs and service/project businesses operate through
disconnected spreadsheets, chat, POS tools, accounting processes,
procurement systems, and manual reporting. A modular platform that
starts with one painful workflow but grows on a common business core may
create recurring commercial value.

## Strategic Advantage

The reusable asset is not one dashboard. It is the governed
business-data foundation that allows FDG to create multiple vertical
solutions without rebuilding the backend every time.

## Potential Vertical Entry Points

-   engineering/service operations
-   hotel/building engineering operations
-   solar EPC/service
-   construction/project delivery
-   retail/trading
-   F&B
-   other Philippine SMEs

These are opportunity categories, not approved products.

## Validation Questions

-   Which workflow creates enough pain to justify payment now?
-   Which customer segment can be reached with the lowest acquisition
    cost?
-   What data already exists in Excel, POS, email, portals, or
    messaging?
-   What minimum integration is needed for a paid MVP?
-   Which features are common core versus vertical-specific?
-   What measurable time, cost, revenue, risk, or compliance outcome is
    improved?
-   Can the solution operate locally/offline and later synchronize to
    web?
-   What recurring service/support/revenue model is defensible?

## Commercial Principle

Validate a narrow outcome before building the full platform. Each
successful vertical MVP should strengthen the shared core rather than
become an isolated codebase.
