# FPJIS-IDEA-0001 --- FDG Business Platform

**Status:** Project idea / candidate\
**Origin:** DizLog + Odoo/OGIS comparative analysis and existing FDG
platform work

## Opportunity Statement

Create an FDG business platform built around a reusable Philippine-ready
business core rather than separate software foundations for every
business. Industry and project capability packs extend the same governed
business data.

## Product Thesis

`Common Business Core + Industry Capability Pack + Workflow + Intelligence + Project/Role Dashboard`

## Potential Initial Capabilities

-   organization, branches, users, roles, approvals
-   customers, suppliers, employees, contractors
-   products, services, materials, assets
-   sales, purchasing, inventory
-   invoicing, payments, expenses
-   projects and project costing
-   accounting interfaces and business state
-   documents and audit trail
-   configurable dashboards
-   intelligence/decision support

## Differentiation

Do not compete only as another POS/ERP. Potential FDG differentiation is
the integration of business operations with engineering, assets,
maintenance, projects, evidence, and decision intelligence.
