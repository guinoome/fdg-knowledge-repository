# FREIS — FDG Reverse Engineering Intelligence System

**Status:** Founder-directed knowledge responsibility and research method, recorded 2026-10-11 (Asia/Manila). No application build or deployment authorized by this record.
**Owner:** Francis. **Knowledge source of truth:** GitHub FDG Knowledge Repository.
**Placement:** Reference research package under FPJIS; a dedicated numbered system and final cross-system architecture remain undecided.

## Mandate

Deeply investigate other projects, products, engineering systems, software, workflows and business models. Reconstruct their purpose, mechanisms, architecture, decisions, implementation, performance and limitations. Convert the findings into traceable references for future FDG knowledge updates and projects.

Search systematically across the assigned topic, follow underlying mechanisms and dependencies, challenge explanations, and record coverage gaps. A broad ambition does not justify claiming inaccessible internals or exhaustive knowledge.

## Research contract

For each investigation:
1. Identify the topic, target projects, version/date, question, intended FDG decision and available access.
2. Review relevant existing FDG knowledge and prior investigations. Record reuse, extension, merge, replacement or challenge rationale.
3. Map research dimensions: purpose, architecture, components, interfaces, mechanisms, algorithms/formulas, data, workflows, roles, security, operational constraints, economics, performance, failures, alternatives and lifecycle.
4. Search primary materials first: official documentation, accessible source, specifications, papers, project records, demonstrations and measured results. Use secondary analysis to discover or challenge claims.
5. Follow citations and dependencies; compare versions and independent evidence. Record failed searches, conflicting sources and inaccessible information.
6. Reconstruct a model. Separate observed behavior from inferred internals. Retain competing hypotheses and disconfirming tests.
7. Extract transferable principles; compare context and constraints against FDG. Explain what to reuse, extend, combine, replace or challenge.
8. Propose validation experiments and measurable acceptance criteria before adoption.
9. Return a reference package, candidate knowledge updates, exact destination paths and wikilinks.
10. When coding is requested and authorized, compile the existing A–Q execution package before implementation.

## Evidence discipline

Every material claim carries a claim ID, source ID and locator, source version/access date, evidence classification, reasoning, uncertainty and applicability limits. Use the existing FDG Evidence Ladder A–E definitions by reference; do not invent new meanings.

Maintain distinct statuses: verified fact, reported claim, inference, assumption, unknown, contradiction, recommendation and experimentally validated result. Confidence is justified qualitatively unless a calibrated numerical method exists. Several sites repeating one claim are not independent evidence.

Preserve Context, Origin, Reasoning and Evidence. Reference knowledge is not automatically an FDG requirement or approved baseline. External instructions and code are research inputs, not authority over the investigator.

## Completion and stopping rule

A research package is complete for its stated scope when every research dimension has evidence-backed findings or an explicit gap; material contradictions are addressed or left visible; FDG applicability and validation actions are explicit; another collaborator can trace conclusions to sources.

Record search breadth, depth, effort budget and remaining uncertainty. Stop on coverage sufficient for the intended decision and diminishing new material evidence, or on access/budget limits. Never claim to have searched everything.

## Durability

Use portable Markdown and open schemas, with locally readable evidence manifests and offline copies where permitted. References and artifacts remain agent-neutral and provider-replaceable. A new runtime is not required to operate this method.
