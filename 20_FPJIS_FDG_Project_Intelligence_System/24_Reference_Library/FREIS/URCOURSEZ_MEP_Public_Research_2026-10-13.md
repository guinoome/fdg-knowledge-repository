## Observed advertisement coverage — concise extraction

C01 [S1, syllabus]: Advertised topics span HVAC, fire protection, plumbing, electrical installation, structured cabling, Revit MEP, CAD, spreadsheets and site defects. HVAC includes equipment connections and balancing; fire coverage includes pumps and operating sequences; plumbing covers supply/drainage equipment. Electrical spans distribution and installation; ICT includes passive/active infrastructure. BIM topics include coordination, schedules, drawing outputs, automation and model-linked progress. Some sections describe planned teaching rather than verified available lessons.

C02 [S1, FAQ/bonuses]: The page advertises 510-plus lectures, completion certificates, prerecorded learning, future additions, BMS reference sheets and CAD files. Its subscription wording conditions access on a valid subscription. These are marketing claims, not audited inventory or competence evidence.

C03 [S2, offer and supplementary sections]: Another English offer advertises lifetime access with installments, CAD practice and prospective fire-alarm, public-address and pool topics. Offer-specific terms must not be merged.

C04 [S3, curriculum]: BMS coverage connects field devices, signal types, input/output points, controllers, DDC panels, network protocols, wiring, supervisory graphics and operating sequences. It mentions VAV, MCC/VFD interfaces and practical equipment examples. The page offers protocol and point-list aids. Its standalone lifetime-access offer is not evidence of the MEP package's terms.

C05 [S4, offer/FAQ]: Editable downloadable method statements are sold with learning access. A marketing scenario links an absent approved method statement to an inspection rejection and payment delay. The FAQ describes purchase, account/dashboard access and support. Exact document titles, contents and suitability were not established.

C06 [S5, enrollment]: A free introductory selection asks for name, email and engineering designation, with account-access information sent by email. This supports an observed introductory-offer pattern; conversion performance is unknown.

C07 [S6, indexed offer]: An annual-access upsell is advertised. Current pricing and eligibility were not transaction-verified and are not adopted as FDG benchmarks.

## Evidence interpretation and unresolved issues

- C01–C07 verify what public advertising says, not engineering correctness or delivery quality.
- Counts, customer totals, salary/career outcomes and testimonials have no independent validation here.
- Related pages and cached renderings differ. Access terms may reflect distinct offers or revisions, not necessarily an error. Preserve URL/date for each claim.
- A completion certificate is not evidence of assessed practical competency, accreditation or professional authorization.
- No paid videos, editable source files, full assessments, internal software, backend architecture, security controls or performance data were inspected.
- Course headings do not establish code editions, jurisdiction, calculation methods or acceptance thresholds.
- The image-based method-statement catalog remains a specific extraction gap; no titles were invented.
- Marketing claims of broad system coverage must not become equipment-control assumptions; control authority is project-specific.
- No course assets are copied into FDG. This record summarizes public claims and develops original FDG proposals.

## Repository decision

The reviewed [[08_FEIS_Engineering_Intelligence_Systems/05_Multidiscipline_Engineering_Intelligence/00_Multidiscipline_Engineering_Intelligence_Master_Index|MDE index]] already prohibits creating a module merely because a course names a profession. [[08_FEIS_Engineering_Intelligence_Systems/05_Multidiscipline_Engineering_Intelligence/FEIS-MDE-0002 - Common Engineering Practice Workflow and Task Pack Standard|The common Task Pack standard]] already covers method statements, inspection, calculation, commissioning and turnover. Extend those capabilities by reference; do not establish a new MEP operating system.
