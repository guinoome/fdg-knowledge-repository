## 1. Mission, authoritative boundary and approval

Test whether FDG can **sell, deliver, obtain acceptance, and economically sustain** one recurring engineering outcome: an evidence-linked monthly maintenance status and action report for a client, without first building another application or deploying AI agents.

This is an execution-specific **extension** of the already prepared 18-part First-Payout Commercial Validation Blueprint Package, not a new canonical service, application, pricing standard, professional credential, sales system, or release authorization.

Technical scope stays with FSvIS/FEIS/FBPOIS and the competent reviewer; this project cannot silently expand it.

## 3. Design choice: validate service first, agents second

The video case study demonstrated a claimed business approach, not FDG production readiness. Pilot 001 tests that approach through a small, **measurable chain of custody**:

Marketing signal → qualified inquiry → agreed assessment → versioned and approved offer → accepted terms → verified payment → accepted client inputs → evidence/review → issued management report → client acceptance/feedback → actual economics → improvement decision.

Existing FBIS commercial stages remain authoritative. Assessment, support, renewal and learning are linked workflows, not competing sales-stage taxonomies.

**Do not block the service pilot on:** production FDG Business Platform account entitlements, new agent runtimes, new dashboards, mobile app construction, automated checkout, or cloud integrations. Manual approved invoicing/collection and secure document delivery may support this first experiment, subject to applicable controls. The Business Platform remains separate TEST/demo functionality until its own gates pass.

## 5. Pilot execution stages and evidence-linked gates

**P001-WP-00 — Readiness and founder-facing offer confirmation (no software).**
- Read the current package, FSvIS-OM-0001, FBIS-BP-003/004, current authority, latest source status and any active work overlap.
- Select one facility/asset group, realistic monthly scope, evidence availability, reviewer capacity, price/campaign validity and explicit exclusions.
- Establish secure private operational record location, backup/restore method and named functional owners.
- Output: readiness decision, accepted offer revision, authorization log and local client intake file. Stop if no professional reviewer, adequate client records or legitimate payment route.

**P001-WP-01 — Demand and qualification.**
- Use already approved outreach/landing messages and direct relationships. Record lead origin, consent/preferences, buyer authority, engineering need, scope readiness, likely recurring value and next action.
- The initial **program-level** 30-day experiment targets from the existing KPI blueprint are 20 qualified prospects, 5 meaningful conversations, 2 formal offers and at least 1 paid pilot; these are planning targets, not forecasts nor guaranteed Pilot 001 results.
- Output: prospect/interaction log and qualified opportunity approval. Marketing does not finalize contracts.

**P001-WP-02 — Controlled offer, contract and verified payment.**
- Review site/asset scope, client dependencies, deliverables, exclusions, corrections, acceptance process, term, tax/payment requirements and actual capacity. Issue a numbered, version-frozen quotation only after human approvals.
- Establish payment method and source, independently verify collection/reference and log confirmation and any reversal. Missing verification → status remains pending; never use browser redirect or screenshot as sole settled-payment authority where stronger verification is available.
- Output: offer revision, client acceptance evidence, controlled payment verification and work authorization. No true payment or client acceptance means no claim of a paying pilot.

**P001-WP-03 — Engineering evidence intake and monthly reporting.**
- Confirm authorized client contact, reporting cutoff and full input inventory (asset register, PM checklist/status, observed readings, photos, work orders/findings where in scope). Preserve source IDs, original media, timestamps, recorder and completeness.
- Normalize statuses as complete/overdue/pending/not evidenced/not applicable only when substantiated; missing and contradictory data remain visible as **Conflict — Review Required**.
- Produce: Maintenance Status Register; Findings and Actions Register; Evidence Index; management-ready Monthly Engineering Report with executive summary, due/overdue, findings, actions, next period and coverage limitations.
- Competent engineer independently checks technical conclusions against original evidence. Unverified observations remain qualified; statutory and professional claims are blocked unless authorized.
- Output: report draft/reviewer evidence/controlled issued revision.

**P001-WP-04 — Client acceptance and measured economics.**
- Document report issue, formal feedback, accepted defects/corrections, revisions and authorized client acceptance; preserve prior issue.
- Record actual founder hours, total delivery/marketing/admin hours, explicit labor cost assumptions, transaction fees, other direct variable expenses, refund/collection status and additional recurring support burden.
- Output: accepted report or unresolved acceptance state, verified financial scorecard, customer value assessment and proposed renewal. A first payment is not a renewal.

**P001-WP-05 — Optional specialist agent SHADOW evaluation.**
- After the human baseline exists, use **approved synthetic/sanitized fixtures** to compare finance, marketing and support outputs with human-reviewed baseline. The use of any real private data or real integrations requires separate FSIS/functional approval.
- CFO: reconstruct direct cost/receipt/contribution estimate and find mismatches; CMO: propose a single measurable acquisition/conversion hypothesis; Customer Service: draft accurate status/resolution response and escalate missing technical evidence.
- Require source ID citations, bounded tool access, reviewable outputs, privacy/no-send controls. Do not grant payment write, campaign publication, customer message send, account manipulation, professional signoff or production data exposure.
- Output: role evaluation evidence; not agent commissioning.

**P001-WP-06 — Pilot decision and knowledge return.**
- Compare actual results to agreed acceptance criteria and baseline; capture both improvements and failures with Context, Origin, Reasoning, Evidence.
- Founder chooses **Continue Assisted / Improve / Reprice-Rescope / Repeat Pilot / Pause / Stop / Propose Automation** with explicit rationale.
- Sanitize and submit reusable learning for FBIS/FSvIS/FEIS/FBPOIS/FPJIS/FWAIS under existing knowledge approval. Software construction and Level 3 autonomy remain separately gated.

Work package numbers are **Pilot 001 subpackages**, not replacements for FPCV-WP-00..12 in [[20_FPJIS_FDG_Project_Intelligence_System/33_First_Payout_Commercial_Validation_Blueprints/11_FMCIS_Work_Package_and_Agent_Execution_Blueprint|FMCIS package map]].

## 7. Decision-ready KPIs, formulas and data-quality guards

| Measure | Definition and evidence | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paid pilot | Count unique accepted engagements with qualifying payment independently verified, net of relevant status adjustments | Do not equate inquiry or proposal with payment |
| Experiment receipts | Sum verified gross receipts in agreed period and currency | Not statutory recognized revenue or net profit |
| Pilot direct contribution estimate | Verified receipts less directly attributable delivery costs and transaction fees (state exclusions) | Explain unrecovered fixed costs/tax and unallocated founder labor |
| Founder revenue leverage | Verified pilot receipts / actually recorded founder hours, when denominator > 0 | Report unavailable with missing hour data |
| Total service effort | Sum delivery, review, revision, admin and support hours; marketing separately attributable | Do not hide founder revisions and support |
| Evidence completeness | Supported required items / required items in frozen contract, where classification is explicit | Client non-supply and not-applicable must be distinguished |
| Report on-time | Accepted/issued report by contract due date / due reports in defined period | Do not declare success if review/issue not completed |
| Revision/rework rate | Number/effort of controlled revised outputs per accepted issue | Preserve revision and defect reasons |
| Client satisfaction | Authorized client survey/acceptance with consent and date | One positive comment is not validated retention |
| Repeat/renewal | Separately verified follow-on commitment/payment in eligible cohort | One first-month client cannot prove recurring retention |
| Critical control incidents | Unauthorized communications/finance/technical decisions, loss, disclosure, fraudulent payment states | Target zero; any occurrence escalates and stops affected workflow |

CFO analyses must reconcile receipt vs invoice vs actual service cost and disclose uncertainty. CMO metrics must declare denominator and attribution window. Support must distinguish closed from customer-accepted resolution. Maintain evidence-based exceptions rather than inventing measurements.

**Pilot success is multi-dimensional:** actual verified collection, an engineering-reviewed report accepted by the client, traceable evidence and missing-data disclosures, economics and time records complete enough to calculate the margin, genuine client-value feedback, and no unresolved critical authority/privacy incident. A failed target is a valid learning outcome, not permission to fabricate evidence.

## 9. Risks and response

- **Client evidence incomplete:** flag coverage gap, request specific records, adjust scope or decline; do not infer PM completion.
- **Professional liability:** no seal/certification/engineering signoff outside assigned competent professional and contractual authority.
- **Underpricing:** record hours and overhead exclusions; price renegotiation requires new versioned offer and prospect agreement.
- **Founder overload:** track hidden coordination, rework and support; system success is not gross receipts alone.
- **Privacy and confidentiality:** scope access, private encrypted/controlled storage where applicable, consent, retention and no public repo/client identifiers.
- **Agent overreach:** block calls to payments, campaign publishing, client messaging and technical approvals unless separately provisioned with tested authority.
- **Premature app build:** repeated need ≠ authorization; trigger FPJIS design gates only after validated bottleneck.
- **Unsupported ROI/retention:** mark data not yet available; no predictive case study from one buyer.

## 10. Pilot decision and stop/scale rules

**Proceed with manual/assisted outreach/delivery only when:** approved offer, qualified reviewer capacity, private record protection, acceptable terms, and legitimate verifier are available. A client-specific engagement remains contingent on acceptance.

**Hold/stop** if: no competent technical reviewer, unresolved scope/contract disagreement, inadequate evidence for an accurate report, uncontrolled finance collection, critical privacy incident, unsafe technical claim, or unacceptable capacity/cost exposure.

**Repeat** at the same bounded scope if value/quality is promising but margin or renewal evidence is unclear. **Improve/Reprice** when controllable burden undermines contribution. **Productize/Automate** only after repeated paying demand, stable process, tested security/quality, and a separately approved FPJIS work package with deployment/agent authority independently authorized.
