# Current Market Signal Register — 2026-10-07

## Use

This is a dated research snapshot supporting the commercial-validation program.

It is **not** permanent market truth and must not auto-update offer scope, pricing or build priorities.

## Signal 01 — Philippine construction activity remains substantial but mixed

**Source quality:** A — Philippine Statistics Authority.

PSA reported 17,081 constructions from approved building permits in June 2026, down 0.3% year over year. Non-residential constructions numbered 3,352 and increased 4.6% year over year. Total construction value reached PHP 74.34 billion; non-residential value reached PHP 43.80 billion and increased 56.4% year over year.

Source:
https://psa.gov.ph/content/construction-statistics-approved-building-permits-june-2026

Latest-releases page observed on 2026-10-07 also lists July 2026 at 15,890 constructions, down 1.2% year over year.

Source:
https://psa.gov.ph/statistics/construction/pcs/index

**Interpretation:** There is a large addressable construction workflow base, but the signal is not a simple universal growth story. Target practical workflow pain rather than macro “boom” messaging.

## Signal 02 — Central Visayas had strong May 2026 permit activity

**Source quality:** A — PSA Central Visayas.

May 2026:
- 1,389 approved construction projects;
- +27.9% year over year;
- PHP 4.313 billion total value;
- 201 non-residential permits;
- Cebu Province led regional non-residential counts with 111;
- City of Cebu reported 21 non-residential permits.

Source:
https://rsso07.psa.gov.ph/content/construction-statistics-approved-building-permits-central-visayas-may-2026-preliminary

**Interpretation:** Supports Visayas-first prospecting and local professional-pack positioning. It does not prove demand for templates or FDG services.

## Signal 03 — Construction template category is crowded

**Source quality:** C — marketplace.

Notion Construction Marketplace displayed 183 templates when observed, including daily logs, punch lists, drawing revision registers, project trackers and paid specialized systems.

Source:
https://www.notion.com/templates/category/construction

**Interpretation:** Generic construction templates are commoditized. FDG needs lifecycle integration, Philippines-first semantics, engineering evidence and role-specific workflows.

## Signal 04 — Professional construction bundles support higher price points

**Source quality:** C — marketplace/product pages.

Observed examples:
- Construction Management Excel Toolkit — USD 49.90;
- ExcelLog Construction Management Templates — USD 49.99;
- commercial/institutional construction PM toolkit — USD 99;
- broader PM bundle — USD 149.99.

Sources:
https://mohddeeb.gumroad.com/l/construction-management-excel-toolkit
https://excelog.gumroad.com/l/construction-management
https://meridianbuild.gumroad.com/l/tvsdf
https://excelog.gumroad.com/l/project-management-bundle

**Interpretation:** A PHP 4,999 flagship pack can be plausible if it is a professional system rather than a generic spreadsheet bundle. This is competitor pricing evidence, not willingness-to-pay evidence for FDG.

## Signal 05 — Low-price mega bundles create price pressure

**Source quality:** C.

A “49+ Excel Templates” construction/manufacturing/oil-and-gas bundle was observed at USD 10+ promotional pricing.

Source:
https://pmschedule.gumroad.com/l/template

**Interpretation:** Competing on template count is weak. FDG should compete on governed workflow capability and professional usefulness.

## Signal 06 — Philippine hard facility management has recurring demand characteristics

**Source quality:** B — industry research.

A 2026 Philippines integrated FM market report states hard FM is forecast to grow faster than soft FM and associates demand with planned maintenance, engineering support and life-safety compliance. It also cites MEP/facility talent shortages as a provider risk and Metro Cebu among industrial clusters supporting technical-upkeep demand.

Source:
https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/philippines-integrated-facility-management-market

**Interpretation:** Supports the Maintenance Evidence + Monthly Engineering Reporting hypothesis, especially as an outsourced visibility/evidence layer. Must be validated with real prospects.
