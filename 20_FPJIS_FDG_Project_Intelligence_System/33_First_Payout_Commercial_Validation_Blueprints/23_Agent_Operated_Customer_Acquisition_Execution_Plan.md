# FPJIS-FPCV-2300 — FDG Agent-Operated Customer Acquisition Execution Plan

> Knowledge path: [[20_FPJIS_FDG_Project_Intelligence_System/33_First_Payout_Commercial_Validation_Blueprints/00_Master_Index|FPCV Master Index]] → this execution plan.

## 1. Founder direction and commercial reset

FDG has already invested substantial effort in engineering platforms, product architecture, workflows, reusable capabilities and operating knowledge. The immediate constraint is no longer a shortage of frameworks or another platform build. The priority is converting existing capability into verified customer payments and repeatable customer acquisition.

The execution direction is:

1. Sell what FDG can already deliver.
2. Start with one offer and one customer segment rather than launching the entire portfolio at once.
3. Use available AI agents and existing OpenAI and Anthropic subscriptions to execute research, content, campaign preparation, lead intelligence and performance analysis.
4. Keep expensive local AI hardware as a parallel future capability track. Do not purchase high-cost hardware before customer revenue or a demonstrated operational bottleneck justifies it.
5. Start Pilot 001 with a **PHP 499 launch price for the first 10 paying customers**.
6. Increase pricing progressively only after real delivery, support burden, customer response, conversion and unit economics have been observed. Review the price after a few months or an earlier evidence-based trigger; no future price is pre-authorized here.
7. Use social media and paid advertising as acquisition channels where justified. AI agents reduce execution effort; they do not make media spend free.
8. Preserve founder control over financial commitments, public campaign activation, external communications and production changes.

This document is an additive execution layer over existing FBIS, FSvIS, FPJIS, FWAIS, FPIS and Pilot 001 authorities. It does not create a duplicate CRM, marketing platform, agent framework, sales funnel or service definition.

## 2. Commercial priority and offer

### 2.1 First campaign: Pilot 001

**Offer:** FDG Engineering Maintenance Evidence & Monthly Engineering Reporting Service.

**Target buyer hypothesis:** facility/property owners and operators, hotel/resort engineering departments, commercial building operators, facility managers and maintenance service providers with recurring monthly maintenance evidence and reporting work.

**Buyer problem to validate:** maintenance checklists, readings, photographs, work history, open defects and action ownership are fragmented across people and channels. Management may lack a reliable, traceable monthly view of completed work, overdue items, unresolved findings and next actions.

**Bounded purchased outcome:** one agreed monthly, evidence-indexed maintenance status and engineering report, with findings/actions, responsible parties, next-period priorities and explicit coverage limitations. Final service scope, professional review, exclusions and acceptance remain governed by FSvIS-OM-0001 and the Pilot 001 charter.

**Initial launch offer:** PHP 499 for each of the first 10 qualifying paying customers. The offer is a controlled launch price, not proof of a permanent list price or an established percentage discount.

**Pricing controls:**
- Count only distinct customers with independently verified qualifying payment; inquiries, likes, verbal commitments, unpaid orders and agent-generated leads do not consume a slot.
- Maintain one global counter for the defined launch offer/version; do not reset it per channel, campaign, agent or salesperson.
- State the exact scope, reporting period, client inputs, exclusions, turnaround, review, acceptance, payment and remedy terms before payment.
- Do not invent a higher reference/list price to advertise a percentage discount. If a genuine standard price is approved and documented later, a truthful percentage comparison may be calculated from it.
- Do not promise unlimited assets, sites, visits, repairs, CMMS migration, statutory certification, professional seal, 24/7 monitoring or other excluded work for PHP 499.
- If the service cannot be delivered safely and economically at this price, stop taking additional launch orders and return to the founder for a scope/price decision. Do not silently change terms for an accepted customer.
- After the first 10 qualifying payments, the next price remains **to be decided**. A later increase should be staged and supported by observed costs, delivery time, support burden, conversion and customer value. No automatic increase is authorized.

### 2.2 Other offers remain queued

FDG Construction Management Professional Toolkit and other products/services remain in the existing portfolio. Do not dilute the first campaign by simultaneously promoting every FDG capability. A second campaign requires a separate offer record, buyer, creative, conversion goal and commercial measurement.

## 3. Agent operating model

Nex coordinates bounded specialist work packages. Agents are execution resources, not independent commercial authorities. Use existing subscriptions and already available connected capabilities first; do not add paid subscriptions or infrastructure without a separate business case and approval.

| Agent/function | Work to execute | Required output / evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Market Intelligence | Identify target organizations and buyer roles; research visible operational signals, public contact routes, competitor positioning and objections | Dated prospect candidates, source links, fit rationale, confidence and unknowns | No fabricated prospect facts, private-data harvesting or unsolicited bulk contact |
| Offer & Positioning Analyst | Convert FSvIS-OM-0001 into buyer-specific value proposition, scope summary, objection handling and claim bank | Versioned offer brief mapped to service authority | Cannot change service scope, price or professional claims |
| Creative / Content Agent | Produce platform-specific post copy, short-video scripts, storyboard, graphics briefs, captions and variants | Editable content pack linked to approved source claims and campaign ID | No fake testimonials, client logos, performance claims or synthetic case studies presented as real |
| Campaign Operations Agent | Prepare campaign structure, audience hypotheses, creative variants, tracking plan and draft configuration | Review-ready campaign package and budget forecast | No paid activation, budget change or public publishing without founder approval |
| Lead Intelligence / Sales Support | Classify inbound inquiries, summarize needs, identify missing qualification fields and draft replies/follow-ups | Lead record, evidence-backed qualification, draft response and next action | No external sending, price negotiation, binding quotation or commitment without delegated authority |
| Performance Analyst | Reconcile reach/clicks/messages with qualified leads, quotes, verified payments, cost and contribution | Cohort scorecard, denominators, attribution limits, keep/change/stop recommendation | No invented attribution, ROAS, CAC or conversion |
| Technical Reviewer | Validate engineering statements, sample report outputs, data limitations and professional boundaries | Signed/recorded review decision and issue list | Only a competent authorized human may approve technical conclusions or professional sign-off |
| FAIS / independent reviewer | Sample-check evidence, pricing counter, privacy, agent actions, campaign claims and calculations | Exception log and audit result | Independent assurance; cannot substitute for commercial or engineering owner |

### 3.1 Agent execution levels

- **Research and internal drafting:** may proceed within available authorized tools and public information.
- **Internal analysis and campaign preparation:** may proceed; preserve source references and versions.
- **External message, post or campaign publication:** draft/preparation only until explicit authorization for the specific content, channel and account.
- **Paid advertising:** draft budget and setup only until founder approves amount, campaign, duration, account and spend ceiling.
- **Price/offer changes, contracts, invoices, refunds, payment collection or commitments:** human approval under FBIS/finance/legal authority.
- **Engineering conclusions, service acceptance and professional certification:** competent human reviewer only.
- **Production deployment, account permission grants, autonomous sending or agent commissioning:** separate security, authority and release gates.

A connected Meta Ads Manager or other advertising integration may be used only after actual account connection, permissions, available functions and the intended action are verified. Tool availability is not evidence of account authorization.

### Phase 1 — Market intelligence and prospect cohort

Create a focused, evidence-backed list of organizations likely to have the problem. Prioritize reachable Philippine prospects and buyer roles with operational responsibility or budget authority.

For each candidate record:
- organization and public source;
- relevant facility/sector and geographic fit;
- likely buyer role and decision-maker unknowns;
- observable trigger or operational signal;
- likely maintenance/reporting pain and evidence level;
- public business contact route, where available;
- source date, confidence and next research action.

Do not infer that an organization has a problem solely because it operates a building. Mark hypotheses as hypotheses. Do not scrape restricted personal data or generate fake conversations.

### Phase 2 — Offer proof and content production

Create a small, proof-led content set rather than a large volume of generic posts. Reuse FBIS-BP-002 archetypes:
- problem → diagnosis → solution;
- before/after workflow demonstration;
- checklist/common documentation gaps;
- short product/service walkthrough;
- engineer commentary;
- evidence-based case example only when an actual authorized case exists.

Initial content should explain the practical difference between fragmented maintenance evidence and a traceable monthly management report. Use an approved sample or clearly labelled illustrative dataset. Never present synthetic data as an FDG customer result.

Required campaign assets:
1. One concise core offer statement.
2. One service scope and exclusions summary.
3. One landing/message destination with a clear inquiry action.
4. Three to five launch creative variants for controlled testing.
5. One short-video script/storyboard suitable for an original Reel.
6. One FAQ / objection response sheet.
7. One qualification checklist and draft response set.
8. Tracking labels for campaign, channel, creative revision and lead source.

### Phase 3 — Campaign preparation and activation gate

Prepare organic publishing and paid campaign options separately. Organic distribution may be used as an approved owned-channel activity; publication still requires the applicable account/content approval. Paid campaign setup must include:
- campaign objective and conversion event;
- target geography and buyer hypothesis;
- audience exclusions and placement rationale;
- creative version and claim references;
- destination and tracking parameters;
- total budget, daily cap, duration and stop conditions;
- expected measurement method and known attribution limits.

**No budget is pre-approved by this plan.** Do not activate paid media, set a live spend ceiling, boost a post or incur any financial charge until Francis approves the specific spend and campaign. If no budget is approved, continue with research, creative preparation, approved organic content and direct inbound handling only.

### Phase 4 — Lead qualification and commercial handoff

Use the existing FBIS funnel and records. Capture:
- source/campaign/creative;
- organization and buyer role;
- facility type and number/site scope;
- current monthly evidence/reporting workflow;
- records available and missing;
- desired reporting outcome and timing;
- decision-maker, procurement path and budget status;
- consent/contact preference;
- qualification status, objection, next action and owner.

A message or click is not a qualified lead. A qualified lead is not a quote. A quote is not an order. An order is not a verified payment. Payment is not delivery or acceptance.

Agents may prepare a draft reply or quote inputs. Only an authorized human approves binding price, scope, contract terms and customer commitments.

### Phase 5 — Delivery and commercial learning

For each qualifying paid customer, use the existing Pilot 001 charter and private operator record pack. Deliver only the contracted scope. Record source evidence, technical review, issued report revision, client acceptance, corrections, actual labor, direct expenses, fees, support and refund/reversal status.

Return sanitized learning to FBIS, FSvIS, FPJIS and FWAIS. Do not put client identities, private documents, personal contact details or confidential facility information in this public repository.

## 5. Measurement system and decision gates

Retain the existing 30-day program targets from FPJIS-FPCV-13:
- 20 qualified prospects;
- 5 meaningful conversations;
- 2 formal offers;
- at least 1 independently verified paid pilot.

These are targets, not achieved results or forecasts. The first-ten paying-customer launch objective is a commercial cohort target, not a claim that 10 customers have been acquired.

Measure at minimum:
- prospects researched / prospects qualified;
- outreach authorized / actually sent;
- replies / meaningful conversations;
- qualified leads / leads assessed;
- offers issued / accepted;
- independently verified paying customers;
- collected receipts and reversals;
- campaign spend and attributable sales effort;
- cost per qualified lead and CAC only when denominators exist;
- delivery hours, founder/reviewer hours, direct costs and fees;
- contribution before and after acquisition, with exclusions;
- report issue and client acceptance;
- customer feedback, repeat purchase and renewal;
- opt-outs, complaints, misleading-claim incidents and privacy/security events.

**Core formulas and controls:**
- CAC = attributable acquisition and prospecting expense / new verified paying customers in the same cohort. If no paying customers, CAC is undefined.
- Quote-to-paid = verified new paying customers / valid priced offers in the defined cohort.
- Contribution must disclose excluded overhead, tax, unpaid founder time and support/rework assumptions.
- Views, reach, clicks, likes and messages are diagnostic activity metrics, not revenue.
- Do not report ROAS or campaign ROI without defensible attribution and a comparable contribution basis.

**Decision rhythm:**
- Daily: review real inbound inquiries, qualification, response backlog, complaints and opt-outs.
- Twice weekly: review creative/message evidence and one controlled acquisition hypothesis.
- Weekly: reconcile prospect → conversation → offer → verified payment, delivery capacity, unit economics and source quality.
- At first 10 qualifying payments or the predeclared review date: assess price, scope, buyer segment, conversion and delivery economics before any increase.
- Pause paid activity if spend exceeds approval, lead quality collapses, complaints/misunderstanding rise, the offer cannot be delivered, critical controls fail or evidence is unreliable.

## 6. Hardware and model infrastructure decision

The high-memory local AI hardware track (including Ryzen AI Halo-class systems) is retained as a **future parallel capability investigation**, not an immediate purchase.

Current operating baseline:
- Existing OpenAI subscription.
- Existing Anthropic subscription.
- Existing FDG knowledge repository and available agent/tool integrations, subject to verified connection and permissions.

Do not assume the subscriptions include unlimited usage, API credits, agent runtimes or external account permissions. Measure actual cost, context limits, task quality, rate limits and operator time. Use current hosted models where economical. Reassess local hardware only when one or more of these is evidenced:
- recurring inference cost materially erodes contribution;
- privacy or offline requirements cannot be met acceptably by the current stack;
- workload volume and utilization justify capital cost;
- local execution provides a measurable reliability or latency advantage;
- customer revenue or approved investment covers the purchase and operating cost.

No hardware purchase, financing or additional subscription is authorized by this plan.

## 7. Risk, truth and governance controls

- No fabricated testimonials, logos, customer counts, case studies, savings, ROI, certifications or product capabilities.
- No misleading “percentage off” calculated from an invented reference price.
- No false scarcity: first-ten count must reconcile to verified qualifying payments.
- No unauthorized use of customer/site photos, documents, brands or identifying information.
- No client data in public GitHub or unapproved model contexts.
- No automated mass messaging, spam, deceptive engagement, fake comments or platform-policy evasion.
- No technical conclusion from incomplete or synthetic evidence represented as a real site finding.
- No price or scope negotiation by agents without delegated authority.
- No external campaign, post, message, financial transaction or production change without the relevant approval.
- Preserve source links, creative versions, approvals, publication receipts and metric definitions.
- Any agent action outside its authority is a critical exception: stop the affected workflow, preserve logs and escalate.
