# KPI, Evidence and Learning Blueprint

## Objective

Measure whether FDG is creating real commercial capability, not activity theater.

## Funnel KPIs

Per offer:
- qualified prospects;
- meaningful conversations;
- offers/proposals;
- verified payments;
- delivered pilots;
- accepted pilots;
- repeat customers;
- monthly renewals.

## Time KPIs

- days to first meaningful conversation;
- days to first proposal;
- days to first verified payment;
- proposal turnaround;
- delivery turnaround;
- customer response delay;
- founder hours per transaction;
- total human hours.

## Economics

### Revenue Leverage
```text
Revenue Leverage = Verified Experiment Revenue / Founder Hours
```

### Contribution Estimate
```text
Contribution = Verified Receipts - Direct Delivery Cost - Direct Transaction Fees
```

State exclusions explicitly.

### Acquisition Effort
Track:
- outreach hours;
- channel cost;
- tools;
- paid ads if any.

## Quality KPIs

- revision count;
- missing-input count;
- error count;
- rejected deliverables;
- refund requests;
- unresolved findings;
- evidence completeness;
- acceptance lead time.

## Product KPIs

For digital packs:
- purchases;
- refunds;
- support requests;
- most-used components;
- unused components;
- requested additions;
- buyer role;
- version adoption.

Do not instrument invasive analytics merely to collect metrics.

## Market Intelligence KPIs

- new signals captured;
- A/B quality signals;
- stale signals;
- signals influencing a test;
- false/noise signal rate;
- competitor price movement;
- new segment/category discovered.

## Decision KPIs

- opportunities advanced;
- paused;
- stopped;
- automated;
- productized;
- build requests rejected due to weak evidence.

A healthy system should sometimes recommend **do not build**.

## Evidence Ladder

### Level A
Primary/official or direct transaction/operational evidence.

### Level B
Strong secondary evidence.

### Level C
Marketplace/reputable observed signal.

### Level D
Community/anecdotal.

### Level E
Hypothesis/untested inference.

The project UI should display evidence level beside consequential decisions.

## Learning Record

A lesson contains:
- context;
- origin;
- reasoning;
- evidence;
- applicability;
- limitations;
- confidence;
- reviewer;
- proposed destination.

## Promotion Rule

```text
Operational Observation
→ Candidate Lesson
→ Review
→ Reusable Principle
→ Canonical Knowledge Update
```

Do not promote:
- one buyer preference as universal;
- one competitor feature as requirement;
- one viral post as demand;
- one successful pilot as scalable business.

## 30-Day Validation Targets

Initial program targets:
- 20 qualified prospects;
- 5 meaningful conversations;
- 2 formal offers;
- at least 1 paid pilot.

These are experiment targets, not forecasts.

## Decision Review

At 30 days or after first five paid transactions, review:
- what sold;
- to whom;
- why;
- margin;
- founder dependency;
- repeat potential;
- automation opportunity;
- productization eligibility;
- what should stop.

## Audit

FAIS should independently test:
- payment evidence;
- KPI formula;
- unsupported marketing claim;
- price/promo integrity;
- founder-time completeness;
- whether a “repeatable” claim has enough repetitions.
