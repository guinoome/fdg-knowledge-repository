# FDG Platform Intelligence System (FPIS)

> **Single-source entry point for the FDG Platform Intelligence System.**

## Mission

FPIS understands the complete state, lifecycle, value, usage, experience, architecture, and evolution of FDG platforms. It identifies evidence-based opportunities to improve and innovate the platforms.

## Architectural Position

FPIS is a **parallel Intelligence System** within the FDG Ecosystem.

It does not replace FDG CORE Intelligence.

- **FDG CORE Intelligence** connects and coordinates the FDG Ecosystem, intelligence systems, and governance. See [[10_FDG_CORE_Intelligence/FDG-CORE-STD-001_CORE_INTELLIGENCE_ARCHITECTURE_STANDARD|FDG CORE Intelligence Architecture Standard]].
- **FPIS** understands and improves the platforms themselves.
- Domain Intelligence Systems remain responsible for their own domains.

## Core Principle

> **FPIS continuously understands the condition, value, architecture, experience, lifecycle, and evolution of FDG platforms and provides evidence for their deliberate improvement.**

## Current Boundary

FPIS may analyze business, usage, subscription, financial, and user evidence **only to understand platform performance and evolution**.

FPIS does not become the system of record for:
- billing
- payment processing
- HR/personnel management
- legal records
- operational transactions
- domain-specific engineering calculations

Those remain with their responsible systems.

## Lifecycle

Concept → Architecture → Prototype → Local Validation → Integration → Deployment → Operation → Evolution → Retirement

## Working Rule

> **Every FPIS record should reduce platform ambiguity, improve a decision, or support platform evolution.**
