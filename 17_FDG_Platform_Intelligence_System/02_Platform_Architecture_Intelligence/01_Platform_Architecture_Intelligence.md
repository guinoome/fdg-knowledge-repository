# Platform Architecture Intelligence

FPIS maintains the structural intelligence of each platform.

## Understand

- architecture
- modules
- components
- interfaces
- data flows
- dependencies
- technologies
- environments
- integrations
- technical constraints
- architectural decisions

## Questions

- What exists?
- Why does it exist?
- What depends on it?
- What depends on another platform?
- Where is duplication occurring?
- Where is architecture becoming fragile?
- What should be standardized?

## Output

Architecture maps, dependency intelligence, identified risks, and evidence-based architecture recommendations.
