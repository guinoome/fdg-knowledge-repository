# FDG Filmmaking Intelligence — Master Index

> [[FDG Ecosystem|FDG Ecosystem]] → [[17_FDG_Platform_Intelligence_System/00_FPI_Home|FPIS]] → [[17_FDG_Platform_Intelligence_System/07_Platform_Experience_Design_Intelligence/00_Index|Platform Experience & Design Intelligence]] → this document.

## 1. Purpose

FDG Filmmaking Intelligence (FFI) is the domain knowledge and decision layer for planning, researching, designing, producing, validating, and improving films and video content across FDG.

It converts external research, creative methods, production tools, source material, and measured outcomes into reusable FDG production capability. Its scope includes commercial/product films, short-form social video, engineering explainers, educational lessons, product demonstrations, campaign assets, and internal communication.

**This is a domain master under FPIS, not a new top-level intelligence system.** It extends the existing FDG Creative Studio and does not duplicate FEXIS, FBIS, FWAIS, FEIS, or other authorities.

## 3. What this master owns

- Filmmaking research synthesis and source evaluation.
- Audience-to-story translation, narrative design and script structure.
- Hooks, retention, pacing, payoff, loop design and ethical curiosity.
- Visual storytelling, shot planning, storyboards and shot lists.
- Production design: visual bible, characters/objects, location, continuity, camera, lighting, composition and motion.
- Editing grammar: cuts, transitions, rhythm, sound design, narration, captions and accessibility.
- Short-form, long-form, product-film, documentary, tutorial and engineering-explainer formats.
- Production tool and provider evaluation, adapter decisions and reproducible workflows.
- Quality rubrics, creative review and post-publication learning.
- Reusable templates, prompts, production checklists and validated case studies.

## 4. What it does not own

- Public trend/topic collection infrastructure or general external discoverability: FEXIS remains the external intelligence source where applicable.
- Campaign strategy, market positioning, pricing, lead qualification or revenue records: FBIS / Marketing & Growth.
- Public posting, scheduled distribution or automated replies: FWAIS under approval rules.
- Engineering truth, calculations, regulated technical claims or test evidence: FEIS / responsible engineering authority.
- Rights clearance, privacy/legal conclusions or security policy: FLIS / FSIS.
- The creation interface or provider-specific product: FDG Creative Studio and its replaceable adapters.

## 5. Core production lifecycle

```text
Business / Learning / Communication Objective
→ Audience and Distribution Context
→ Research and Evidence Intake
→ Creative Brief
→ Story / Script
→ Visual Bible and Storyboard
→ Shot / Scene Production
→ Edit / Sound / Captions
→ Creative and Domain QA
→ Rights / Privacy / Approval Gates
→ Publish or Deliver
→ Measure Outcomes
→ Review and Encode Learning
```

Each stage must have an owner, inputs, output artifact, acceptance criteria, status, and traceable source references.

## 6. Evidence and truth classes

All factual or consequential claims must be classified:

- **VERIFIED_FACT** — supported by an authoritative source or direct observation.
- **CALCULATED** — produced by a reproducible calculation with inputs and method.
- **SIMULATED** — generated by a documented model with assumptions and validation limits.
- **ILLUSTRATIVE** — visual explanation, not evidence of a real event or measured result.
- **OPINION / CREATIVE_DIRECTION** — editorial judgment, not objective fact.
- **UNVERIFIED** — pending source or domain review; must not be presented as established fact.

Generative video is not a source of physical law, engineering topology, product functionality, measured results, or proof of an actual site condition.

## 10. Governance and maturity

This master is a repository knowledge structure, not proof of an operational intelligence agent or automated YouTube monitor.

Use maturity labels per capability:
- **DOCUMENTED** — written and linked.
- **SOURCE_REVIEWED** — external source checked and findings recorded.
- **PROTOTYPED** — a bounded workflow/tool has been tried.
- **RUNTIME_VERIFIED** — actual execution and logs checked in a named environment.
- **PILOT_ACCEPTED** — defined acceptance criteria passed with reviewer sign-off.
- **OPERATIONAL** — repeatable owner, monitoring, recovery and governance exist.

No capability advances by implication. New methods are additive; conflicting or superseded methods require an explicit decision record.
