# Platform Experience & Design Intelligence

## Purpose

FPIS designs, manages, evaluates, and evolves the user-facing experience of FDG platforms.

## Scope

- dashboard design
- UI/UX patterns
- themes
- layouts
- components
- navigation
- data visualization
- responsive behavior
- accessibility
- personalization
- role-based starting experiences

## Dashboard Intelligence

FPIS can provide reusable design systems and intelligent defaults for:

- Executive dashboards
- Manager dashboards
- Supervisor dashboards
- Technician dashboards
- Auditor dashboards
- Customer dashboards
- Other platform-specific roles

## Theme Intelligence

A platform may expose configurable themes such as:

- FDG Professional
- FDG Engineering
- FDG Executive
- FDG Operations
- FDG Minimal
- Dark
- Custom

Theme configuration may include:

- colors
- typography
- density
- cards
- navigation
- widgets
- backgrounds
- component appearance

## Personalization Principle

FPIS provides intelligent defaults. The user retains control over the final experience.

## Experience Learning Loop

Design → Use → Observe → Analyze → Improve → Validate → Learn

## Reuse

FDG should maintain reusable design patterns rather than independently redesigning every platform.

## Active implementation mandate

This architecture is governed by [[17_FDG_Platform_Intelligence_System/07_Platform_Experience_Design_Intelligence/03_FDG_Premium_Experience_Design_and_Implementation_Mandate|FDG Premium Experience Design & Implementation Mandate]]. It extends this earlier note by requiring every major platform to combine domain-native product experience, honest client-facing value, real workflow, mobile-first execution, and measurable business value. Existing themes and dashboard patterns remain reusable primitives, not a sufficient product identity by themselves.
