## 1–3. Purpose, one-sentence standard and scope

Execute dashboard remastering, module/hero/command redesign, immersive overlays, mobile-first upgrades and client-magnet enhancement using the environment as interface, with glass data, contextual hotspots and readable operational panels. Use on home/module/site/branch/plant/command/network overviews and product previews. Do not force this treatment on long tables, settings, administrative forms, invoice-only pages, dense accounting or data-entry-only workflows.

## 4. First decision

Identify what exists, what works, what looks generic, what can be remastered safely, and what stays utility-first. Remaster before rebuilding.

## 5–7. Output and visual formula

Hero environment + glass/transparent data layer + contextual hotspots + summary metrics + progressive drill-down. Maintain visible context, readable metrics, restrained depth, meaningful status, compact detail and a clear focal area. Use domain scenes, translucent panels, badges/mini charts only with evidence, subtle connectors and compact actions. Tap opens a drawer, modal, side panel or mobile bottom sheet.

## 8. Reject

Generic SaaS/ERP/admin layouts; hero banner added to a template; random KPI wall; opaque blocks hiding the scene; unrelated flat cards; sci-fi clutter; AI slop; workflow-free concept art; desktop merely shrunk onto mobile.

## 9. Remaster sequence

1. Preserve business/workflow/data meaning, valid navigation and useful modules.
2. Identify the domain environment.
3. Recompose as environment-first overview, metrics, hotspots and secondary actions.
4. Layer summary → detail on demand → deeper workflow.
5. Adapt phone overlays, tap targets and bottom sheets while retaining scene visibility.
6. Review screenshots: would this help sell the product?

## 10–12. Mobile, patterns and components

Mobile is primary: retain hero, prioritize 3–6 facts, use compact glass summaries, tappable zones, bottom-sheet details, thumb-friendly actions and optional useful subview swipes. Do not hide the hero, bury essentials in long lists, crowd floating panels or require hover.

Main overview: large scene, metrics, 3–6 contextual overlays, status strip, one main CTA/mode switch. Site: localized scene/hotspots, today's results, status and branch metrics. Network: connected map/environments, branch states, movements and exceptions. Engineering: equipment callouts, readings, controller/checklist/findings. Preview: restrained overlays, outcome story and honest demonstration CTA.

Reuse scene, glass panel, metric pill, hotspot, connector, mini trend, side rail, bottom sheet/detail drawer, toolbar, mode switch and mobile-summary primitives as needed. Do not create all primitives preemptively.

## 13–16. Semantics, glass, hotspots and hierarchy

Green normal/verified; amber attention/pending; red failure/critical; blue measured/information; gray neutral/reference. Brand accents never override meaning.

Use soft transparency, modest backdrop blur, rounded corners, subtle borders/shadow and readable spacing. Avoid over-neon, reflections, unreadable blur and excessive layers.

Hotspots point to scene elements, carry short meaningful labels plus one or two core metrics, and expand on tap. No random floating, heavy overlap, hero obstruction or duplicated side-panel content.

Primary facts: performance/status/top alert/key asset/throughput. Secondary: trends/supporting metrics/zones/context. Tertiary: reports/history/settings/logs/tables/forms.

## 18–20. Utility and acceptance

Engineering overview explains; utility screens act; evidence, measurement meaning and traceability remain unambiguous. Business overview attracts and explains while execution screens support actual work.

Minimum gate: distinctive FDG identity, visible environment, restrained glass, integrated truthful data, readability, immersive mobile, client-attractive screenshot, preserved workflows, no decorative clutter.

## 21–22. Review and agent brief

Ask: what environment, still visible, contextual overlays, excessive opacity, obstructed scene, generic-card regression, premium phone, clear next action, rapid client understanding, meaningful differentiation?

Execution brief: remaster overview into domain environment with transparent readable data and contextual hotspots; preserve working workflows; keep mobile primary; reject generic card walls and invented live claims.

## 23–25. Start, finish and handover

Start: read standard, identify module/screen, read latest handover, clarify mandate if needed, inspect UI, list preservation scope, identify scene, sketch overlays, build, review.

Finish: test workflow, phone and desktop; capture and compare screenshots; record gaps; update notes and continuation handover.

Handover: remastered surface, preserved behavior, asset, components, mobile behavior, screenshot assessment, unresolved issues, exact next actions and whether original canonical mandate review is still needed.

## 26–28. Repository and governing shortcut

Link this companion to the governing mandate. Preserve prior notes and document evolution. Hero environment first, glass intelligence second, real workflow preserved, mobile-first and client-ready presentation. The environment is the hero; data is the glass intelligence layer.
