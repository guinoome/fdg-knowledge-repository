## Governing outcome

The environment is the hero. The data is the glass intelligence layer.

For home, station/site/branch/plant overviews and client previews, the domain environment is the interface itself: hero environment + translucent data layer + contextual hotspots + summary metrics + progressive drill-down. Do not substitute an admin template with a hero banner, opaque card wall, generic ERP dashboard, or decorative concept with no workflow.

Preserve data semantics, business/engineering calculations, approvals, evidence, existing useful routes, and recoverability. Remaster first; rebuild only when necessary.

## Ownership and boundaries

FPIS owns experience primitives, responsive composition, glass/hotspot accessibility and visual QA. FBPOIS owns fuel/business workflow meaning; FBIS owns reporting definitions; FSIS governs permission and audit boundaries; FWAIS governs review and recovery. Keep this ownership map internal, not customer-facing dashboard content.

Real source status remains explicit. A generated environment is a concept, not a site survey, digital twin, live camera or verified safety design. No sensor, pump state, transaction count, compliance, AI, performance or growth claim without supporting records. Missing data is not zero. No marketing override of measured truth.

## UI hierarchy

Primary: selected-period sales/throughput, key asset condition, top exception, next action. Secondary: trends, related zones, contextual detail. Tertiary: full reports, history, settings, logs and detailed forms.

Keep the hero visible. Use a few contextually anchored overlays, short labels, subtle connectors, high-contrast typography, restrained blur, and detail drawers/bottom sheets. On phones retain the scene and 3–6 essential facts, large tap targets, one-handed actions and progressive disclosure; never shrink the desktop canvas wholesale.

Utility-first surfaces remain appropriate for tables, settings, invoices, accounting, and data-entry. An immersive overview leads into working forms; it does not replace them.

## Protected semantics

Green normal/verified; amber attention/pending; red critical/failure; blue measured/informational; gray reference/inactive. Brand lime is not evidence of health. Labels accompany color.
