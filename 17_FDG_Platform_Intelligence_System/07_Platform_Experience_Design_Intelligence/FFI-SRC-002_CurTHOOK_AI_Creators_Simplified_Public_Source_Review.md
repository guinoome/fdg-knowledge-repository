## 2. Public positioning and offer

The landing page positions ACS as a structured path for Filipino creators and freelancers to learn practical AI creation, rather than collecting disconnected prompts.

Publisher-stated claims and offer details observed in the indexed page:
- Approximately 2,430 creators inside the community at the time the indexed page was captured.
- Listed offer: ₱2,997 one-time payment for lifetime access; crossed-out/list price ₱5,000.
- A 24-hour money-back/satisfaction guarantee is advertised, subject to the page's stated process.
- Price increase is described as occurring at 2,450 members in several places; another section inconsistently says 1,880 members. Treat member-count/price urgency as unverified, internally inconsistent marketing copy, not a reliable deadline.
- Creator's self-reported background: 7+ years Facebook Ads experience, P100M+ managed in Meta Ads, approximately ₱500K invested in AI learning, and AI speaking engagements. These are publisher claims, not independently verified credentials.
- The creator's personal story describes moving from corporate work and online businesses to client Facebook Ads, investing in AI learning from early 2024, and seeking a work model that preserves family time. Treat as creator-provided biography.
- The page says the community is Filipino-first and lessons/support use Filipino-English code-switching.
- Third-party AI subscriptions (examples named: Midjourney and Runway) are explicitly not included.
- The page states members can start with free tool tiers and upgrade where needed; this is a publisher statement and must be checked against current tool restrictions.

The page includes member testimonials describing AI-agency work, international freelance clients, AI video editing, camera movement, video/audio generation and time savings. These are individual testimonials and cannot establish typical outcomes, causal effects or FDG ROI.

## 7. High-value research hypotheses for FDG

These are hypotheses to test, not adopted standards:

### H1 — Asset-to-shot pipeline
A repeatable chain of brief → reference pack → image generation → image-to-video → edit → sound/captions may reduce rework when every asset is tied to a shot ID and continuity requirements.

### H2 — Specialized assistants as bounded production roles
Assistants for creative direction, prompt writing, VFX scripts, voiceover and story planning may improve throughput if each has explicit inputs, outputs, constraints, evidence requirements and human acceptance gates. A single general assistant should not silently own the entire production chain.

### H3 — Node graphs as inspectable production recipes
Node automation may make a workflow more repeatable than manually copying prompts, provided graphs are versioned, portable where possible, observable, recoverable and cost-accounted.

### H4 — Marketing review must optimize for commercial intent
A video with high views but no qualified inquiry or conversion may be a failed commercial asset. Define the intended audience, promise, proof, CTA and conversion event before production.

### H5 — Learn through one complete project
The best test is not reproducing every tutorial. Select one real FDG capability and take it through research, script, visual development, production, edit, technical/legal QA, distribution approval and FBIS measurement.

## 8. Risks, gaps and unknowns

- **Lesson evidence gap:** module summaries do not show actual instruction, demonstrations, settings, prompts or outputs.
- **Tool/version drift:** no exact provider/model/version matrix or test dates are exposed in the public catalogue.
- **Assistant opacity:** names, six assistant specifications, model providers, data handling, tool permissions and reproducibility are not disclosed.
- **Node-graph portability:** Magnific Spaces is a named dependency; export/import, API availability, execution limits, retry semantics and lock-in are unknown.
- **Quality:** no independent benchmark for image quality, motion quality, temporal consistency, lip sync, voice quality, editing quality or repeatability.
- **Rights and consent:** cloning face/voice, AI influencer creation, music generation and commercial client work require explicit rights and consent controls.
- **Commercial claims:** creator income, client acquisition, time saved and conversion results are testimonial/publisher claims until independently evidenced.
- **Price/urgency inconsistency:** the public page gives inconsistent member thresholds (2,450 versus 1,880) for the stated price increase.
- **Offer scope ambiguity:** assistants are described as included in the main offer but also as standalone/Inner Circle-related in one catalogue description. Confirm actual entitlement before any purchase decision.
- **Cost completeness:** third-party subscriptions are excluded; total cost must include provider plans, credits, rendering, storage, editing and human QA.

When authorized member material becomes available, capture each lesson with:
1. Course, module, lesson title and exact source location/timestamp.
2. Lesson date accessed and any revision/version marker.
3. Stated learning objective and prerequisites.
4. Tool/provider/model/version/plan and required subscriptions.

9. Existing FDG overlap and exact knowledge gap.

11. Disposition: REUSE / EXTEND / MERGE / REPLACE / NEW / REJECT.

Do not copy or republish paid lesson videos, course files, proprietary prompts or assistant instructions into the FDG repository. Store concise FDG-authored method summaries, citations, observations and test evidence within permitted use.
