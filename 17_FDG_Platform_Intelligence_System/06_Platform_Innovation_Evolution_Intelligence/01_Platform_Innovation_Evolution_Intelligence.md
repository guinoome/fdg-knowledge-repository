# Platform Innovation & Evolution Intelligence

This is a primary FPIS function.

## Purpose

Convert evidence into opportunities for platform improvement and innovation.

## Innovation Loop

Problem → Evidence → Pattern → Opportunity → Proposal → Decision → Experiment → Implementation → Validation → Learning

## Sources of Innovation

- user feedback
- usage patterns
- subscription behavior
- platform performance
- technical changes
- business results
- operational experience
- architecture constraints
- emerging technologies
- lessons learned

## FPIS Should Identify

- missing capabilities
- unnecessary capabilities
- workflow friction
- architecture improvements
- automation opportunities
- new product opportunities
- platform extensions
- consolidation opportunities
- retirement candidates

## Principle

Innovation is evidence-driven. FPIS recommends; authorized owners decide.
