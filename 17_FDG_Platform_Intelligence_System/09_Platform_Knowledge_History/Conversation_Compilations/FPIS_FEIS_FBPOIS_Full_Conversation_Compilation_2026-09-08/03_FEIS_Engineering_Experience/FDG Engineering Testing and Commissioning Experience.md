# FDG Engineering Testing and Commissioning Experience

## Core product story
**From Installation to Proven Performance**

Flow:
Installed → Ready for Test → Testing → Finding/Correction if needed → Retest → Accepted → Handover

## Primary visual experience
- system topology / 3D twin;
- asset selection;
- readiness status;
- live readings;
- design/acceptance comparison;
- spatial finding hotspots;
- evidence capture;
- retest state;
- digital handover package.

## Public/client value story
Do not sell “an AI dashboard.” Show how the platform turns installation evidence into a proven, defensible performance record.
