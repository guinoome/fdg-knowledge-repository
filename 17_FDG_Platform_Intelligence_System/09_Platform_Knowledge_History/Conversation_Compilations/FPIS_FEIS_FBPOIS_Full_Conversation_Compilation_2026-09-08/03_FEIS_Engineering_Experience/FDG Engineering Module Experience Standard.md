# FDG Engineering Module Experience Standard

## Current naming convention
Use **FDG Engineering [Module Name]** for now. Keep brand namespace replaceable so a later move to [[CORE Integrated Engineering]] does not require changes to domain logic or data models.

## Domain-native primary workspaces
### [[FDG Engineering Testing and Commissioning Experience]]
System topology, readiness, test evidence, measurements, findings, retest, handover.

### [[FDG Engineering Energy Audit Experience]]
Building energy X-ray, flow maps, baselines, opportunities, evidence, CAPEX/payback.

### BIM
Spatial model, clashes, system relationships.

### RCA
Evidence graph, causal hypotheses, confidence.

### QTO
Drawing/model, quantities, confidence/evidence.

### Maintenance
Asset health, PM workflow, evidence, findings, recommendations, retest/history.

### Construction Inspection
Drawing/spec reference, field evidence, nonconformance, closeout.

### CAPEX
Condition, alternatives, lifecycle economics, recommendation/approval.
