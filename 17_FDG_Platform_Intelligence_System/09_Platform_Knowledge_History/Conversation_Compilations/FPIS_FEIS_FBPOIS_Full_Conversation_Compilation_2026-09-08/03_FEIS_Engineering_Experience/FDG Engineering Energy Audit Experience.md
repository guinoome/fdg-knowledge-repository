# FDG Engineering Energy Audit Experience

## Core product story
**See where your building is losing energy—and prove what is worth fixing.**

## Primary visual workspace
- 3D building / floor layers;
- energy Sankey from utility → building → systems → equipment → useful energy/loss;
- actual / baseline / expected / abnormal / potential savings;
- hotspot findings;
- opportunity prioritization;
- CAPEX, annual saving, payback, uncertainty;
- evidence chain from measured data through recommendation.

## Evidence chain
Measured Data → Calculation → Reasoning → Recommendation → CAPEX → Annual Saving → Payback → Uncertainty → Evidence
