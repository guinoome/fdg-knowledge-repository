# Fuel, Retail, Restaurant, Tire and Bakery Concepts

## Fuel / Gas Station
Operational language: forecourt, pumps, tanks, wet stock, shift closeout, delivery, safety, POS.

## Micro Fuel Station
Compact community-ready station concept with controlled inventory and transaction visibility. Visual concept only; actual tank/dispenser layout, clearances and regulatory compliance require verified engineering/regulatory design.

## Sari-Sari Store
Neighborhood retail language: quick POS, e-wallet, inventory, supplier restock, customer/suki ledger, pricing, margin and demand.

## Restaurant
Dining/kitchen spatial flow, reservations, tables, KDS/order flow, menu engineering, food cost, inventory, delivery and guest feedback.

## Tire Business
Motorcycle, passenger car, SUV/light truck and commercial tire workflows. Service bays, fitment, inventory, work orders, alignment/balancing, technician progress and payment.

## Bakery Multi-Branch
Central kitchen/production, branch orders, replenishment, delivery fleet, recipes/costing, POS, production schedule and daily closeout.

## Marketing note
The later attached business designs already represent the “after marketing” direction because they contain outcome-oriented messaging, stronger brand hierarchy, customer-growth framing and persuasive product presentation.
