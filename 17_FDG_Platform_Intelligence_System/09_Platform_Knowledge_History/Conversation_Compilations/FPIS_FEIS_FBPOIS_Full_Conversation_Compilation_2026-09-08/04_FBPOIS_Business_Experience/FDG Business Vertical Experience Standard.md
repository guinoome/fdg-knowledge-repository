# FDG Business Vertical Experience Standard

## Rule
Do not force every business into the same dashboard. Reuse platform capabilities but let each vertical present its own operating reality.

## Verticals discussed
- Fuel / gas station
- Micro fuel station
- Sari-sari store
- Restaurant
- Tire shop
- Bakery
- Multi-branch network / fleet

## Shared business primitives
- branches/sites;
- sales/orders;
- inventory;
- people/shifts;
- assets/equipment;
- alerts/exceptions;
- fleet/logistics;
- finance/profitability;
- executive portfolio view.
