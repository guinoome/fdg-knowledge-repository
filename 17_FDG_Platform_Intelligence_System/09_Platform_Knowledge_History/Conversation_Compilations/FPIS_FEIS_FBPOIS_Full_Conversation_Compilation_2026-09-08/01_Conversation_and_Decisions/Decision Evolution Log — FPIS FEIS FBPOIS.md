# Decision Evolution Log — FPIS FEIS FBPOIS

This document preserves old and new directions. A later decision does not delete the earlier one.

## Visual quality
### Earlier
Initial concepts established general dashboard direction.

### Refinement
Generic AI/SaaS dashboard styling was rejected. The standard became bespoke, domain-native, product-grade, client-magnet design.

## Naming
### Earlier
CORE Integrated Engineering was explored as an umbrella identity.

### Refinement
Use **FDG Engineering [Module Name]** for the current presentation layer. Keep brand prefix replaceable so future transition to [[CORE Integrated Engineering]] is centralized rather than a rewrite.

## Static showcase → interactive product experience
### Earlier
Static desktop showcase concepts.

### Refinement
Interactive 3D, live state visualization, responsive mobile/tablet views, field workflows, animated engineering flows, findings hotspots, and motion semantics.

## Shared platform → domain-native experiences
### Earlier
One visual family across modules.

### Refinement
Shared platform DNA remains, but each engineering/business vertical owns a primary workspace reflecting its real operating model.

## Marketing layer
### Earlier
Focus was mainly on product UX and operational clarity.

### Refinement
Marketing becomes part of FPIS experience architecture for attraction, trust, conversion, onboarding, retention, and expansion—without modifying engineering truth.

## White-labeling
### Earlier
FDG branding dominated first-party concepts.

### Refinement
Tenant branding may dominate enterprise deployments through governed Experience Profiles. Platform provenance remains centrally configurable and normally visible.

## Motion demo
### Earlier
Local MP4s used motion-board/slideshow treatment.

### Refinement
A true demo should simulate product behavior: taps, 3D movement, flow animation, map routes, status changes, findings, retest, voiceover, and sound design.

## Maintenance scope
### Earlier
Fire Pump PM appeared as a potential standalone workflow.

### Refinement
Do not build a Fire Pump app. Build [[FEIS Preventive Maintenance Engine]] with Fire Pump as [[Fire Pump PM Template 001]].

## Evidence capture
### Earlier
Fire Pump photos and checklist evidence.

### Refinement
Generalize into [[FDG Maintenance Evidence Capture Engine]] reusable by all PM domains.

## Recommendations
### Earlier
Findings could produce recommendations.

### Refinement
Use deterministic rule-based recommendation logic first; generative assistance is secondary and must not replace the underlying rule/evidence basis.

## 3D reconstruction
### Earlier
3D view was treated as a designed/schematic experience.

### Refinement
Technician photos may eventually feed assisted photo-to-3D reconstruction. Generated models remain unverified until human confirmation.

## Deployment timing
### Earlier
Production-grade PM module discussed in weeks.

### Refinement
Because this is the first FEIS/FBPOIS field deployment, make the core workflow functional in hours, then patch continuously.

## Conflict handling
### Earlier
Generic multi-user conflict state: “Conflict — Review Required.”

### Refinement
Each PM report has a primary technician owner based on the signed-in account. Other technicians are participants/manual names. Most normal technician conflicts are therefore avoided. Internal data should still use separate child records/versioning rather than whole-record overwrites.

## Premium Experience mandate

### Earlier
Product-grade, domain-native, client-magnet design was established as direction, while implementation could still be interpreted as an operational dashboard with marketing kept mostly outside the product.

### Refinement — 2026-09-09
[[17_FDG_Platform_Intelligence_System/07_Platform_Experience_Design_Intelligence/03_FDG_Premium_Experience_Design_and_Implementation_Mandate|FDG Premium Experience Design & Implementation Mandate]] makes the gate explicit: a major FDG platform is simultaneously a product experience, marketing asset, operational tool, and business-growth instrument. Mobile web is primary; generic admin-template delivery is rejected; public and operational experiences remain connected without blurring evidence or domain truth. Implementation patches forward and preserves working logic.

## Unified business account and modular subscriptions

### Earlier
Fuel Operations was the deployed root experience, while future FDG Business verticals were primarily expressed as roadmap concepts and visual boards.

### Refinement — 2026-09-10
[[Projects/Active/FDG Business Platform/docs/FDG_Business_Platform_Unified_Account_Modular_Subscription_Merge_Mandate|the Unified Account, Modular Subscription & Experience Merge Mandate]] establishes one ecosystem account above independently governed module subscriptions and branch instances. The main [[Projects/Active/FDG Business Platform/README|FDG Business Platform]] now owns discovery, client-magnet storytelling, `My Platforms`, portfolio navigation, and shared scope visibility. Modules retain operational workflows, users, permissions, data, settings, and subscription lifecycles. Fuel Operations remains connected at `/fuel-station/`; cancellation of one local prototype subscription preserves the account and other modules. Real authentication, server authorization, and payments remain gated rather than implied.
