# Marketing + UI UX = Business Growth

## Business-growth principle
Marketing brings the right users to the product. UX understands their needs and friction. UI makes the solution clear and engaging. Product value proves the outcome. Together these improve conversion, adoption, retention, referrals, and expansion.

A useful lifecycle for FPIS is:
**Attract → Understand → Explain → Prove → Decide → Act → Measure → Retain → Grow**

## Three experience layers
### Marketing Experience
Used for public pages, demos, case studies, proposals, pricing, onboarding, and conversion experiences.

Marketing can influence:
- headline hierarchy;
- visual storytelling;
- proof of value;
- before/after narratives;
- client outcomes;
- CTA placement;
- case-study evidence;
- onboarding guidance.

### Product UX
Used for actual work. The goal is task completion, low friction, correct decisions, and visible value.

### Operational / Engineering UI
Protected layer. Marketing must not alter:
- safety/status semantics;
- acceptance criteria;
- measurements;
- evidence provenance;
- audit trail;
- financial calculation basis;
- compliance meaning.

## FPIS Business-Outcome Design Principle
Every experience should connect:
**User need → insight → decision → action → measurable outcome.**

Visual quality supports that chain; it does not replace it.
