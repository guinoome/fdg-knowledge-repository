# FDG Responsive Experience Standard

## Governing rule
**Progressive rearrangement, not progressive information loss.**

The same project/asset/evidence information remains accessible across desktop, tablet, mobile browser, and installable PWA. Layout changes by screen size; engineering truth does not disappear.

## Example composition
Desktop may show simultaneously:
- 3D/system view
- system tree
- findings
- live data
- evidence

Tablet stacks or collapses secondary panels while preserving access.

Mobile can use:
- full-screen 3D/system context;
- bottom sheet tabs for Status, Measurements, Findings, Evidence, History, Actions.

## Field requirements
- touch-first controls;
- camera capture;
- QR access;
- outdoor/plant-room readability;
- offline project persistence;
- clear sync state;
- no dependence on continuous connectivity to finish PM work.
