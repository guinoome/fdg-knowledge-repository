# Experience Composer and White-Label Governance

## Principle
**Configurable experience, not configurable engineering truth.**

## Experience layers
ENGINEERING / BUSINESS PLATFORM CORE
→ DOMAIN MODULE
→ FPIS EXPERIENCE SYSTEM
→ TENANT EXPERIENCE PROFILE
→ CLIENT PLATFORM

## Branding modes
1. Logo-only
2. Full Brand Kit
3. Guided Auto-Design

Potential profiles:
- Executive Minimal
- Industrial Technical
- Hospitality Premium
- Infrastructure Command
- Sustainability Visual

## Client-configurable areas
- logo and brand colors;
- typography preference;
- imagery style;
- landing experience;
- navigation family;
- dashboard composition;
- executive/technical emphasis;
- 3D starting view;
- motion intensity;
- report branding;
- terminology.

## Protected areas
- engineering semantics;
- evidence provenance;
- acceptance states;
- safety indicators;
- audit trail;
- calculation methodology;
- status meaning.

## Attribution
Normal enterprise rule:
**Powered by FDG Engineering Platform**

Attribution should be centrally configurable for future provider naming, for example:
- PLATFORM_PROVIDER_NAME
- PLATFORM_PROVIDER_LOGO
- PLATFORM_PROVIDER_ATTRIBUTION
- PLATFORM_PROVIDER_URL

Complete removal is a contract/OEM licensing decision, not a normal tenant toggle.
