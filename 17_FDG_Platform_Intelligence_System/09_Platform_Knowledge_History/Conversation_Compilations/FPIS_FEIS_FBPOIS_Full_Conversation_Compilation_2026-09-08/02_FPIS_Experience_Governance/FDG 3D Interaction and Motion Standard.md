# FDG 3D Interaction and Motion Standard

## Purpose
3D and motion should reveal relationships that are difficult to understand in flat forms.

## Interaction patterns
- rotate and isolate equipment;
- section/explode a system;
- show design vs installed geometry;
- highlight spatial findings;
- animate flows through piping/ducts/electrical paths;
- show before/after state transitions;
- reveal fault propagation;
- scrub historical state;
- move from building → room → system → asset → evidence.

## Energy Audit
Use the building as an “energy X-ray” with layers for HVAC, lighting, pumps, DHW, kitchen, laundry, vertical transport, plug loads, renewables, water, and fuel.

## T&C
Use topology, readiness, live measurements, acceptance states, findings, and retest visualization.

## Maintenance
Use asset/room spatial context, evidence hotspots, degradation/finding states, corrective action and retest status.

## Truth rule
A visually generated model is not automatically an as-built record. Generated or inferred geometry must be visibly marked until verified.
