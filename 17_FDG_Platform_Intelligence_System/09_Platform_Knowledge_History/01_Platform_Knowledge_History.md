# Platform Knowledge & History

FPIS maintains the longitudinal memory of FDG platforms.

## Preserve

- previous architectures
- major releases
- architecture changes
- experiments
- failures
- successful patterns
- lessons learned
- deprecated capabilities
- retired technologies
- migration history
- design evolution

## Rule

Do not erase historical knowledge merely because a platform changed.

The current state should be clear while previous states remain recoverable and traceable.
