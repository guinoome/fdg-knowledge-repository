# Platform Decision Intelligence

FPIS preserves decisions that affect platform evolution.

## Record

- decision
- date
- platform
- problem
- evidence
- alternatives
- selected approach
- rejected alternatives
- rationale
- expected result
- validation method
- actual result
- follow-up

## Decision Types

- architecture
- technology
- UX
- lifecycle
- subscription model
- platform capability
- integration
- performance
- retirement
- innovation

## Principle

A platform decision without recorded rationale becomes future ambiguity.
