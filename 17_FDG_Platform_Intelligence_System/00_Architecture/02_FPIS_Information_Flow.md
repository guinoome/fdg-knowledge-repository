# FPIS Information Flow

```text
Users
  ↓
Platform Usage
  ↓
Platform Evidence
  ↓
FPIS
  ├── Architecture
  ├── Lifecycle
  ├── Performance & Value
  ├── User / Usage / Subscription
  ├── Experience & Design
  └── Innovation & Evolution
        ↓
   Recommendation
        ↓
   Human Decision
        ↓
 Platform Change
        ↓
 Validation
        ↓
 Learning
```

FPIS does not need to own every underlying dataset. It needs reliable access to the evidence required to understand platform condition and evolution.
