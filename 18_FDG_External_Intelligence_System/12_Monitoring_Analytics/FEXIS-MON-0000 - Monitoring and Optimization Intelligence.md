# FEXIS-MON-0000 - Monitoring and Optimization Intelligence

## Purpose

Continuously measure external discoverability, representation quality, and interoperability.

## Metrics

- Search visibility
- Answer inclusion
- Generative visibility
- Citation frequency
- Entity consistency
- Machine-readability health
- Agent discoverability
- External representation integrity
- Optimization impact

Metrics must be interpreted with evidence and context rather than treated as truth by themselves.
