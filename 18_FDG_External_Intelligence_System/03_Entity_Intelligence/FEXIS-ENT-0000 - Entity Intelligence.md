# FEXIS-ENT-0000 - Entity Intelligence

## Purpose

Maintain consistent, authoritative representations of FDG entities across external ecosystems.

## Scope

- Organization identity
- Brands
- Products and services
- People and roles where authorized
- Locations
- Projects and capabilities
- Relationships between entities
- External entity consistency

## Principle

Entity representations must trace back to authoritative FDG knowledge and approved external information.
