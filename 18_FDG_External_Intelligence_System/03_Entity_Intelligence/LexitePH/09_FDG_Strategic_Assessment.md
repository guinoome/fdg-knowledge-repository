## Differentiation

LexitePH appears oriented toward operational software and industrial technology.

FDG's intended differentiator is the engineering intelligence layer:

- engineering knowledge
- engineering calculations
- optimization
- review/compliance
- evidence/provenance
- decision intelligence
- memory/context
- organizational learning

These are complementary rather than mutually exclusive.
