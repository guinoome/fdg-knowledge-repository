# Security, Ownership and Data Portability

## Questions for vendor due diligence

### Data ownership
Who owns customer operational data?

### Tenant isolation
How is one customer prevented from accessing another customer's data?

### Encryption
Is data protected in transit and at rest?

### Audit
Can every material change be attributed to a user/service/time?

### Vendor access
Can vendor personnel access customer data?
Under what controls?
Is privileged access logged?

### Export
Can a customer export:

- assets
- work orders
- maintenance history
- inventory
- procurement records
- audit records
- documents
- users
- attachments

### Source code / IP
For custom development, determine whether software is:

- vendor-owned SaaS
- licensed
- customer-owned
- jointly developed
- subject to escrow

### Termination
Define data retrieval, format, timing, deletion, and migration support.

## FDG position

Operational data is a long-term organizational asset. Vendor lock-in must be treated as a lifecycle risk.
