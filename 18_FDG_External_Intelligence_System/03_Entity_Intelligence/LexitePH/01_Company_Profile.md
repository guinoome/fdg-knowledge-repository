# LexitePH — Company Profile

## Observed positioning

LexitePH presents itself around:

- Software Development
- Information Technology
- Engineering

The public portfolio spans operational software, industrial automation, smart technology, ICT infrastructure, and engineering services.

## Publicly presented capability areas

- CMMS
- MES
- Academe management
- Event management
- SCADA
- Custom software
- ICT infrastructure
- IoT / smart buildings / smart factories
- Mechanical/electrical/CAD engineering services

## Interpretation

This is broader than a conventional software-development vendor. The positioning crosses:

Physical engineering
→ automation / instrumentation
→ operational software
→ business process
→ data / intelligence

That overlap is the primary reason LexitePH is strategically relevant to FDG.

## Caution

Public marketing material establishes what LexitePH presents as capabilities. It does not, by itself, establish production scale, technical architecture, customer count, security maturity, uptime, or actual integration depth. Those remain due-diligence items.
