# OWL — Product Analysis

## Why OWL matters

OWL appears to represent a product evolution from a focused CMMS toward a broader operational platform:

CMMS
+ MES
+ Procurement
+ Audit
+ Lex AI

This trajectory is important because it validates a platform approach in which operational functions are connected instead of isolated.

## Lex AI

Publicly presented AI use cases appear focused on operational assistance, such as:

- querying live work-order status
- answering operational questions
- providing how-to guidance
- drafting work orders with user confirmation

FDG opportunity:
Use this as the lower operational-assistant layer. FDG CORE can go further into evidence-backed engineering reasoning, engineering review, decision records, and organizational learning.

## Pricing observation

Publicly presented CMMS pricing is asset-oriented and heavily front-loaded in year one, with recurring pricing shown separately. Treat published figures as marketing information, not verified commercial terms.
