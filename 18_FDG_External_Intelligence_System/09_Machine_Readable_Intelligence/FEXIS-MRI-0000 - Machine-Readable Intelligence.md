# FEXIS-MRI-0000 - Machine-Readable Intelligence

## Purpose

Convert approved organizational knowledge into representations that can be reliably consumed by software, search systems, intelligent systems, and agents.

## Scope

- Structured metadata
- Semantic relationships
- Knowledge graph representations
- Machine-readable service descriptions
- Organizational schemas
- Provenance and identifiers

## Principle

Machine-readable representation is a controlled projection of authoritative knowledge, not an independent source of truth.
