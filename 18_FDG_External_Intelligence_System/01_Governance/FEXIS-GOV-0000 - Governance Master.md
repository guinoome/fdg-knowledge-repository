# FEXIS-GOV-0000 - Governance Master

## Purpose

Establish governance boundaries, authority, evidence requirements, approval controls, and lifecycle responsibilities for FEXIS.

## Rules

1. Authoritative FDG knowledge remains governed outside FEXIS unless explicitly delegated.
2. External optimization shall never justify factual distortion.
3. Automated learning must preserve provenance and confidence.
4. Unverified observations remain observations or hypotheses.
5. High-impact external changes require appropriate approval.
6. Security, privacy, legal, and confidentiality constraints override discoverability objectives.
7. All material automated decisions must be auditable.

## Evidence Progression

Observation → Hypothesis → Evidence → Validated Knowledge → Approved Knowledge

## Change Classes

- Informational observation
- Optimization recommendation
- Low-risk automated representation update
- Controlled external publication
- High-impact organizational representation change

Approval requirements shall increase with impact and risk.
