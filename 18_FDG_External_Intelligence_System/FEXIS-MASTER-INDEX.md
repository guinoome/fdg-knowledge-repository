# FEXIS-MASTER-INDEX

## System

17_FDG_External_Intelligence_System (FEXIS)

## Master Definition

FEXIS is the FDG Intelligence System responsible for making authorized FDG organizational knowledge discoverable, understandable, verifiable, representable, and interoperable across search engines, answer engines, generative systems, intelligent systems, and autonomous agents.

## Continuous Learning

FEXIS contains Continuous External Learning Intelligence as a specialized capability. The reusable continuous-learning mechanism belongs in FDG CORE.

## Governing Statement

FEXIS may continuously learn, but it shall not continuously redefine truth.
