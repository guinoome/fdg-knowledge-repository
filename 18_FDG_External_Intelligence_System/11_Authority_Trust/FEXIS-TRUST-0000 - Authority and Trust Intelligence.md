# FEXIS-TRUST-0000 - Authority and Trust Intelligence

## Purpose

Protect the credibility and evidentiary integrity of FDG's external representation.

## Controls

- Evidence provenance
- Source authority
- Credential verification
- Cross-source consistency
- Claim verification
- Confidence classification
- Change history

## Rule

Visibility, ranking, or citation frequency shall never be treated as proof of factual correctness.
