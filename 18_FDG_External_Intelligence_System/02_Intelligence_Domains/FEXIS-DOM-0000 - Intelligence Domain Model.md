# FEXIS-DOM-0000 - Intelligence Domain Model

FEXIS is organized into specialized intelligence domains:

1. Search Intelligence
2. Answer Intelligence
3. Generative Search Intelligence
4. LLM Discoverability Intelligence
5. Agent Discoverability Intelligence
6. Entity Intelligence
7. Authority and Trust Intelligence
8. Machine-Readable Intelligence
9. External Interface Intelligence
10. Monitoring and Optimization Intelligence
11. Continuous External Learning Intelligence

These domains share common FDG CORE capabilities for evidence, knowledge, reasoning, security, governance, and learning.
