# FSIS-0700 — Intellectual Property Security Master Index

Protect the combination of FDG:

- architecture;
- standards;
- source code;
- workflows;
- business methods;
- engineering methods;
- organizational design;
- strategic plans;
- historical decisions;
- accumulated knowledge.

The objective is not to claim that similar ideas can never be recreated elsewhere. The objective is to prevent unauthorized disclosure of the proprietary combination that constitutes the FDG Ecosystem.
