# FSIS-1001 — Security Risk Intelligence

Maintain a security risk register containing:

```text
Risk ID
Asset
Threat
Vulnerability
Exposure
Likelihood
Impact
Risk Rating
Existing Controls
Treatment
Owner
Status
Review Date
Evidence
```

Prioritize risks based on credible impact and exposure.

Do not invent quantitative risk values without a defined methodology.
