# FSIS-ROADMAP-002 — FSIS Maturity Model

### Level 1 — Principles
Security boundaries and doctrine are documented.

### Level 2 — Classification
Knowledge is classified and owned.

### Level 3 — Controlled Collaboration
Collaborator access is bounded by work package.

### Level 4 — Enforced Access
Identity and authorization controls are technically enforced.

### Level 5 — Auditable Security
Security-relevant activity is traceable.

### Level 6 — Security Intelligence
Evidence is analyzed for risk and anomalies.

### Level 7 — Policy Automation
Validated policies are automatically enforced.

### Level 8 — Ecosystem Security Intelligence
FSIS continuously protects and learns across the FDG Ecosystem.
