# FSIS-0200 — Identity Intelligence Master Index

Identity intelligence answers **who or what is requesting access**.

Identity classes:

- FDG owner
- Human collaborator
- Contractor
- AI collaborator
- AI agent
- Service
- Application
- Device

Minimum identity attributes should include a stable identifier, status, owner, role/classification, and authorization state.

Identity is separate from authorization. Knowing who a collaborator is does not automatically grant access.
