# FSIS-0900 — Audit and Traceability Master Index

Auditability provides evidence of security-relevant activity.

Where technically and legally appropriate, record:

```text
Who
What
When
Why / authorization basis
Resource
Action
Result
```

Important events may include access, modification, export, permission change, approval, revocation, and security incident activity.

Audit logs themselves require protection against unauthorized alteration.
