# FCMIS — Viral Hook, Visual Signature & Publishing Intelligence
Status: Proposed architecture; production and posting not deployed.
Date: 2026-10-16
Owner: FDG Creative & Marketing Intelligence System (FCMIS)

## Purpose
Create original, engaging, engineering-verified MEP educational and promotional media and turn measured audience attention into qualified leads and revenue. FDG Creative Studio is the production interface; FCMIS governs intelligence, workflow and evidence.

## Signature 001: Five-second engineering challenge
0–2 seconds: show a technically solvable question and visual evidence.
2–7 seconds: visible 5-second countdown with continuous, meaningful camera motion and no distracting false cues.
7–12 seconds: reveal verified answer.
12–25 seconds: explain the mechanism using verified engineering visualizations.
25–30 seconds: relevant question, save/share or commercial call to action.
Test 3s, 5s and no-countdown variants; do not assume countdown improves outcomes.

## Visual DNA
1. Cinematic equipment cutaways.
2. Animated, unit-checked engineering calculations.
3. Physically plausible fluid, airflow, heat and energy flow.
4. High-contrast technical overlays, readable on mobile.
5. Cause-and-effect transitions; consistent FDG identity.
Never depict simulated failure as documented real-world evidence.

## Reusable production contract
Each content item has: content_id; engineering_topic; audience; business_goal; evidence_sources; technical_reviewer; hook_variants; scene_timeline; visual_style_version; assets_and_licenses; master_video; platform_variants; captions; approval_status; platform_permissions; publishing_schedule; publication_receipts; analytics; attributable_leads; costs; lessons.
Production states: DISCOVERED -> SOURCED -> SCRIPTED -> ENGINEERING_REVIEWED -> PRODUCED -> QA_APPROVED -> SCHEDULED -> PUBLISHED -> MEASURED -> LEARNED.
Reject publication without appropriate authorization, source review, IP clearance and accurate technical claims.

## Platform adapters
Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, FDG websites and authorized client campaigns. Implement platform-specific API adapters with capability checks, OAuth scopes, retries, rate limits, audit receipts and revoke access. Never assume direct auto-posting is supported for every account or content type.

## Topic radar
Periodically research publicly available MEP topics; preserve links, dates, platform and engagement evidence. Distinguish measured trends from predicted potential. Rank by audience fit, freshness, hook strength, verifiability, differentiation, production effort, and qualified lead potential.

## Learning
Track first-seconds retention where available, completion, rewatches, saves, shares, comments, clicks, leads, revenue, and production costs. Compare hooks within comparable topic/platform cohorts; avoid declaring causality from raw view counts. Use approved learning records to refine subsequent content.

## Implementation slices
1. One engineering-approved 30-second challenge video with 5-second countdown and two alternative hooks.
2. Platform-ready exports and caption/thumbnail package; manual publishing.
3. Authorized scheduled publishing adapter and publication ledger.
4. Analytics ingestion and measured hook experiments.
5. Multi-platform distribution and recurring topic radar.
6. Client campaign isolation and commercial service packaging.

## Governance
Local-first asset/archive, offline-capable production where feasible, agent-neutral contracts, provider-replaceable generation adapters. Capture once, reuse everywhere. Reuse existing FEIS engineering evidence and FDG branding; avoid duplicate standards. Preserve provenance and human review for safety-sensitive material.
