## Purpose and scope
Build a repeatable FDG-owned content operation for MEP education, engineering service marketing and external client creative campaigns. Discovery, technical evidence, original creative production, platform-specific distribution, analytics, lead attribution and learning must share a single content record. Avoid duplicating FEIS engineering rules or FPJIS governance.

## Cadence — initial 30-day pilot (Asia/Manila)
Monday: five-second-countdown engineering challenge.
Tuesday: cinematic equipment cutaway / how it works.
Wednesday: verified engineering calculation / energy or cost saving.
Thursday: spot-the-defect inspection challenge.
Friday: failure mechanism / root-cause animation.
Saturday: 60-second practical MEP lesson.
Sunday: highlights, polls, audience question response.
Target five distinct master content packages per day, then repurpose for eligible Facebook Reels, Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn and FDG websites. Long-form and client-specific videos only when justified by evidence and capacity.
Publishing target per active eligible platform: at least five posts daily, initial test slots 07:00, 10:00, 13:00, 16:00, 20:00 Asia/Manila. For six active platforms, this is 30 daily publications and 900 in 30 days, based on five master content packages per day with platform-specific variants. These are capacity targets, not evidence of publication. Platform policies, format suitability and analytics may require adaptive scheduling rather than blindly forcing five videos daily. Production runs as a rolling queue: 07:00 trend scan, 09:00 script/storyboard batch, 12:00 engineering and creative QA, 21:00 performance review. Maintain a 3–7 day preapproved content buffer; scheduling and production are decoupled. Times are proposals, not currently scheduled tasks. Test platform-specific posting times empirically.

## Video formats
10 seconds: visual hook and payoff.
30 seconds: 0–2 challenge setup, 2–7 five-second countdown, 7–12 verified reveal, 12–25 explanation, 25–30 relevant CTA.
60 seconds: expanded demonstration with deeper engineering reasoning.
Long-form: detailed instruction and case studies.
Test countdown against no-countdown and alternative durations; maintain a hook variant library and engagement data. Do not promise virality.

## Visual signatures
Cinematic equipment cutaways; validated formula animation; plausible fluid/air/heat/energy flow; high-contrast mobile-readable overlays; strong cause-effect transitions. Consistent FDG branding, visual tokens and ending treatment. Create original media and preserve licensing provenance.

## Autonomous workflow
TREND_DISCOVERY -> TOPIC_RANKING -> SOURCE_EVIDENCE -> HOOK_EXPERIMENT -> SCRIPT -> ENGINEERING_REVIEW -> STORYBOARD -> PRODUCTION -> VISUAL_QA -> APPROVAL -> PLATFORM_VARIANTS -> SCHEDULE -> PUBLISH -> VERIFY_RECEIPT -> ANALYTICS -> LEAD_FOLLOWUP -> LEARNING.
Engineering-sensitive content requires reviewer approval. Claims and computations must be sourced. Client posting requires explicit client authorization and account separation. Never claim that an unverified simulation is real footage.
Use official APIs and approved publishing integrations with token security, permissions, rate limits, retry queues, idempotency keys, publication URLs, revocation and audit trails. API eligibility differs by platform and account. Failed posts stay in a visible exception queue.

## Hashtag discovery, selection and QA
For each master content package, generate **at least 10 relevant candidate hashtags** from (a) precise MEP subject tags, (b) audience/profession tags, (c) contextual trending tags supported by recent platform evidence, and (d) FDG brand/campaign tags. Store the candidate set with topic match, platform, observed source/date, relevance score, trend evidence, and approval state. Never invent a hashtag's popularity or label it viral without platform evidence.
**Publication rule:** do not blindly publish all ten on every platform. Select a platform-appropriate subset according to current platform limits, best practices and audience fit; when permitted and justified, use ten or more, otherwise keep ten in the internal candidate pool and publish fewer. Avoid unrelated trending tags, repetitive hashtag stuffing, misleading claims and banned/restricted tags. Refresh trends before scheduling; log the selected tags and subsequent performance for learning.
Example candidate pool for a verified pump-cavitation explainer (not verified trending): #MechanicalEngineering #MEP #HVAC #Pumps #PumpMaintenance #Cavitation #EngineeringExplained #FluidMechanics #EngineeringEducation #FDGEngineering #PlantEngineering #MaintenanceEngineering.

## Ninety-day follower growth mission (stretch targets, not forecasts)
Campaign window: first 90 days following actual launch, not document creation. Target 1,000,000 combined platform follower accounts as stretch; 3,000,000 as exceptional breakthrough. Cross-platform sum is not deduplicated unique people. Establish current follower baseline, platform eligibility and attributable growth before launch. Suggested *illustrative* cumulative stretch checkpoints: day 30 50,000; day 60 250,000; day 90 1,000,000. More conservative operating checkpoints: day 30 10,000; day 60 40,000; day 90 100,000. Neither track is a prediction or promise.
At 1 million net new follows, average requirement ~11,111/day. A hypothetical 0.5% view-to-follow conversion implies ~200 million relevant views; measure actual conversion before capacity or spend decisions. Five master packages daily = 450 over 90 days; six channels = up to 2,700 platform publications, subject to permissions and policies.
Experiment framework: compare countdown vs non-countdown hooks, 10/30/60-second durations, topic clusters, endings, subtitles and visual signatures in comparable cohorts; track impressions, qualified views, early retention, completion, follows per 1,000 views, shares, saves, qualified leads, verified sales, margin and production cost. Prioritize original series and evidence-backed sequels; no purchased followers, fabricated engagement or misleading claims. Weekly review can reallocate capacity by audience fit and economics, not raw view count alone. Protect engineering QA and authorized publishing gates. Revenue and follower growth are separate KPIs.
