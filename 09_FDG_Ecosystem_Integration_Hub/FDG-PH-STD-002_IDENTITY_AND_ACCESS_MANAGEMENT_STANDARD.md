# Purpose

This standard defines the identity, authentication, authorization, and access governance framework for the FDG Platform Hub.

The purpose is to ensure that every person, application, and system interacting with the FDG Ecosystem has controlled and traceable access.

---

# Core Principle

Identity establishes trust.

Every access request must answer:

- Who is requesting access?
- What role do they have?
- What information do they need?
- What actions are they authorized to perform?

---

# Objective

The FDG Identity and Access Management system shall provide:

- unified identity management
- role-based access control
- secure authentication
- permission governance
- activity traceability
- access lifecycle management
