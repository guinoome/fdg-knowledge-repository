# Purpose

This standard defines how the FDG Platform Hub shall scale in capability, users, businesses, technologies, and operational complexity.

The objective is to ensure FDG can grow without requiring complete architectural redesign.

---

# Core Principle

Growth should increase capability, not complexity.

---

# Objective

The Scalability Architecture shall enable:

- additional FDG businesses
- additional platforms
- increasing users
- increasing data volume
- expanding integrations
- long-term maintainability
