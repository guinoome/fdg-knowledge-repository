# Purpose

This standard defines the architecture for preserving, organizing, retrieving, and improving knowledge across the FDG Ecosystem.

The objective is to transform individual experience into reusable organizational capability.

---

# Core Principle

Technology changes.

Knowledge must continue improving.

---

# Objective

The FDG Knowledge Architecture shall provide:

- centralized knowledge preservation
- engineering memory
- business intelligence continuity
- lessons learned capture
- institutional capability growth
