# Purpose

This standard defines the Business Intelligence Layer of the FDG Platform Hub.

The purpose is to transform operational data from FDG platforms into meaningful insights that support strategic, operational, and engineering decisions.

---

# Core Principle

Data becomes valuable when it improves decisions.

The Business Intelligence Layer converts information into organizational understanding.

---

# Objective

The FDG Business Intelligence Layer shall provide:

- ecosystem visibility
- performance monitoring
- executive dashboards
- operational analytics
- trend identification
- decision support
