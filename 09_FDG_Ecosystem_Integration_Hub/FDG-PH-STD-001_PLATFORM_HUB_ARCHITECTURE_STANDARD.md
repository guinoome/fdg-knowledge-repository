# Purpose

This standard defines the architecture, role, and governing principles of the FDG Platform Hub.

The FDG Platform Hub serves as the central coordination layer connecting FDG digital platforms while preserving the independence of each specialized system.

---

# Core Principle

The FDG Platform Hub does not replace specialized platforms.

It enables them to operate together as one ecosystem.

---

# Strategic Objective

The FDG Platform Hub shall provide:

- unified ecosystem access
- application coordination
- identity governance
- controlled data exchange
- platform visibility
- future expansion capability
