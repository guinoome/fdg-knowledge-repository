# Purpose

This standard defines the organizational architecture of the FDG Ecosystem.

It establishes how engineering capability is organized, governed, coordinated, and continuously improved.

The organizational architecture is independent of specific people, software platforms, AI models, or implementation technologies.

---

# Core Principle

Engineering capability is designed.

It is not accumulated by chance.

The organization is the primary engineering system.

Technology supports the organization.

It does not define it.

---

# Organizational Objectives

The organizational architecture shall:

- create repeatable engineering capability
- separate strategic thinking from execution
- maximize knowledge reuse
- enable specialization
- preserve organizational intelligence
- remain scalable across people and technologies

---

# Organizational Layers

The FDG Ecosystem is organized into the following layers.

Engineering Vision

↓

Governance

↓

Organizational Architecture

↓

Knowledge Repository

↓

Engineering Operations

↓

Projects

↓

Execution

↓

Learning

↓

Organizational Intelligence

Each layer strengthens the layers above and below it.

---

# Organizational Building Blocks

The organizational architecture consists of:

- Engineering Capability
- Organizational Roles
- Decision Authority
- Delegation
- Collaboration
- Work Packages
- Interfaces
- Continuous Improvement

Each building block has one primary responsibility.

---

# Separation of Responsibilities

The organization distinguishes between:

## Strategy

Determines what should be achieved.

---

## Orchestration

Determines how work should be organized.

---

## Specialization

Provides domain expertise.

---

## Execution

Performs engineering work.

---

## Governance

Reviews and approves organizational knowledge.

Each responsibility shall remain clearly defined.

---

# Organizational Independence

The organizational architecture shall not depend on:

- specific collaborators
- software platforms
- AI models
- programming languages
- vendors

Organizational capability shall remain transferable.

---

# Organizational Growth

Growth shall occur through:

- improved capability
- stronger governance
- reusable knowledge
- standardized work
- controlled evolution

Growth shall not depend solely on increasing headcount or adopting new technologies.

---

# Responsibilities

Francis

Defines strategic direction.

Approves organizational architecture.

---

Nex

Designs organizational workflows.

Orchestrates engineering work.

Coordinates specialized collaborators.

Improves organizational capability through controlled evolution.

---

Specialized Collaborators

Execute within their approved domains.

Contribute technical expertise.

Return validated knowledge to the repository.

---

# Organizational Principle

The FDG Ecosystem is engineered as an organization first and implemented through technology second.

---

# Continuous Improvement

The organizational architecture shall evolve through evidence, operational experience, and controlled governance while preserving organizational continuity.

---

# Governing Principle

The enduring competitive advantage of the FDG Ecosystem is its engineered organizational capability.

---

End of Standard
