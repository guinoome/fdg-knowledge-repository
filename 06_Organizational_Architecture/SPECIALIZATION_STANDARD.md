# Purpose

This standard defines how specialization is established, governed, and utilized within the FDG Ecosystem.

Specialization enables deeper expertise while maintaining organizational coordination through Engineering Intelligence Architecture.

---

# Core Principle

Capability grows through specialization.

Coordination preserves unity.

The organization shall optimize for both.

---

# Objectives

Specialization shall:

- deepen engineering expertise
- improve quality of outcomes
- reduce cognitive overload
- increase execution efficiency
- support organizational scalability
- strengthen reusable organizational capability

# Principles of Specialization

Every specialization shall:

- have a clearly defined scope
- develop deep domain expertise
- follow organizational governance
- contribute reusable knowledge
- collaborate through defined interfaces

No specialization operates independently of the organization.

---

# Engineering Domains

Specializations may include, but are not limited to:

- Mechanical Engineering
- Electrical Engineering
- Civil Engineering
- Structural Engineering
- Software Engineering
- Data Engineering
- Financial Engineering
- Business Systems
- Marketing Engineering
- Operations Engineering

New domains shall be introduced through controlled governance when organizational capability requires them.

---

# Scope Boundaries

Each specialization shall define:

- responsibilities
- decision authority
- interfaces
- dependencies
- expected deliverables
- quality requirements

Clear boundaries reduce overlap while encouraging collaboration.

---

# Cross-Domain Collaboration

Engineering challenges often span multiple domains.

Cross-domain work shall be coordinated through Engineering Intelligence Architecture.

Specialists shall contribute expertise without assuming responsibilities outside their approved scope unless explicitly authorized.

---

# Organizational Independence

Specializations are organizational capabilities.

They are not tied to:

- individuals
- AI models
- software platforms
- vendors

Implementations may change while the specialization remains.

---

# Responsibilities

## Francis

Approves new engineering domains.

Defines strategic capability priorities.

---

## Nex

Identifies specialization needs.

Coordinates domain collaboration.

Detects capability gaps.

Recommends organizational improvements.

---

## Specialized Collaborators

Develop and apply expertise within approved domains.

Produce validated engineering outputs.

Return lessons learned to the Knowledge Repository.

---

# Specialization Quality Checklist

Before establishing a specialization, verify:

✓ Organizational need exists.

✓ Scope is clearly defined.

✓ Interfaces are identified.

✓ Authority is assigned.

✓ Knowledge contribution is expected.

✓ Long-term organizational value is justified.

---

# Organizational Principle

Specialization increases organizational capability only when coordinated through a common engineering operating model.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Engineering domains shall evolve as organizational capability expands, while preserving governance, interoperability, and organizational cohesion.

---

# Governing Principle

The FDG Ecosystem grows stronger by increasing specialized capability without fragmenting organizational intelligence.
