- informing HR interview-preparation materials and candidate guidance.

## Evaluation Requirements Before Adoption

If the guide is acquired or its contents become available for review:

1. Inspect all material before extracting or adapting its methods.
2. Separate generally useful interview techniques from generic scripts, unsupported promises and role-specific advice.
3. Test relevance across job families and seniority levels; do not treat one answer template as suitable for every role.
4. Align candidate preparation with the approved job definition and the requesting department's functional/technical selection criteria.
5. Preserve structured, consistent and job-related assessment. Candidate coaching must not replace interviewer scoring, evidence capture, reference checks or authorized selection decisions.
6. Check copyright and licensing terms before copying, reproducing, distributing or incorporating any substantial content into FDG-owned materials.
7. Record the review outcome as ADOPT / ADAPT / REJECT / UNVERIFIED, with evidence and an owner.

## FDG Hiring Governance

This reference must remain subordinate to the canonical hiring ownership in [[06_Organizational_Architecture/NEX-STD-125_ENTERPRISE_FUNCTION_AND_DEPARTMENT_STANDARD|NEX-STD-125 Enterprise Function and Department Standard]]:

- HR owns the hiring process, interview coordination and records.
- The requesting department owns functional and technical selection criteria and participates in interviews.
- Finance validates budget and compensation affordability.
- Legal supplies employment-law requirements.
- Approval authority approves according to delegation.

The canonical hiring chain remains:

Workforce Need → Approved Requisition → Job Definition → Sourcing → Screening → Department Interview / Technical Assessment → Selection → Offer / Approval → Onboarding.
