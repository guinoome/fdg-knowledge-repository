# Purpose

This catalog completes the minimum Very Strong organizational-knowledge baseline by defining the critical records, controls, competencies, KPIs, major risks, and learning/escalation expectations of every canonical FDG enterprise function.

# Enterprise Function Control Catalog

## 1. Executive, Strategy and Governance

Critical records: enterprise strategy; approved objectives; authority matrix; policy register; executive decisions; investment approvals; risk acceptances.

Core controls: reserved authority; delegated limits; conflict-of-interest disclosure; decision traceability; periodic governance review.

Competencies: strategy, governance, capital allocation, organizational design, decision-making, risk oversight.

KPIs: strategic-objective completion; decision cycle time; overdue executive actions; portfolio value realization; unresolved critical risks.

Major risks: founder bottleneck, unclear authority, conflicting priorities, uncontrolled commitments, weak succession.

Escalation and learning: unresolved cross-functional conflicts and material risk acceptance escalate here; decisions feed governance standards and lessons learned.

## 2. Engineering and Technical

Critical records: calculations; designs; specifications; equipment/asset records; technical reviews; test/commissioning evidence; maintenance/reliability records; technical acceptance.

Core controls: design/review separation where required; calculation validation; approved methods; revision control; technical acceptance; competency authorization.

Competencies: discipline engineering, reliability, technical analysis, standards, asset systems, verification and technical communication.

KPIs: reliability, defects/rework, technical review closure, first-pass acceptance, asset performance, engineering response time.

Major risks: unsafe or incorrect design, undocumented assumptions, technical obsolescence, key-person dependency, poor acceptance.

Escalation and learning: material technical uncertainty escalates to appropriate professional authority; validated lessons enter FEIS/CORE and standards.

## 3. Operations and Service Delivery

Critical records: work/service orders; schedules; dispatch records; SOP evidence; service logs; handovers; incidents; closeout records.

Core controls: authorized work; scheduling/capacity control; handover requirements; exception escalation; service acceptance; continuity plans.

Competencies: operations control, service delivery, coordination, troubleshooting, customer handling, SOP execution.

KPIs: SLA attainment; backlog; turnaround time; downtime; first-time completion; utilization; service recovery.

Major risks: missed service, uncontrolled backlog, handoff failure, capacity mismatch, repeated operational incidents.

Escalation and learning: recurring failures feed Engineering, Quality, Risk and FSvIS improvement loops.

## 4. Commercial, Sales and Business Development

Critical records: leads; qualified opportunities; pipeline; proposals; quotations; commercial assumptions; approval records; contracts/handoffs; account plans.

Core controls: opportunity qualification; pricing/margin approval thresholds; proposal review; commitment authority; contract handoff; conflict/discount controls.

Competencies: consultative sales, proposal development, negotiation, business development, account management, commercial reasoning.

KPIs: qualified pipeline; win rate; proposal conversion; sales cycle; booked revenue; gross-margin quality; renewals/expansion.

Major risks: unprofitable commitments, unauthorized discounts, overpromising, weak handoff, concentration risk.

Escalation and learning: lost/won analyses feed pricing, marketing, service design and product strategy.

## 5. Marketing and Growth

Critical records: brand standards; campaign plans; content register; channel calendar; lead-source data; campaign results; marketing claims evidence.

Core controls: brand approval; claim validation; consent/privacy control; campaign budget; source attribution; lead-handoff rules.

Competencies: positioning, content, digital marketing, market research, analytics, campaign operations, brand management.

KPIs: marketing-qualified leads; acquisition cost; conversion by channel; attributed pipeline; engagement quality; campaign ROI.

Major risks: misleading claims, poor brand consistency, wasted spend, weak attribution, privacy violations.

Escalation and learning: campaign evidence feeds Commercial, Product/Project, FBIS and FEXIS.

## 6. Finance, Accounting and Treasury

Critical records: chart of accounts; journals; invoices; receivables; payables; bank records; reconciliations; budgets; forecasts; tax records; financial statements; payroll payment records.

Core controls: approval matrix; segregation of duties; three-way match; reconciliation; close checklist; journal review; bank/payment authorization; budget control; audit trail.

Competencies: accounting, treasury, FP&A, tax coordination, management accounting, financial controls, reporting.

KPIs: close cycle; reconciliation exceptions; DSO; payable aging; cash forecast accuracy; budget variance; working capital; gross margin; control exceptions.

Major risks: misstated records, cash loss, fraud, late filing, weak collections, liquidity shortfall, uncontrolled spending.

Escalation and learning: material misstatement/control exceptions escalate to Executive and Audit; trend learning feeds FBIS and planning.

## 7. Human Resources and People Operations

Critical records: workforce plan; requisitions; job descriptions; candidate records; interview/assessment records; offers; employee master; compensation/benefits; leave/attendance; performance; training/competency; disciplinary and separation records.

Core controls: approved headcount; standardized hiring; background/reference controls where justified; compensation authorization; personnel-record privacy; access triggers; performance-cycle control; offboarding checklist.

Competencies: recruitment, HR operations, compensation/benefits, employee relations, L&D, performance management, workforce planning, HR analytics.

KPIs: time to fill; offer acceptance; turnover; retention; absenteeism; onboarding completion; performance-cycle completion; competency gaps; training effectiveness.

Major risks: poor hiring, unauthorized compensation, employment-law breaches, weak records, access not revoked, skill gaps, key-person dependency.

Escalation and learning: legal issues to FLIS/qualified counsel; budget issues to Finance; technical competency to department owner; recurring people issues feed workforce planning.

## 8. Procurement, Purchasing and Supply Chain

Critical records: PR/SRF; sourcing events; RFQs/tenders; bids; comparison sheets; supplier qualification; approved vendor list; POs; contracts; delivery/receiving records; supplier scorecards; supply-risk register.

Core controls: approved requisition; competitive sourcing threshold; conflict-of-interest declaration; supplier qualification; delegated PO approval; specification traceability; receiving/acceptance; invoice match.

Competencies: sourcing, negotiation, commercial evaluation, supplier management, supply risk, purchasing controls, logistics coordination.

KPIs: procurement cycle time; competitive sourcing coverage; supplier on-time delivery; supplier quality; emergency purchase rate; PO compliance; savings/value realization.

Major risks: favoritism/fraud, supplier failure, poor quality, excessive price, delivery delay, unauthorized purchase, single-source dependency.

Escalation and learning: supplier issues feed Engineering/Operations, Finance, Legal, HSE, Quality and future sourcing strategy.

## 9. Project and Portfolio Management

Critical records: charter; scope; schedule; budget baseline; RAID log; change log; status reports; stage-gate decisions; closeout; benefits record.

Core controls: approved initiation; baseline control; change control; milestone acceptance; cross-functional ownership; project closeout; benefits review.

Competencies: project management, planning, scheduling, cost control, risk/issue management, stakeholder coordination, portfolio management.

KPIs: schedule variance; cost variance; milestone reliability; change frequency; risk closure; benefits realization; closeout completeness.

Major risks: scope drift, schedule/cost overrun, unclear ownership, unmanaged dependencies, incomplete closeout.

Escalation and learning: lessons feed FPJIS, Engineering, Service, Finance and governance.

## 10. Quality and Continuous Improvement

Critical records: QMS standards; inspection/verification records; NCRs; CAPA; quality audits; process metrics; improvement register.

Core controls: acceptance criteria; verification/validation; NCR classification; root-cause requirements; CAPA effectiveness; document/change control.

Competencies: quality management, QA/QC, process improvement, root cause, statistics, verification, CAPA.

KPIs: defect rate; first-pass yield; repeat NCR; CAPA closure; CAPA effectiveness; customer quality complaints; process capability.

Major risks: repeated defects, ineffective CAPA, inspection gaps, quality drift, metric gaming.

Escalation and learning: systemic quality issues to process owners, Risk and Executive; independent assurance remains with Audit.

## 11. Enterprise Risk Management

Critical records: risk taxonomy; enterprise risk register; assessments; treatment plans; control map; risk reports; acceptance records.

Core controls: defined risk criteria; assigned risk owner; treatment due dates; residual-risk review; escalation thresholds; formal risk acceptance.

Competencies: enterprise risk, control design, scenario analysis, facilitation, risk reporting.

KPIs: overdue high risks; treatment completion; control coverage; residual-risk trend; recurring risk events.

Major risks: unowned risks, superficial scoring, stale register, weak escalation, risk acceptance without authority.

Escalation and learning: material residual risk to Executive; Audit independently assesses framework effectiveness.

## 12. Internal Audit and Assurance

Critical records: audit universe; risk-based plan; audit programs; evidence register; findings; CAPA verification; reports; closure record; audit history.

Core controls: independence; evidence standards; auditor conflict disclosure; finding classification; remediation ownership separation; closure verification.

Competencies: auditing, controls, evidence, data analysis, process assessment, interviewing, report writing.

KPIs: audit-plan completion; high-risk findings; repeat findings; overdue CAPA; closure verification quality; control-effectiveness trend.

Major risks: loss of independence, weak evidence, auditing own work, superficial closure, untracked findings.

Escalation and learning: critical findings to governing authority; systemic lessons feed FAIS and domain owners.

## 13. Legal and Compliance

Critical records: legal source register; obligation register; contracts; clause library; compliance register; IP register; privacy records; legal-risk register; disputes/claims; regulatory-change records.

Core controls: source/provenance verification; applicability assessment; contract review; obligation ownership; legal escalation; privileged/sensitive handling.

Competencies: legal research, contract analysis, regulatory interpretation, compliance management, IP/privacy awareness, legal-risk analysis.

KPIs: obligations mapped; review cycle; overdue compliance actions; regulatory-change response; unresolved legal risks; contract exceptions.

Major risks: unauthorized legal conclusions, missed obligations, weak contracts, IP loss, privacy breach, unresolved claims.

Escalation and learning: matters requiring licensed authority escalate externally; confirmed interpretations feed FLIS.

## 14. Technology, Data and Cybersecurity

Critical records: asset inventory; service catalog; architecture; access register; incidents; vulnerabilities; backups; recovery tests; change records; data models; integration records.

Core controls: least privilege; MFA/secrets control; secure change management; backups; monitoring; incident response; recovery; data governance; access review.

Competencies: IT operations, cloud/platform, cybersecurity, data architecture, integration, support, resilience.

KPIs: availability; incident rate; MTTR; vulnerability age; backup success; recovery-test success; access-review completion; data-quality exceptions.

Major risks: outage, data loss, unauthorized access, vendor lock-in, weak recovery, insecure automation.

Escalation and learning: incidents feed FSIS/FPIS/FWAIS and enterprise risk.

## 15. Corporate Services and Administration

Critical records: administrative requests; correspondence; meeting/actions; travel records; common supplies/assets; renewal calendar; office records; service-provider records.

Core controls: request ownership; record retention; administrative approvals; common-asset custody; renewal reminders; segregation from specialist functions.

Competencies: administration, records coordination, scheduling, logistics, communication, service coordination.

KPIs: request turnaround; backlog; record completeness; renewal timeliness; service satisfaction; administrative cost.

Major risks: lost records, missed renewals, unclear ownership, specialist responsibilities hidden inside Admin, inefficient coordination.

Escalation and learning: specialist matters route to responsible department rather than being absorbed by Admin.

## 16. Health, Safety, Environment and Sustainability

Critical records: HSE policy; hazard/risk assessments; permits; incident/near-miss reports; inspections; emergency plans; contractor HSE records; training records; environmental records; corrective actions.

Core controls: hazard assessment; permit-to-work where applicable; incident investigation; contractor safety qualification; emergency readiness; compliance monitoring; corrective-action closure.

Competencies: occupational safety, environmental compliance, incident investigation, emergency planning, risk assessment, safety training.

KPIs: incidents; near misses; corrective-action closure; compliance status; training completion; contractor safety; environmental deviations.

Major risks: injury/fatality, environmental harm, regulatory breach, weak contractor controls, repeated hazards.

Escalation and learning: serious incidents escalate to Executive, Legal and relevant authorities; lessons feed Engineering, Operations, HR, Procurement and FAIS.

# Governing Rule

Every enterprise function shall maintain the records and controls appropriate to its actual scale and legal context. The catalog defines the organizational knowledge baseline; operational implementation must be proportionate, evidence-based and governed.
