# Purpose

This standard defines Engineering Capability as the primary organizational asset of the FDG Ecosystem.

Engineering capability is the organization's ability to consistently achieve engineering objectives through governed processes, reusable knowledge, effective collaboration, and continuous improvement.

---

# Core Principle

Engineering capability is the product.

Projects are evidence of capability.

Technology enables capability.

Knowledge strengthens capability.

The organization preserves capability.

---

# Objectives

Engineering Capability shall:

- produce consistent engineering outcomes
- reduce dependence on individuals
- improve decision quality
- enable specialization
- accelerate organizational learning
- remain transferable across technologies and collaborators

# Capability Components

Engineering Capability consists of:

## Governance

Defines authority and organizational direction.

---

## Organizational Design

Defines responsibilities, roles, and collaboration.

---

## Knowledge

Provides reusable engineering intelligence.

---

## Processes

Standardizes repeatable engineering work.

---

## People

Apply engineering judgment and expertise.

---

## Technology

Implements and accelerates engineering capability.

Technology shall support—not define—the capability.

---

# Capability Development

Engineering capability improves through:

- operational experience
- validated lessons learned
- controlled experimentation
- standardization
- knowledge reuse
- continuous improvement

---

# Capability Assessment

Engineering capability should be evaluated against the following criteria:

- Correctness
- Reliability
- Maintainability
- Scalability
- Security
- Reusability
- Organizational Learning
- Cost Effectiveness

Cost shall be evaluated only after engineering quality has been established.

---

# Capability Preservation

Engineering capability shall be preserved by:

- documenting decisions
- governing standards
- maintaining the knowledge repository
- developing reusable templates
- evolving organizational processes

The organization shall never rely solely on individual memory.

---

# Organizational Principle

The FDG Ecosystem is designed to continuously increase engineering capability rather than simply complete engineering projects.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Capability improvements shall be evidence-based, measurable where practical, and incorporated into organizational standards through controlled evolution.

---

# Governing Principle

The long-term value of the FDG Ecosystem is measured by the engineering capability it builds, preserves, and continuously improves.
