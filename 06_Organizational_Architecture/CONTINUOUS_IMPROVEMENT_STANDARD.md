# Purpose

This standard defines how the FDG Ecosystem learns, improves, and evolves through controlled organizational learning.

Continuous improvement converts operational experience into reusable engineering intelligence.

---

# Core Principle

Experience alone does not create capability.

Experience must be captured, evaluated, integrated, and reused.

---

# Objectives

Continuous improvement shall:

- increase engineering capability
- prevent repeated mistakes
- preserve institutional knowledge
- improve standards
- strengthen decision quality
- enable controlled evolution

---

# Improvement Classification

All proposed improvements shall be classified as:

## Correction

Fixes an identified problem.

Example:

Removing incorrect information.

---

## Optimization

Improves existing performance.

Example:

Reducing execution time.

---

## Evolution

Changes an approved approach based on new evidence.

Example:

Previous decision:
"Obsidian is the knowledge management system."

New evidence:
"A hybrid architecture improves scalability."

Evolution:
"Obsidian remains the human knowledge layer while structured intelligence systems extend capability."

---

## Innovation

Introduces a new capability.

Example:

New engineering automation capability.

---

# Controlled Evolution Rules

Approved systems shall not be redesigned from memory.

Before changing an approved standard:

1. Review current approved knowledge.

2. Identify existing decision baseline.

3. Evaluate new evidence.

4. Document reasoning.

5. Preserve historical context.

6. Update version history.

7. Obtain appropriate approval.

## New Evidence

---

## Evaluation

## Decision

---

## Impact

Affected standards, workflows, and systems.

# Improvement Quality Checklist

Before implementing improvement:

✓ Current baseline reviewed.

✓ Existing decisions identified.

✓ New evidence documented.

✓ Tradeoffs evaluated.

✓ Impact assessed.

✓ Approval obtained.

✓ Knowledge updated.

---

# Anti-Pattern Prevention

The FDG Ecosystem shall avoid:

## Knowledge Reset

Recreating decisions that already exist.

---

## Improvement Without Evidence

Changing systems based only on preference.

---

## Documentation Accumulation

Creating notes without integration.

---

## Technology-Driven Redesign

Changing architecture because a new tool exists.

---

# Organizational Learning Principle

The organization shall continuously improve through evidence-based evolution, not uncontrolled change.

---

# Governing Principle

Every completed engineering activity should make the organization more capable than before.
