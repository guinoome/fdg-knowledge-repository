# Purpose

This standard defines engineering roles within the FDG Ecosystem.

Engineering roles describe responsibilities within the organizational architecture rather than specific job titles, software platforms, or individuals.

---

# Core Principle

Roles define responsibility.

People, collaborators, and technologies fulfill roles.

Roles endure even when implementations change.

---

# Objectives

Engineering roles shall:

- establish clear responsibilities
- reduce ambiguity
- support specialization
- enable delegation
- improve coordination
- preserve organizational continuity

---

# Engineering Role Model

Engineering work is organized into the following roles:

Engineering Vision

↓

Engineering Governance

↓

Engineering Intelligence Architecture

↓

Specialized Engineering

↓

Engineering Execution

↓

Engineering Review

↓

Knowledge Integration

Each role has one primary responsibility.

---

# Engineering Roles

## Engineering Vision

Defines long-term objectives.

Determines what the organization should achieve.

Responsible for strategic direction.

---

## Engineering Governance

Maintains engineering principles.

Approves standards.

Approves organizational evolution.

Protects engineering integrity.

---

## Engineering Intelligence Architecture

Transforms engineering objectives into structured work.

Coordinates specialists.

Determines sequencing.

Manages dependencies.

Maintains organizational context.

Converts execution into reusable intelligence.

---

## Specialized Engineering

Provides domain expertise.

Examples include:

Mechanical Engineering

Electrical Engineering

Civil Engineering

Software Engineering

Data Engineering

Financial Engineering

Marketing Engineering

Additional specializations may be introduced through controlled evolution.

---

## Engineering Execution

Implements approved work packages.

Produces engineering deliverables.

Reports execution outcomes.

---

## Engineering Review

Validates engineering quality.

Evaluates compliance.

Confirms readiness for approval.

---

## Knowledge Integration

Captures validated outcomes.

Updates organizational knowledge.

Supports controlled evolution.

Strengthens engineering capability.

---

# Role Independence

Roles shall remain independent of:

individual people

specific AI models

software platforms

vendors

organizational structure

Implementations may change.

Roles remain stable.

---

# Multiple Implementations

One individual may perform multiple roles.

One role may be fulfilled by multiple collaborators.

Responsibilities remain unchanged regardless of implementation.

# Organizational Principle

Engineering roles define organizational capability.

They are responsibilities, not identities.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Engineering roles shall evolve through organizational learning while preserving clear separation of responsibilities.

---

# Governing Principle
