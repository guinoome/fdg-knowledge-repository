# Purpose

This standard defines how work is delegated within the FDG Ecosystem.

Delegation enables scalable execution while preserving governance, accountability, organizational capability, and engineering quality.

---

# Core Principle

Delegate execution.

Retain accountability.

Preserve governance.

---

# Objectives

Delegation shall:

- improve organizational leverage
- enable specialization
- reduce unnecessary coordination
- maintain engineering quality
- preserve accountability
- increase organizational capability

# Delegation Principles

Delegation shall:

- begin with a clearly defined objective
- assign complete work packages where practical
- include required constraints
- define expected outcomes
- establish acceptance criteria
- preserve decision authority boundaries

---

# What May Be Delegated

Examples include:

- research
- engineering analysis
- software implementation
- calculations
- documentation
- reporting
- validation
- automation
- knowledge extraction

Delegation shall focus on outcomes rather than individual tasks whenever practical.

---

# What Shall Not Be Delegated

The following remain under approved authority unless explicitly reassigned through governance:

- constitutional changes
- engineering principles
- organizational architecture
- governance approval
- strategic direction
- final approval of organizational knowledge

---

# Delegation Levels

## Level 1 — Assisted Execution

Recommendations only.

No autonomous execution.

---

## Level 2 — Guided Execution

Execution requires explicit approval before completion.

---

## Level 3 — Managed Execution

Approved work packages may be executed independently within defined constraints.

Outputs require review.

---

## Level 4 — Trusted Execution

Routine work packages may execute autonomously within approved authority.

Exceptions are escalated.

---

## Level 5 — Adaptive Execution

The executing collaborator may optimize execution methods while remaining within governance, authority, and work package constraints.

Strategic intent shall not be altered.

---

# Delegation Requirements

Every delegation shall specify:

- engineering objective
- scope
- constraints
- expected deliverables
- acceptance criteria
- escalation conditions

Ambiguity shall be minimized before execution begins.

---

# Escalation

Delegated work shall be escalated when:

- authority limits are reached
- governance conflicts arise
- requirements become ambiguous
- significant organizational impact is identified
- engineering risk exceeds approved tolerance
