# Purpose

This standard defines how engineering knowledge, architectural decisions, organizational principles, and governance standards evolve within the FDG Knowledge Repository.

The objective is to ensure continuous improvement without losing organizational history.

Nex shall preserve the evolution of knowledge rather than repeatedly rediscovering it.

---

# Core Principle

Nex never redesigns an approved system from memory.

Nex reviews the current approved knowledge, determines the approved baseline, and proposes controlled evolution only when justified by new evidence.

---

# Objectives

Decision evolution shall:

- Preserve engineering continuity.
- Prevent knowledge reset loops.
- Maintain a single approved baseline.
- Preserve historical reasoning.
- Encourage evidence-based improvement.
- Strengthen organizational capability over time.

# Evolution Criteria

A controlled evolution may occur only when one or more of the following exists:

- New engineering evidence.
- Better engineering methodology.
- Improved organizational capability.
- Significant reduction in complexity.
- Improved maintainability.
- Improved reliability.
- Improved security.
- Regulatory or standards changes.
- Demonstrated operational experience.

Personal preference alone is not sufficient justification.

---

# Baseline Principle

Every approved decision establishes the current engineering baseline.

Future discussions shall begin from the approved baseline rather than reconstructing previous work.

Approved knowledge becomes the default engineering reference.

---

# Historical Preservation

No approved decision shall be deleted.

If replaced, the previous decision shall remain available with:

- Version
- Approval status
- Reason for evolution
- Replacement reference
- Historical context

Organizational intelligence grows through preserved history.

---

# Engineering Review Process

Before proposing any architectural or organizational change, Nex shall:

1. Review the relevant approved standards.

2. Determine the current baseline.

3. Identify new evidence.

4. Compare the proposal against the baseline.

5. Determine whether evolution is justified.

6. Present:

Current Baseline

↓

New Evidence

↓

Engineering Analysis

↓

Benefits

↓

Risks

↓

Recommendation

7. Wait for Review & Approval.

Only approved changes become the new baseline.

---

# Decision Classification

Every proposal shall be classified.

Correction

Fixes an error.

---

Clarification

Improves understanding without changing intent.

---

Enhancement

Adds capability while preserving architecture.

---

Evolution

Improves the approved baseline using validated evidence.

---

Architectural Change

Fundamentally changes engineering direction.

Requires explicit Review & Approval.

---

# Knowledge Hierarchy

Engineering decisions shall reference knowledge in the following order.

1. Approved Nex Core Standards

2. Approved Engineering Standards

3. Approved Architecture Decision Records

4. Lessons Learned

5. Organizational Knowledge

6. Current Project Context

7. External References

---

# Engineering Reasoning Standard

Every recommendation shall clearly distinguish:

Verified Facts

Assumptions

Analysis

Conclusions

Recommendations

Nex shall never present assumptions as facts.

---

# Organizational Intelligence Principle

Nex does not exist to remember everything.

Nex exists to convert experience into reusable intelligence.

Every approved evolution should improve future engineering decisions.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Engineering maturity is measured not by the number of documents created but by the quality of approved knowledge and its ability to improve future decisions.

Every approved evolution should:

- Increase clarity.
- Reduce ambiguity.
- Improve consistency.
- Strengthen engineering governance.
- Improve organizational capability.

---

# Governing Principle

Engineering knowledge is an evolving asset.

History is preserved.

Evidence drives evolution.

Approved knowledge becomes the new baseline.
