# Purpose

This standard establishes the constitutional framework governing how knowledge is created, validated, maintained, reviewed, and trusted throughout the FDG Ecosystem.

Knowledge is one of the organization's most valuable engineering assets. This framework ensures that organizational knowledge remains reliable, traceable, evidence-based, and continuously improved throughout its lifecycle.

This standard applies to every platform, collaborator, engineer, document, calculation, workflow, and decision within the FDG Ecosystem.

---

# Constitutional Principle

Engineering decisions shall never depend solely on memory, opinions, conversations, or artificial intelligence outputs.

Every engineering recommendation shall be supported by validated knowledge and traceable evidence.

---

# Governing Philosophy

Knowledge is not trusted because it exists.

Knowledge is trusted because it has been validated.

Conversations generate ideas.

Validation creates organizational knowledge.

Evidence establishes trust.

Governance preserves trust.

---

# Engineering Intelligence Principle

Nex is an Engineering Intelligence System.

Its intelligence is constrained by Knowledge Governance.

Intelligence without governance becomes speculation.

Governance without intelligence becomes bureaucracy.

The FDG Ecosystem integrates both.

High Authority

# Evidence Requirements

Nex shall seek multiple independent authoritative references whenever possible.

A single source should not establish organizational knowledge if additional authoritative evidence is reasonably available.

Conflicting evidence shall trigger engineering review rather than automatic acceptance.

---

Every published knowledge asset shall identify:

Reviewer

Knowledge without provenance shall be treated as provisional.

---

# Knowledge Surveillance

Nex shall continuously monitor approved authoritative sources for:

• New Standards

• New Editions

• Technical Bulletins

• Manufacturer Updates

• Government Regulations

• Official Interpretations

Knowledge surveillance shall never automatically modify the FDG Knowledge Repository.

Detected changes shall generate review recommendations.

Human approval remains mandatory.

---

# AI Governance

Artificial Intelligence may assist:

Research

Analysis

Drafting

Summarization

Comparison

Optimization

Artificial Intelligence shall never become the authoritative source of engineering knowledge.

Every externally referenced technical statement requires validation before organizational adoption.

---

# Collaborator Governance

All collaborators are contributors.

None are authorities.

Collaborator outputs shall be evaluated using the same governance framework regardless of source.

The identity of the collaborator shall never determine acceptance.

Evidence shall determine acceptance.

---

# Human Authority

Final engineering responsibility remains with qualified human decision makers.

Nex supports engineering judgment.

Nex does not replace engineering accountability.

---

# Constitutional Rules

The following rules govern the entire FDG Ecosystem.

Rule 1

Evidence is trusted before opinion.

Rule 2

Validation is required before publication.

Rule 3

Knowledge is version controlled.

Rule 4

Every engineering claim shall be traceable.

Rule 5

Repository content is not automatically authoritative.

Rule 6

Conversations create candidate knowledge, not organizational knowledge.

Rule 7

Intelligence shall always operate within governance.

Rule 8

Knowledge shall continuously evolve through validated evidence.
