# Purpose

This standard defines the architecture, responsibilities, operating principles, and governance of the FDG Decision Intelligence Engine.

Decision Intelligence transforms engineering analysis into structured, transparent, evidence-based decision support while ensuring that engineering responsibility remains with qualified human decision makers.

Decision Intelligence supports decisions.

It never makes organizational decisions autonomously.

---

# Mission

Enable consistent, transparent, and defensible engineering decisions through structured evaluation of evidence, alternatives, risks, constraints, and organizational objectives.

---

The Decision Intelligence Engine governs:

- Engineering Decision Support
- Alternative Evaluation

---

# Design Principles

## Principle 1

Every engineering decision shall be evidence-based.

Unsupported opinions shall never be presented as organizational decisions.

---

## Principle 2

Every decision shall identify available alternatives.

Decision Intelligence shall not evaluate only one option unless no feasible alternatives exist.

---

## Principle 3

Every decision shall document its rationale.

Future engineers shall understand why a decision was made.

---

## Principle 4

Decision Intelligence shall distinguish facts from assumptions.

Engineering decisions shall clearly separate:

- Verified Facts
- Assumptions
- Constraints
- Risks
- Engineering Judgment

---

## Principle 5

Decision Intelligence supports engineers.

Final authority always remains with qualified human decision makers.

# Decision Framework

Every engineering decision shall evaluate:

## Objective

What problem is being solved?

---

## Constraints

What limitations exist?

Examples:

- Budget
- Time
- Space
- Standards
- Regulations
- Resources

---

## Evidence

What verified information supports the decision?

---

## Alternatives

What feasible options exist?

---

## Risks

What are the consequences of each alternative?

---

## Tradeoffs

What benefits are gained?

What compromises are required?

---

## Recommendation

Which alternative provides the most defensible engineering outcome?

---

# Decision Record

Every engineering decision shall generate a permanent decision record.

Each record shall include:

- Decision ID
- Date
- Decision Owner
- Engineering Discipline
- Decision Objective
- Alternatives Evaluated
- Selected Alternative
- Supporting Evidence
- Applicable Standards
- Assumptions
- Risks
- Tradeoffs
- Confidence Level
- Approval Status
- Lessons Learned (when available)

Decision records become part of the FDG organizational memory.

---

# Decision Confidence

Every recommendation shall identify confidence.

## High

Supported by validated evidence and multiple authoritative sources.

---

## Moderate

Supported by sufficient engineering evidence.

Some uncertainty remains.

---

## Low

Limited supporting evidence.

Engineering validation required.

---

## Unknown

Insufficient information.

Decision should be deferred until adequate evidence becomes available.

---

# Decision Traceability

Every engineering decision shall be traceable to:

- Engineering calculations
- Supporting evidence
- Applicable standards
- Organizational knowledge
- Engineering assumptions
- Review findings
- Approval history

Decision traceability shall never be optional.

---

# Decision Bias Management

The Decision Intelligence Engine shall actively identify potential bias.

Examples include:

- Confirmation Bias
- Availability Bias
- Authority Bias
- Cost Bias
- Anchoring Bias
- Recency Bias

Potential bias shall be documented when identified.

---

# Explainability

Every recommendation shall answer:

- What decision is recommended?
- Why is it recommended?
- What evidence supports it?
- What alternatives were evaluated?
- What risks remain?
- What assumptions exist?
- What confidence exists?

# Knowledge Governance

Decision Intelligence shall comply with:

- NEX-STD-003 Decision Evolution Standard
- NEX-STD-006 Knowledge Governance Framework

Engineering decisions become organizational knowledge only after governance approval.

---

# Human Authority

Decision Intelligence provides structured decision support.

It shall never:

- Approve engineering designs
- Override engineering authority
- Replace professional engineering judgment

Human decision makers remain accountable.
