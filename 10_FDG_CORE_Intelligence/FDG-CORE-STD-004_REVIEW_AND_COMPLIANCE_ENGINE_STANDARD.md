# Purpose

This standard defines the architecture, responsibilities, operating principles, and governance of the FDG Review & Compliance Engine.

The Review & Compliance Engine verifies that engineering outputs comply with applicable engineering standards, organizational requirements, regulations, and project-specific criteria before recommendations are accepted.

The engine evaluates compliance.

It does not replace engineering review.

---

# Mission

Provide transparent, evidence-based verification that engineering outputs satisfy applicable standards, regulations, organizational requirements, and engineering best practices.

---

The Review & Compliance Engine governs:

- Engineering Design Review
- Standards Compliance

- Internal Standard Verification

---

# Design Principles

## Principle 1

Review shall always be evidence-based.

Compliance shall never be determined by assumptions or unsupported opinions.

---

## Principle 2

Every compliance decision shall identify the governing requirement.

Examples:

- ISO
- ASME
- ASHRAE
- NFPA
- IEC
- IEEE
- Philippine Laws
- Manufacturer Documentation
- FDG Standards

---

## Principle 3

Compliance verification shall be transparent.

Every identified issue shall reference the specific requirement being evaluated.

---

## Principle 4

Unknown compliance shall never be reported as compliant.

Insufficient evidence shall be clearly identified.

---

## Principle 5

Compliance review supports engineering judgment.

Final engineering approval remains with qualified human decision makers.

# Compliance Verification Process

The Review & Compliance Engine shall verify:

- Applicable standards identified
- Correct engineering methodology
- Proper calculations
- Supporting evidence available
- Required documentation complete
- Organizational requirements satisfied

---

# Compliance Status

Every reviewed item shall receive one of the following classifications.

## Compliant

All applicable requirements satisfied.

---

## Conditionally Compliant

Minor issues identified.

Engineering judgment required.

---

## Non-Compliant

Mandatory requirements not satisfied.

Corrective action required.

---

## Insufficient Evidence

Unable to determine compliance due to missing information.

Additional evidence required.

---

## Not Applicable

Requirement does not apply to the current engineering scope.

---

# Nonconformity Management

Every nonconformity shall identify:

- Requirement violated
- Evidence
- Engineering impact
- Risk level
- Recommended corrective action
- Responsible reviewer

---

# Risk Classification

Engineering findings shall be classified according to risk.

## Critical

Immediate engineering action required.

Potential safety, regulatory, or operational consequences.

---

## Major

Significant engineering concern requiring corrective action.

---

## Moderate

Engineering improvement recommended.

Does not immediately compromise system integrity.

---

## Minor

Editorial or documentation improvement.

No significant engineering impact.

# Human Oversight

Engineering approval remains the responsibility of qualified engineers.

The Review & Compliance Engine provides technical verification and decision support.

It shall never independently approve engineering work.
