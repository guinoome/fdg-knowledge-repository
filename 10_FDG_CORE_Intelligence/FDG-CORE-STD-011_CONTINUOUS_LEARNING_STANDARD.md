# Purpose

This standard defines the architecture, responsibilities, operating principles, and governance of the FDG Continuous Learning Engine.

The Continuous Learning Engine transforms validated engineering experience, approved organizational decisions, and verified external knowledge into continuous organizational improvement.

Continuous Learning improves organizational capability.

It never bypasses engineering governance.

---

# Mission

Continuously strengthen the FDG Ecosystem by converting validated engineering experience into reusable organizational knowledge while preserving governance, traceability, and engineering accountability.

---

# Scope

The Continuous Learning Engine governs:

- Lessons Learned
- Organizational Learning
- Knowledge Improvement
- Engineering Feedback
- Decision Evolution
- Process Improvement
- Engineering Best Practices
- Organizational Capability Development
- Continuous Improvement

---

# Objectives

The Continuous Learning Engine shall:

- Capture engineering knowledge
- Improve organizational capability
- Prevent repeated mistakes
- Preserve engineering experience
- Strengthen decision quality
- Improve engineering standards
- Support sustainable organizational growth

---

# Design Principles

## Principle 1

Learning shall be evidence-based.

Experience alone shall not become organizational knowledge without validation.

---

## Principle 2

Learning shall improve the organization.

Individual experience becomes valuable only after organizational validation.

---

## Principle 3

Continuous Learning shall preserve history.

Previous decisions shall remain available even after improvements are approved.

---

## Principle 4

Learning shall be governed.

Organizational knowledge shall evolve through the Knowledge Governance Framework.

---

## Principle 5

Learning shall strengthen engineering capability.

The objective is organizational improvement, not model improvement.

# Learning Validation

Before organizational knowledge is updated, learning shall be evaluated for:

- Technical Accuracy
- Supporting Evidence
- Repeatability
- Engineering Applicability
- Organizational Value
- Governance Compliance

Unvalidated learning shall remain a candidate for review.

---

# Organizational Learning Record

Every approved learning event shall include:

- Learning ID
- Source
- Engineering Discipline
- Summary
- Supporting Evidence
- Lessons Learned
- Recommended Improvements
- Evidence Level
- Approval Status
- Repository References
- Date Approved

---

# Repository Improvement

Continuous Learning may recommend updates to:

- Engineering Standards
- Procedures
- Templates
- Design Guides
- Checklists
- Workflows
- Decision Frameworks

Repository updates require governance approval.

---

# Learning Boundaries

The Continuous Learning Engine shall never:

- Automatically rewrite standards
- Delete historical engineering knowledge
- Override governance decisions
- Modify evidence classifications
- Replace engineering judgment

Learning recommendations require engineering review.

---

# Performance Measurement

The effectiveness of Continuous Learning shall be evaluated using:

- Repeated Issue Reduction
- Engineering Quality Improvement
- Decision Consistency
- Standards Maturity
- Knowledge Reuse
- Organizational Capability Growth
- Repository Improvement Rate

Metrics shall support improvement rather than optimization for numerical targets alone.

# Knowledge Governance

The Continuous Learning Engine operates under:

- NEX-STD-003 Decision Evolution Standard
- NEX-STD-006 Knowledge Governance Framework

Learning recommendations shall become organizational knowledge only after formal validation and approval.

---

# Human Oversight

Qualified engineers remain responsible for:

- Validating lessons learned
- Approving organizational improvements
- Revising engineering standards
- Maintaining organizational knowledge integrity

Continuous Learning augments organizational capability.

It does not replace engineering governance.

---

# Success Criteria

The Continuous Learning Engine succeeds when:

- Organizational knowledge continuously improves.
- Engineering experience is preserved.
- Lessons learned are reusable.
- Engineering standards mature over time.
- Organizational capability increases.
- Governance and traceability are maintained.
