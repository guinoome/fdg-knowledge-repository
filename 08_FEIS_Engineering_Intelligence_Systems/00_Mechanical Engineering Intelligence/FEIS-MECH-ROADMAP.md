# Mechanical Engineering Intelligence Roadmap

> **FEIS — Mechanical Engineering Intelligence**
>
> This document defines the long-term strategic roadmap for the Mechanical Engineering Intelligence branch.
>
> Unlike the Branch Constitution (FEIS-MECH-0000) and Development Guidelines (FEIS-MECH-0001), this roadmap is expected to evolve frequently as new engineering ideas, technologies, research, and organizational priorities emerge.

---

# 1. Purpose

This roadmap provides strategic direction for future development.

It records:

- Approved future capabilities
- Research topics
- Engineering concepts
- Planned milestones
- Deferred ideas
- Experimental technologies

This roadmap is **not a commitment** that every listed capability will be implemented.

Features shall move into active development only after engineering validation and formal approval.

---

# 2. Roadmap Principles

The roadmap follows these principles:

- Engineering value before feature quantity.
- Research before implementation.
- Validation before deployment.
- Stable architecture before expansion.
- Reusable capabilities before isolated tools.
- Organizational knowledge before software complexity.

---

# 3. Development Status

Current Stage

Current Priority

- Governance
- Knowledge Repository
- Engineering Standards

# 7. Research Backlog

The following concepts require additional engineering research before implementation.

Examples:

- Interactive airflow animation
- Real-time pressure visualization
- Parametric CAD generation
- CFD integration
- Digital Twin integration
- VR engineering visualization
- AR maintenance support
- AI-assisted engineering review
- Automated code compliance checking

Research items shall not be implemented until technical feasibility has been validated.

---

# 8. Deferred Features

The following capabilities are intentionally deferred.

Examples:

- Autonomous engineering design
- Automated equipment procurement
- Construction robotics integration
- Real-time factory automation
- Fully autonomous engineering review

These items remain outside the current development scope.
