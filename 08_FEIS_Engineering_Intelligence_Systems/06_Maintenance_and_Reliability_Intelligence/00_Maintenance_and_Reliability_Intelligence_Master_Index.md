## Three Meanings of "Maintenance Ready"

The phrase must not remain ambiguous inside FDG.

### 1. Work Ready
A maintenance job/work order is sufficiently planned and constrained to enter a committed schedule.

### 2. Asset Maintenance Ready
A new, modified, or transferred asset has the master data, maintenance strategy, parts, documents, baselines, responsibilities, and support information needed for sustained operation.

### 3. Maintenance Program Ready
The organization has the roles, processes, backlog controls, systems, data, resources, governance, and feedback loops required to manage maintenance effectively.

These are related but separate readiness states.
