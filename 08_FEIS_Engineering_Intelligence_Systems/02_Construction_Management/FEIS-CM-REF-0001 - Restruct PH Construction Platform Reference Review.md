# FEIS-CM-REF-0001 — Restruct.PH Construction Platform Reference Review

## Purpose

Founder-submitted reference for FEIS Construction Management collaborators to inspect a Philippine construction-operations product and identify useful product, workflow, packaging and commercial ideas. This is a competitive/product-learning reference, not a recommendation to buy, copy or adopt Restruct.PH.

FDG should learn from observable customer workflows and product packaging while retaining its own construction domain model, engineering truth, approval authority, data portability and organizational architecture.

## Collaborator review assignment

FMCIS / FEIS should assemble a small review team with relevant competence in construction project controls, estimating/QS, procurement/materials, site execution, billing/commercial management, product architecture and UX. A role assignment is not proof of dispatch; record real collaborator invocation receipts. If dispatch is unavailable, mark DISPATCH BLOCKED and retain this work package as pending.

### Review objectives

1. Inspect the public website, pricing page, onboarding/demo material and available product workflows lawfully. Do not bypass account restrictions or paywalls.
2. Capture current pricing accurately:
   - plan names and billing periods
   - PHP/USD currency and tax treatment
   - included users/seats/projects/sites
   - feature gates and usage limits
   - trial/promotional terms and expiry
   - onboarding, support and enterprise charges
   - cancellation, renewal, data export and post-expiry access
3. Determine which workflows are actually implemented versus merely advertised:
   - lead/opportunity and project setup
   - estimate, BOQ and budget
   - procurement, purchase requests/orders and supplier records
   - material receipt, inventory/warehouse and issuance
   - manpower, attendance and daily site records
   - progress measurement, quantities and photos
   - cost control, commitments, actuals and forecast
   - progress billing, client invoices and collections
   - documents, drawings, revisions, RFIs and submittals
   - approvals, audit trail, notifications and reporting
4. Map the end-to-end user journey from field capture to office validation, project-control visibility, commercial approval and customer-facing outputs.
5. Assess mobile usability, offline behavior, sync conflicts, low-connectivity field operation, data capture burden and role-based access.
6. Inspect data ownership, tenant isolation, permissions, auditability, backups, retention, deletion, export and security/privacy terms. Do not enter real FDG or customer data during exploration.
7. Identify useful pricing/packaging ideas: entry plan, user/site/project limits, modules, free trial, upgrade path, onboarding and support. Evaluate against FDG's target customers and operating costs; do not assume vendor pricing is sustainable for FDG.
8. Compare product experience and claims against FEIS-CM canonical lifecycle and the FDG Project Operations OS blueprint. Identify gaps, overlaps and possible reusable patterns.
9. Return evidence-backed recommendations using REUSE / EXTEND / MERGE / REPLACE / NEW. Preserve existing FEIS authority and avoid building duplicate systems.

## FEIS comparison dimensions

Use the existing FEIS-CM architecture as the evaluation baseline, especially:

- Capture Once → Validate Once → Reuse Everywhere.
- Structured operational records are authoritative; reports are projections.
- Opportunity → Bid/Tender → Award → Contract → Mobilization → Planning → Procurement → Execution → QA/QC → Progress → Billing → T&C → Punch List → Turnover.
- QTO owns drawing measurement, quantity derivation, calibration and provenance; Construction Management owns budget, procurement, delivery, installed progress, billing and change workflows.
- Project-specific approvals and professional/commercial authority must remain explicit.
- Historical authorship, revisions, evidence links and audit history must be preserved.
- Offline-capable field capture and safe synchronization are required where field conditions demand them.
- Customer experience must be simple without weakening engineering truth or control.

### Specific design questions

- What is the minimum field capture that can generate useful daily reports, manpower records, material receipts and progress evidence without duplicate entry?
- How are BOQ quantities linked to work packages, cost codes, procurement lines, delivered materials, installed quantities and progress claims?
- Does the product distinguish budget, committed cost, actual cost, accrued cost, forecast-to-complete and cash flow?
- How are variations, backcharges, wastage, returns, retention, VAT, EWT and progress billing represented?
- Are material receipts linked to purchase orders, delivery notes, inspection/acceptance and inventory movements?
- Can photos and documents be tied to a specific project, location, WBS/work package, date, author and revision?
- Can the project continue when the site engineer, foreman or project manager changes?
- Are approvals and commercial commitments attributable to named authorized users?
- What happens when the connection is lost, two users edit the same record, or a sync fails?
- Can the customer export all structured records and attachments in usable formats?
