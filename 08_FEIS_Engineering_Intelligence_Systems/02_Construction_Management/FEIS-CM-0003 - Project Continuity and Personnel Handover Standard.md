# FEIS-CM-0003 — Project Continuity and Personnel Handover Standard

**Status:** Approved Direction — Implementation Baseline  
**Effective:** 2026-09-30

## Purpose

Preserve company-owned project capability when personnel resign, transfer, become unavailable, or are replaced.

## Core Principle

> People may leave. Project knowledge must remain.

## Identity Preservation

Historical work must remain attributed to the person who actually created, performed, verified or approved it.

A departed employee account must not be renamed into a replacement employee.

Instead:

- deactivate or end access for the departing identity
- preserve historical attribution
- record departure/transfer status where appropriate
- reassign open responsibilities through a traceable transfer
- identify successor ownership separately

## Required Continuation View

A qualified replacement should be able to enter a project and determine:

- current project and package context
- completed work
- pending work
- overdue work
- reasons for pending/blocked status
- prior decisions and rationale
- evidence already collected
- governing drawings/specifications/standards
- open RFIs/submittals/inspections
- QA/QC issues and punch items
- variation/claim/billing status where authorized
- upcoming milestones
- dependencies and risks
- assigned next actions
- critical contacts
- recent changes
- documents requiring attention

## Responsibility Transfer

Transfer must preserve:

- previous owner
- successor
- transfer date/time
- open items transferred
- excluded items
- unresolved risks
- acknowledgement / acceptance where required
- management authorization where required

## Auto-Generated Continuation Brief

The system should be able to generate a successor brief from live records rather than depending on a departing employee to recreate project history manually.
