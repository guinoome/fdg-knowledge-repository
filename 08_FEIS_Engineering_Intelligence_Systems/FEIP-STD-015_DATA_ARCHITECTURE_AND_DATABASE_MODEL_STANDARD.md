# Purpose

This standard defines the data architecture, information model, and storage strategy of FEIP.

---

# Core Principle

Store structured engineering facts, preserve engineering knowledge, and connect decisions through relationships.

---

# Objective

The FEIP Data Architecture shall support:

- operational data management
- engineering knowledge preservation
- analytics
- calculation traceability
- intelligent retrieval
- future scalability
