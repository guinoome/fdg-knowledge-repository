# Purpose

This standard defines the architecture, boundaries, and module structure of the FDGuinoo Engineering Intelligence Platform (FEIP).

---

# Core Principle

FEIP is the operational engineering platform that transforms Nex Core Intelligence into practical engineering capability.

---

# Platform Definition

FEIP:

FDGuinoo Engineering Intelligence Platform

is an engineering business operating system designed to support:

- engineering operations
- asset management
- maintenance intelligence
- project execution
- calculations
- reporting
- decision support
