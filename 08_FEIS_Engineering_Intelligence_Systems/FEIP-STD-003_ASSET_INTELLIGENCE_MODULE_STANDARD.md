# Purpose

This standard defines the structure and operation of the FEIP Asset Intelligence Module.

---

# Core Principle

An asset record is the beginning of intelligence, not the end of documentation.

---

# Objective

The FEIP Asset Intelligence Module shall provide:

- complete asset visibility
- lifecycle tracking
- equipment relationships
- maintenance intelligence
- reliability improvement

# Asset Maintenance Readiness Extension — 2026-10-09

A new or modified asset is not considered maintainable merely because an asset record or commissioning certificate exists.

Asset Intelligence shall support the Maintenance-Ready Gate defined in:

[[08_FEIS_Engineering_Intelligence_Systems/06_Maintenance_and_Reliability_Intelligence/FEIS-MNT-0002 - Asset Maintenance Readiness and Project Handover Standard|Asset Maintenance Readiness & Project Handover]].

Applicable readiness data includes asset hierarchy, technical master data, criticality, maintenance strategy, PM definitions, JobPlans, spares, manuals/drawings, warranty, isolation/LOTO, training, condition baselines, compliance obligations and validated FMIS/CMMS load.

Lifecycle changes to an asset should trigger a maintenance-readiness impact review rather than leaving PM/spares/documents stale.
