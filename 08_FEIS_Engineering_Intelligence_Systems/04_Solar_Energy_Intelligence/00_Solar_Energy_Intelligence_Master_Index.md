**Branch:** FEIS Solar Energy Intelligence

**Owner:** FDG Ecosystem  
**Date Established:** 2026-10-04

## Purpose

This branch preserves the engineering architecture, research, calculation contracts, implementation handover, and future capability plan for FDG solar engineering products, beginning with **FDG Solar Visayas**.

The branch is intentionally broader than a single calculator. It is designed to evolve from deterministic solar sizing into a traceable solar engineering intelligence capability covering load analysis, PV/inverter/battery sizing, electrical validation, roof/layout intelligence, energy yield, BOM, economics, proposal generation, project handoff, commissioning, turnover, O&M, and future predictive performance.
