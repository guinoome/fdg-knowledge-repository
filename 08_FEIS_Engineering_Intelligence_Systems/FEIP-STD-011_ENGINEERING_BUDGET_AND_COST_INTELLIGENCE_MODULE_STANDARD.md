# Purpose

This standard defines the structure and operation of the FEIP Engineering Budget and Cost Intelligence Module.

---

# Core Principle

Engineering cost decisions must consider lifecycle value, not only immediate spending.

---

# Objective

The FEIP Budget Intelligence Module shall support:

- OPEX planning
- CAPEX planning
- cost forecasting
- lifecycle analysis
- budget justification
- financial decision support
