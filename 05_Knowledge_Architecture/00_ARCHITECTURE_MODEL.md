# Purpose

This standard defines the architectural organization of the FDG Knowledge Repository.

Knowledge Architecture determines how approved organizational knowledge is structured, connected, discovered, and maintained.

It does not govern knowledge.

It organizes knowledge.

---

# Core Principle

Organization enables intelligence.

Knowledge that cannot be located, understood, or connected has limited organizational value.

Architecture exists to maximize discoverability and reuse.

---

---

# Architectural Objectives

Knowledge Architecture shall:

- organize approved knowledge
- maximize discoverability
- support reuse
- reduce duplication
- strengthen traceability
- remain platform-independent
- support long-term maintainability

---

# Design Principles

The architecture shall be:

simple

consistent

scalable

modular

maintainable

technology-independent

---

# Repository Philosophy

The repository is not a document library.

The repository is an engineering knowledge system.

Documents exist to support engineering capability.

---

# Organizational Principle

Knowledge Architecture organizes intelligence.

Knowledge Management develops intelligence.

Governance authorizes intelligence.

Together they form the Engineering Governance Layer of the FDG Ecosystem.

---

# Continuous Improvement

The repository architecture should evolve through controlled evolution while preserving navigation consistency and historical integrity.

---

# Governing Principle

A well-designed repository reduces the effort required to transform knowledge into engineering decisions.

---
