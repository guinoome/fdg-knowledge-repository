# Purpose

This standard defines the top-level organizational structure of the FDG Knowledge Repository.

The repository structure provides a stable, scalable, and technology-independent architecture for organizing engineering knowledge.

---

# Core Principle

Structure follows organizational responsibility.

Repository organization shall reflect engineering governance rather than software capabilities.

---

# Repository Layers

The repository shall be organized into distinct architectural layers.

Layer 1

Engineering Governance

Defines why the organization operates.

---

Layer 2

Engineering Identity

Defines who participates.

---

Layer 3

Engineering Operation

Defines how engineering work is performed.

---

Layer 4

Knowledge Management

Defines how organizational intelligence evolves.

---

Layer 5

Knowledge Architecture

Defines how organizational intelligence is organized.

---

Layer 6

Reusable Assets

Provides templates, checklists, and reusable engineering resources.

---

Layer 7

Engineering Decisions

Preserves architectural and strategic engineering decisions.

---

Layer 8

Domain Knowledge

Contains discipline-specific engineering knowledge.

---

Layer 9

Projects

Contains project-specific engineering work.

Projects consume standards.

Projects do not redefine standards.

---

# Repository Rules

Each knowledge asset shall have one authoritative location.

Cross-references shall be preferred over duplication.

Repository structure shall remain stable.

Changes to top-level architecture require controlled evolution.

---

# Repository Independence

The repository architecture shall remain independent of:

- Obsidian
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Gemini
- Databases
- Future AI systems

Technology implements the repository.

Technology does not define the repository.

---

# Scalability

Repository growth shall occur by extending existing structures rather than introducing parallel organizational models.

Structural consistency takes precedence over convenience.

---

# Repository Integrity

The repository shall maintain:

- traceability
- discoverability
- consistency
- maintainability
- organizational continuity

---

# Organizational Principle

The repository is the engineering memory of the FDG Ecosystem.

Its structure shall support engineering capability for decades rather than individual projects.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Repository architecture may evolve through the Decision Evolution Standard while preserving backward compatibility whenever practical.

---

# Governing Principle

The structure of the repository shall strengthen organizational intelligence rather than reflect the limitations of current technology.
