# Purpose

This standard defines how reusable templates shall be designed, governed, and maintained within the FDG Knowledge Repository.

Templates standardize the creation of knowledge assets while preserving engineering consistency.

---

# Core Principle

Templates standardize structure.

Engineers contribute knowledge.

Governance preserves quality.

---

# Objectives

Templates shall:

- improve consistency
- reduce document creation effort
- simplify governance
- support onboarding
- improve maintainability
- strengthen organizational capability

---

# Scope

Templates may be created for:

- Standards
- Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)
- Procedures
- Checklists
- Project Documents
- Lessons Learned
- Engineering Guides
- Calculation Methods
- Meeting Records
- Decision Logs

---

# Template Design Principles

Templates shall be:

simple

modular

reusable

maintainable

technology-independent

focused on organizational needs rather than software features

---

# Standard Template Structure

Where applicable, templates should include:

## Document Control

Document identification and governance metadata.

---

## Purpose

Why the knowledge asset exists.

---

## Scope

What is included and excluded.

---

## Core Principle

Primary engineering philosophy.

---

## Main Content

Knowledge specific to the asset type.

---

## References

Related standards and supporting knowledge.

---

## Organizational Principle

Contribution to organizational capability.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Guidance for future evolution.

---

## Governing Principle

Final engineering rule.

---

# Reuse Rules

Templates shall encourage reuse of existing standards.

They shall avoid embedding duplicated governance content.

Shared governance belongs in authoritative standards.

---

# Responsibilities

Francis

Approves organizational templates.

---

Nex

Maintains template consistency.

Identifies opportunities for template reuse.

Recommends template evolution.

---

# Version Management

Templates are governed knowledge assets.

They shall:

- have unique document identifiers
- follow version control
- evolve through controlled governance
- preserve historical versions

---

# Template Quality Checklist

Before approving a template, verify:

✓ Organizational purpose is clear.

✓ Governance requirements are included.

✓ Reuse opportunities identified.

✓ Duplicate content minimized.

✓ Related standards referenced.

✓ Template remains technology-independent.

---

# Organizational Principle

Templates capture proven organizational patterns.

Every reusable template reduces variation while increasing engineering consistency.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Templates shall evolve as organizational maturity increases.

Changes should improve reuse without disrupting existing knowledge assets.

---

# Governing Principle

Templates should standardize engineering quality without limiting engineering thinking.
