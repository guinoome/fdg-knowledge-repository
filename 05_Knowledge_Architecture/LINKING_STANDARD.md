# Purpose

This standard defines how knowledge assets are connected within the FDG Knowledge Repository.

Linking transforms isolated knowledge into an integrated engineering intelligence network.

---

# Core Principle

Knowledge gains value through relationships.

A repository of disconnected documents is a library.

A repository of connected knowledge is organizational intelligence.

---

# Linking Objectives

Linking shall:

- improve discoverability
- expose engineering relationships
- reduce duplication
- support navigation
- strengthen engineering reasoning
- enable future intelligent retrieval

---

# Types of Links

## Structural Links

Connect knowledge according to repository organization.

Examples:

Repository → Section

Section → Domain

Domain → Topic

Topic → Knowledge Asset

---

## Reference Links

Connect related engineering knowledge.

Examples:

Related Standards

Supporting References

Engineering Guides

Calculation Methods

---

## Decision Links

Connect knowledge to engineering decisions.

Examples:

Standard → ADR

ADR → Standard

Decision → Lesson Learned

---

## Workflow Links

Connect sequential engineering activities.

Examples:

Planning

↓

Design

↓

Review

↓

Approval

↓

Execution

↓

Verification

---

## Cross-Domain Links

Connect knowledge shared across multiple engineering disciplines.

Examples:

Mechanical ↔ Electrical

Engineering ↔ Finance

Projects ↔ Governance

---

# Linking Rules

Every significant knowledge asset should link to:

its governing standards

its related knowledge

its parent Map of Content

its major dependencies

its significant successors where applicable

---

# Authoritative Source Rule

Links shall point toward the authoritative source.

Knowledge shall not be duplicated simply to improve navigation.

---

# Bidirectional Thinking

Where beneficial, relationships should be navigable in both directions.

Understanding should improve regardless of the user's starting point.

---

# Link Quality

Links shall provide engineering value.

Avoid creating links solely because two documents share terminology.

Meaningful relationships take precedence over link quantity.

---

# Repository Navigation

Navigation should naturally emerge through connected knowledge.

Engineers should discover relevant standards without relying exclusively on search.

---

# Relationship Integrity

Links should be reviewed whenever:

standards evolve

documents are archived

repository organization changes

new engineering domains are introduced

Broken relationships reduce organizational intelligence.

---

# Responsibilities

Francis

Approves repository linking philosophy.

---

Nex

Maintains relationship integrity.

Detects orphan knowledge assets.

Identifies missing engineering relationships.

Recommends improvements to repository connectivity.

---

# Linking Quality Checklist

Before approving a knowledge asset, verify:

✓ Parent Map of Content linked.

✓ Governing standards referenced.

✓ Related knowledge connected.

✓ Duplicate links avoided.

✓ Authoritative sources identified.

---

# Organizational Principle

Engineering knowledge should be intentionally connected.

Relationships enable organizational intelligence to grow beyond individual documents.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Repository connectivity shall evolve alongside organizational knowledge while preserving authoritative relationships and minimizing unnecessary complexity.

---

# Governing Principle

The strength of the FDG Knowledge Repository is measured not only by the quality of its knowledge assets, but also by the quality of the relationships between them.
