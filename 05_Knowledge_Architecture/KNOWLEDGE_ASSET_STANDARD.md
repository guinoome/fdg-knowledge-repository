# Purpose

This standard defines the minimum structural requirements for every knowledge asset maintained within the FDG Knowledge Repository.

A consistent structure improves readability, governance, discoverability, and long-term maintainability.

---

# Core Principle

Consistency reduces cognitive effort.

Engineers should immediately recognize the structure of every knowledge asset regardless of its subject.

---

# Knowledge Asset Definition

A knowledge asset is any approved organizational artifact that contributes to engineering capability.

Examples include:

- standards
- architecture decision records
- templates
- procedures
- checklists
- engineering references
- lessons learned
- calculation methods

---

# Standard Structure

Every governed knowledge asset shall contain the following sections where applicable.

## Document Control

Defines ownership and governance.

Typical fields include:

- Document ID
- Version
- Status
- Owner
- Approver
- Related Documents

---

## Purpose

Explains why the asset exists.

---

## Core Principle

Defines the primary engineering philosophy governing the asset.

---

## Main Content

Contains the engineering knowledge specific to the asset.

The organization of this section shall reflect the asset's purpose.

---

## Organizational Principle

Explains how the asset strengthens organizational capability.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Describes how the asset should evolve over time.

---

## Governing Principle

Provides the final engineering rule that summarizes the standard.

---

# Structural Consistency

Knowledge assets should present information in a predictable order.

Readers should focus on engineering content rather than learning different document layouts.

---

# Modularity

Each knowledge asset shall have one primary responsibility.

If multiple unrelated responsibilities emerge, they should be separated into independent assets.

---

# Reuse

Knowledge assets should reference existing standards rather than duplicate them.

Cross-linking is preferred over repeated content.

---

# Quality Requirements

Knowledge assets shall be:

- accurate
- concise
- traceable
- reusable
- maintainable
- technology-independent where practical

---

# Organizational Principle

Standardized knowledge structures reduce onboarding time, improve navigation, and increase engineering consistency across the FDG Ecosystem.

---

# Continuous Improvement

The standard structure may evolve through controlled evolution as the repository matures.

Improvements shall enhance consistency without disrupting existing knowledge.

---

# Governing Principle

A knowledge asset should communicate engineering intent clearly, consistently, and independently of the technology used to store it.
