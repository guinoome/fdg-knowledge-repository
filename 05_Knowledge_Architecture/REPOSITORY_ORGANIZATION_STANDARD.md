# Purpose

This standard defines how engineering knowledge is organized within the FDG Knowledge Repository.

Repository organization shall support engineering reasoning, governance, discoverability, and long-term maintainability.

---

# Core Principle

Organization follows engineering responsibility.

Knowledge shall be organized according to its organizational purpose rather than the software used to store it.

---

# Organizational Hierarchy

The repository shall organize knowledge using the following hierarchy.

Repository

↓

Section

↓

Domain

↓

Topic

↓

Knowledge Asset

Each level provides increasing engineering specificity.

---

# Organizational Rules

Each knowledge asset shall have:

one authoritative location

one responsible owner

one governing purpose

multiple relationships where appropriate

Organization shall minimize duplication.

Relationships shall maximize discoverability.

---

# Repository Sections

Repository sections organize engineering knowledge by organizational responsibility.

Examples include:

Governance

Identity

Agentic Framework

Knowledge Management

Knowledge Architecture

Templates

Architecture Decision Records

Domain Knowledge

Projects

Sections define responsibility.

Not implementation.

---

# Domains

Domains organize engineering disciplines or organizational functions.

Examples:

Mechanical Engineering

Electrical Engineering

Solar Engineering

Hotel Engineering

Business Systems

Artificial Intelligence

Each domain inherits governance from repository standards.

---

# Topics

Topics organize specialized knowledge within a domain.

Examples:

HVAC

Hydraulics

Maintenance Planning

Energy Analysis

Risk Assessment

Topics improve navigation without redefining standards.

---

# Knowledge Assets

Knowledge assets include:

standards

templates

procedures

calculations

reference material

lessons learned

decision records

Each asset shall support organizational capability.

---

# Cross-Domain Knowledge

Knowledge applicable to multiple domains shall remain in the most appropriate authoritative location.

Other domains shall reference it through links or Maps of Content.

Cross-domain reuse shall not create duplicate authoritative documents.

---

# Repository Growth

Repository expansion shall occur by:

extending existing organizational structures

adding new domains

adding new topics

adding new knowledge assets

Structural redesign shall be avoided unless justified through controlled evolution.

---

# Repository Quality

Repository organization shall support:

clarity

consistency

discoverability

traceability

maintainability

scalability

organizational continuity

---

# Responsibilities

Francis

Approves organizational structure.

---

Nex

Maintains repository consistency.

Detects duplication.

Identifies orphaned knowledge.

Recommends organizational improvements.

---

Specialized Collaborators

Contribute knowledge within their approved organizational scope.

---

# Organizational Principle

Organization reduces engineering complexity.

A well-organized repository enables engineers to locate knowledge rather than search for it.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Repository organization shall evolve incrementally while preserving stable navigation and organizational continuity.

---

# Governing Principle

Engineering knowledge shall be organized according to organizational purpose rather than technological convenience.

---

End of Standard
