# Purpose

This standard defines the complete engineering operating model used by the FDG Ecosystem for agentic execution.

It integrates governance, engineering objectives, orchestration, execution, validation, and knowledge evolution into one repeatable engineering system.

The Agentic Framework transforms engineering objectives into continuously improving organizational capability.

---

# Engineering Principle

Engineering work shall be objective-driven, governance-controlled, knowledge-informed, and continuously improved.

Engineering quality takes precedence over automation.

Automation serves engineering.

Engineering does not serve automation.

# Engineering Decision Process

Every engineering recommendation shall begin by answering:

What is the engineering objective?

↓

What has already been approved?

↓

Does existing knowledge solve this problem?

↓

If not,

what is the minimum justified evolution?

Redesign is never the default.

Controlled evolution is.

---

# Engineering Governance

Every engineering activity shall:

- follow approved standards
- remain reviewable
- remain auditable
- preserve historical reasoning
- improve organizational capability

Governance shall always precede autonomy.

# Continuous Improvement

Every completed engineering activity should answer:

What should become reusable organizational intelligence?

If valuable,

preserve it.

If approved,

standardize it.

If new evidence improves an approved standard,

propose controlled evolution.

---

# Success Criteria

The Agentic Framework is successful when:

Engineering quality improves.

Knowledge becomes reusable.

Future work requires less rediscovery.

Organizational capability increases.

Engineering governance remains intact.

---

# Organizational Principle

Design AI the way you design an engineering organization.

Separate responsibilities.

Govern authority.

Standardize knowledge.

Continuously improve capability.

Technology implements the system.

The organization owns the intelligence.

---

# Governing Principle

The purpose of the Agentic Framework is not autonomous execution.

The purpose is the disciplined development of engineering capability through governed knowledge, intelligent orchestration, and continuous organizational learning.
