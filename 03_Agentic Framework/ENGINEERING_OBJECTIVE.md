# Purpose

This standard defines how engineering objectives are created within the FDG Ecosystem.

Engineering objectives establish the desired outcome.

They do not prescribe implementation.

The objective defines *what* success looks like.

Nex determines *how* to achieve it.

---

# Core Principle

Engineering objectives describe outcomes.

They do not describe procedures.

Objectives should maximize engineering clarity while allowing intelligent orchestration.

# Definition

An Engineering Objective is a clear description of the desired engineering outcome together with its constraints and success criteria.

An objective answers:

What should be achieved?

Why is it important?

How will success be measured?

What constraints must be respected?

---

# Required Components

Every Engineering Objective should define:

## Desired Outcome

The final engineering result.

---

## Business Purpose

Why the objective exists.

---

## Definition of Done

Observable conditions indicating completion.

---

## Success Criteria

Measurable indicators used to validate success.

---

## Constraints

Engineering, organizational, financial, regulatory, or operational limits.

---

## Assumptions

Explicit assumptions made before execution.

---

## Risks

Known uncertainties affecting execution.

---

## Dependencies

Existing standards, projects, systems, or collaborators required.

---

# Engineering Objective Rules

Objectives shall:

describe outcomes

remain technology independent whenever practical

avoid prescribing implementation

align with approved standards

support engineering validation

---

Objectives shall not:

define implementation details

select collaborators prematurely

contain engineering solutions

replace engineering planning

---

# Objective Validation Checklist

Before orchestration begins, Nex shall verify:

✓ Objective is clear.

✓ Desired outcome is measurable.

✓ Definition of Done exists.

✓ Constraints are identified.

✓ Success criteria are defined.

✓ Dependencies are known.

✓ Relevant standards have been reviewed.

---

# Reverse Engineering Principle

If an objective is incomplete,

Nex shall determine what additional information is required before planning.

Nex should minimize unnecessary questions.

Only information that materially affects engineering decisions should be requested.

# Continuous Improvement

Completed objectives should improve future objectives.

Lessons learned shall strengthen future engineering planning.

Objectives evolve through organizational learning rather than repeated rediscovery.

---

# Governing Principle

Well-defined objectives produce well-engineered solutions.

Engineering quality begins before execution.
