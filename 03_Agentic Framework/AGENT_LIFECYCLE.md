# Purpose

This standard defines the lifecycle through which engineering capabilities are developed, validated, governed, and continuously improved within the FDG Ecosystem.

The lifecycle applies equally to Nex, specialized collaborators, workflows, and future engineering capabilities.

---

# Core Principle

Engineering capability is developed through continuous improvement.

Knowledge accumulation without governance produces information.

Governed knowledge produces organizational intelligence.

# Stage 3 — Context Review

Purpose

- Review identity.

- Define authority.
- Identify dependencies.
- Plan validation.

Deliverable

# Stage 8 — Knowledge Preservation

Purpose

Activities

- Update templates.
- Record ADRs.

# Stage 9 — Controlled Evolution

Purpose

Improve approved knowledge using new evidence.

Activities

- Compare against baseline.
- Preserve historical reasoning.
- Version changes.
- Document justification.

Deliverable

---

# Stage 10 — Organizational Capability

Purpose

Strengthen the FDG Ecosystem.

Success is measured by improved future engineering performance rather than completed individual tasks.

Deliverable

Higher Organizational Maturity.

---

# Lifecycle Rules

Every lifecycle stage shall:

produce documented outputs

support engineering review

remain auditable

strengthen future capability

---

No lifecycle stage shall:

bypass governance

skip validation

replace engineering judgment

discard historical reasoning

establish new standards without approval

---

# Feedback Loop

Every completed lifecycle shall answer:

What organizational knowledge should be preserved?

↓

What engineering standards should improve?

↓

What future work becomes easier?

↓

Has organizational capability increased?

If not,

identify the limiting factor and improve the system.

---

# Organizational Principle

Engineering work creates value once.

Engineering knowledge creates value repeatedly.

The lifecycle exists to maximize reusable organizational intelligence.

---

# Continuous Improvement

The objective of the lifecycle is not faster execution.

The objective is increasing organizational capability through disciplined learning and governed evolution.

---

# Governing Principle

Every completed engineering activity should leave the FDG Ecosystem more capable than before it began.
